% ========== COLUMN 1 — THE PROBLEM ==========
\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{The Problem}
    About \Households{} Bangladeshi farming households have no reliable
    advisory service in Bengali. The national Krishi Call Center (\Krishi)
    took \Ncalls{} calls in FY2025--26.

    Undetected crop disease costs \YieldLoss{} of yield. Wrong agrochemical
    dosage poisons people and contaminates soil and water.

    Across six large language models under oracle evidence, the chemical
    hallucination floor sits at 4.05--7.00\%. For a dosage instruction, that
    error rate is not acceptable.

    \figbox{15cm}{Field photo --- Pabna campaign, May 2026\\[1ex]
      real photograph, not a diagram}
  \end{block}


  \begin{block}{What Exists, and What We Added}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.52\linewidth}ccc@{}}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Capability} & \textbf{Generic} & \textbf{K.B.} & \textbf{Ours} \\ \midrule
      Safety check before retrieval        & \nomark   & \nomark   & \yes \\
      14-field dosage verifier             & \nomark   & \nomark   & \yes \\
      Audit log + analytics panel          & \nomark   & \nomark   & \yes \\
      Bengali + \Ndialects{} dialects      & \partmark & \partmark & \yes \\
      Published \Nbench{} benchmark        & \nomark   & \partmark & \yes \\
      Photo $\rightarrow$ grounded treatment & \partmark & \nomark & \yes \\
      Field soil dataset + model           & \nomark   & \nomark   & \yes \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}

    \vspace{0.5ex}
    {\footnotesize \yes\ present \quad \partmark\ partial \quad \nomark\ absent
    \quad K.B.\ = KrishokBondhu}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Four Contributions}
    \begin{enumerate}
      \item A four-stage safety-verified agent pipeline where unsafe
        categories stop before any retrieval runs.
      \item A structured claim verifier: 14-field schema, 6 relation types,
        deterministic-first, fail-closed on missing evidence.
      \item Two original field datasets --- \SoilPhotos{} tensiometer-labelled
        soil images from Pabna, and a \Nfarmerq-query farmer benchmark from
        \Ninterviews{} field interviews.
      \item An \Nbench-instance provenance-traced benchmark, 4 tracks,
        CC-BY-4.0.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{block}

\end{column}

\separatorcolumn

% ========== COLUMN 2 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (hero) ==========
\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{System Architecture}
    One farmer question enters a four-stage pipeline. Unsafe categories stop
    at stage 1 --- retrieval, generation, and verification never run.

    \figbox{55cm}{TikZ D0 --- vertical 4-stage pipeline\\[1ex]
      Safety/Router $\rightarrow$ Retrieval $\rightarrow$ Generation $\rightarrow$ Verifier\\[1ex]
      clay branch: banned chemical $\rightarrow$ \Krishi\\
      self-harm risk $\rightarrow$ \Emergency{} + \Krishi\\[1ex]
      audit log $\rightarrow$ /analytics}
  \end{block}


\end{column}

\separatorcolumn

% ========== COLUMN 3 — WHAT WE BUILT ==========
\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{Crop Disease: Classify, Then Route}
    A router identifies the crop, then hands the photo to a small per-crop
    classifier --- no single large model, so it runs on a mid-range phone.

    \figbox{8cm}{TikZ D2 --- classify-then-route\\[1ex]
      leaf photo $\rightarrow$ crop router $\rightarrow$ 5 per-crop models\\
      Rice YOLO26n 6.2 MB / 1.1 ms · Potato TFLite INT8 1.6 MB\\
      $\rightarrow$ grounded Bengali treatment}

    \vspace{0.5ex}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lrl@{}}
      Brassica (11 classes) & \AccBrassica & \solobar{1.00} \\
      Corn (4 classes)      & \AccCorn     & \solobar{0.99} \\
      Rice (8 classes)      & \AccRice     & \solobar{0.99} \\
      Potato (3 classes)    & \AccPotato   & \solobar{0.97} \\
      Wheat (live sweep)    & \AccWheat    & \solobar{0.90} \\
    \end{tabular}

    \heading{Trained on field photographs, not isolated leaves}
    Public disease datasets photograph one detached leaf on a plain
    background. Our images come from the field --- soil, hands, and other
    foliage in frame.

    \figbox{7.5cm}{Sample pair --- real photographs\\[1ex]
      LEFT: plain-background leaf (public dataset style)\\
      RIGHT: our field photo --- cluttered background\\[1ex]
      both correctly classified}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Soil Moisture: Field to Irrigation Call}
    Three days in Pabna District, May 2026. Every photo carries a
    tensiometer reading, 0.0--21.5 kPa, across 6 soil textures and 14 crops.

    \figbox{8cm}{TikZ D4 --- field to advisory\\[1ex]
      tensiometer reading $\rightarrow$ soil-moisture model $\rightarrow$ irrigation call\\
      $<$10 kPa adequate · 10--15 monitor · $>$15 irrigate}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Grounded in Bangladesh}
    Every answer traces to a named government publication or to our own
    field data. Splits are series-stratified and leakage-checked.

    \figbox{7cm}{TikZ D3 --- grounded data foundation\\[1ex]
      \Npubs{} publications, \Ninst{} institutions (BARC, BARI, BRRI, DAE)\\
      $\rightarrow$ \Nnodes{} nodes, \Ntriples{} triples\\
      + \SoilPhotos{} soil photos + \Nfarmerq{} farmer queries}
  \end{block}


\end{column}

% ========== COLUMN 4 — EVIDENCE ==========
\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{alertblock}{Measured Results}
    \heading{Bengali general QA --- token-F1}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.52\linewidth}rl@{}}
      \toprule
      \textbf{Model} & \textbf{F1} & \\ \midrule
      \rowcolor{ochretint}\textbf{KrishokChat-4B} (ours) & \textbf{\FoneOurs} & \solobar{1.00} \\
      LLaMA-3.1-8B          & \FoneLlama  & \solobar{0.53} \\
      Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite & \FoneGemini & \solobar{0.33} \\
      Gemma-4-26B           & \FoneGemma  & \solobar{0.28} \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}

    \vspace{0.5ex}
    {\footnotesize Ours runs 4-bit on-device. LLaMA-3.1-8B is zero-shot,
    Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite is a hosted API, Gemma-4-26B a much larger base.}

    \vspace{0.5ex}
    \textbf{\GeminiGain{} Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite} · \textbf{\Fonegain{} LLaMA-3.1-8B}
    ($p \approx 6.7\mathrm{e}{-}5$). A 4B model running on the phone beats a
    hosted API and a 26B base on Bengali agriculture.

    \heading{Retrieval --- Recall@10, 900 answerable queries}
    \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.52\linewidth}rl@{}}
      \rowcolor{ochretint}\textbf{Hybrid RRF} {\footnotesize ours} & \textbf{\RhybridRRF} & \solobar{1.00} \\
      BM25            & \Rbm       & \solobar{0.94} \\
      ColBERT         & \Rcolbert  & \solobar{0.90} \\
      Dense           & \Rdense    & \solobar{0.86} \\
      BGE-M3          & \Rbge      & \solobar{0.76} \\
    \end{tabular}

    \vspace{0.5ex}
    BM25 runs 9\% above dense ($p < 0.001$, Wilcoxon). Dense retrieval falls to
    \Rdensecolloq{} on colloquial farmer queries, so lexical matching carries
    Bangla dialect.\,[1]

  \end{alertblock}


  \begin{block}{Chemical-Safety Verifier}
    \figbox{15cm}{TikZ D5 --- 14-field claim schema\\[1ex]
      Bengali advisory sentence $\rightarrow$ 14 slots\\
      formulation · dose · carrier · interval · PHI · polarity\\[1ex]
      6 relations: supported / contradicted / partially /\\
      unsupported / ambiguous / not-applicable\\
      missing evidence $\rightarrow$ abstain $\rightarrow$ \Krishi}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Impact}
    \textbf{Health and safety.} Refusing unsafe agrochemical queries and
    verifying dosages reduces pesticide misuse and accidental poisoning.
    At-risk users reach \Krishi{} and \Emergency.

    \textbf{Environment.} Source-grounded dosage means less over-application
    of pesticide and fertiliser, so less soil and water contamination.

    \textbf{Access.} Offline-capable Android models under 30 ms reach farmers
    in the connectivity gap.
  \end{block}

\end{column}

% ========== COLUMN 5 — PRODUCT, IMPACT, BUSINESS, LINKS ==========
\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{The Product}
    Web client and native Android app. The Android app ships the full web
    capability set: advisory chat, photo diagnosis, and offline disease models.

    \figbox{22cm}{Screenshot triptych\\[1ex]
      /chat --- banned chemical query, stages skipped, \Krishi{} redirect\\
      /detect --- rice leaf $\rightarrow$ diagnosis $\rightarrow$ Bengali treatment\\
      /analytics --- audit donut, category bars, CSV export}

    {\footnotesize \bn{কৃষি পত্রক} (Krishi Patrak, ``field notebook''):
    Bengali-first interface, voice input, an animated agent trace on every
    query, and a sunlight high-contrast mode for outdoor use.}
    \vspace{0.5ex}
  \end{block}



  \begin{exampleblock}{Business Model}
    \textbf{1 · Freemium and B2B.} Free advisory and diagnosis for farmers.
    Paid audit and analytics dashboard for agro-dealers, SAAOs, and extension
    officers.\\
    {\footnotesize Comparators: PxD 7.8M users; ACI Fosholi EUR 3.5M target.}

    \textbf{2 · Government partnership.} An AI front-end to \Krishi{} for
    triage and escalation. The audit engine is the trust asset for a DAE or
    a2i partnership.

    \textbf{3 · Dataset and API licensing.} The \Nbench-instance benchmark and
    \SoilPhotos-image soil dataset support research licensing and an agri-fintech
    advisory API.

    \textbf{Ongoing.} Voice output for low-literacy users · extended dialect
    coverage · soil-model refinement · B2B pilot with a district extension
    office.
  \end{exampleblock}
