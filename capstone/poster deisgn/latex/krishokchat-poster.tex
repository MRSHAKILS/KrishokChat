% ==========  COLUMN 1 -- WHY  ==========
\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{The Problem}
    {\small Bangladesh has \Households{} smallholder farming households with no reliable
    advisory in their own language. A wrong pesticide dose can harm a family and pollute
    local water. Standard chatbots always give a chemical dosage answer when asked, whether
    or not the answer is correct. Crop disease left undetected costs \YieldLoss{} of yield.}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Our Approach: A Layered Trust Stack}
    {\small We built a system where every answer is tied back to an official government
    publication. The pipeline runs in strict order:

    \vspace{0.5ex}
    \textbf{Publications} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Knowledge Graph} $\rightarrow$
    \textbf{Safety Check} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Hybrid Retrieval} $\rightarrow$
    \textbf{Bengali Generation} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Dosage Verifier}
    $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Cited Answer or Human Escalation}

    \vspace{0.3ex}
    Each stage was added because our measurements showed the previous stage was not enough
    on its own. See column 4 for the failure data behind each decision.}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Key Contributions}
    {\small
    \begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.3ex}
      \item \Nbench-instance Bengali agricultural benchmark across 4 evaluation tracks,
            every instance citation-traced to source pages.
      \item \Nnodes-node agricultural knowledge graph from \Npubs{} official publications
            across \Ninst{} national research institutions.
      \item Register-aware hybrid retrieval that closes the colloquial/formal language gap
            measured at R@10 \Rdenseformal{} (formal) vs.\ \Rdensecolloq{} (colloquial).
      \item Pre-retrieval safety gate for 6 agrochemical risk categories with a 14-field
            dosage verifier; only \NonCompliance{} non-compliance on 323 unsafe queries.
      \item On-device crop disease models (1.6--21.8\,MB) achieving 95--97\% top-1 accuracy
            across 5 crops, with no internet required.
      \item 722-image soil-moisture dataset with paired tensiometer readings; first
            field-collected soil dataset for Bangladesh agricultural AI.
    \end{enumerate}}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Real-World Impact}
    {\small
    \textbf{Health.} Any query involving a banned chemical, overdose risk, or self-harm
    is blocked and routed to the \Krishi{} Krishi Call Center or \Emergency{}.
    This safety layer is present in every tier of the product at no cost to the farmer.

    \textbf{Environment.} Correct dosing based on verified sources reduces
    over-application. Bangladesh sensor-irrigation pilots report up to \WaterSave{} water
    savings and \UreaSave{} urea savings, pointing in the same direction as our soil work.

    \textbf{Access.} Works in standard Bengali and \Ndialects{} regional dialects,
    accepts voice input, and runs fully offline on mid-range Android phones.}
  \end{block}

  \begin{exampleblock}{Roadmap}
    {\small Voice output for low-literacy users $\cdot$ broader dialect support $\cdot$
    soil-vision refinement for irrigation advisories $\cdot$
    district-level pilot with a DAE field extension office.}
  \end{exampleblock}

\end{column}

  \begin{block}{Why Each Stage Exists: Failure Before Fix}
    {\small
    \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.46\linewidth}p{0.50\linewidth}@{}}
      \toprule
      \textbf{What we measured} & \textbf{What we built in response} \\ \midrule
      LLMs produce wrong chemical dosages at \HallucFloor{} of queries even when
        the correct answer is given to them directly
        & Safety check runs \textbf{before} retrieval, plus a 14-field verifier
          that refuses to generate if evidence is absent \\[0.5ex]
      Dense retrieval scores R@10 of \Rdenseformal{} on formal queries but drops
        to \Rdensecolloq{} on the same questions in colloquial farmer language
        & Hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion \\[0.5ex]
      \EntityGap{} of technical disease terms in official documents do not appear
        in any form in farmer queries; a farmer writes
        \bn{পাতা হলুদ হয়ে যাচ্ছে} where the manual says ``Yellow Mosaic Virus''
        & Retrieval is register-aware across \Ndialects{} dialects \\[0.5ex]
      The national Krishi Call Center handled \Ncalls{} calls in one year with a
        fixed human team
        & AI handles routine queries automatically; agents receive only high-risk
          cases with a full audit trail \\
      \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}}
