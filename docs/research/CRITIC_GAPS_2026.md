# KrishokTech Improvement Gap Analysis — Adversarial Review

Date: 2026-08-14. Scope: 19 candidates — 12 transferable techniques from `LITERATURE_SCOUT_2026.md` and 7 opportunities from `competitive-landscape.md` — pressure-tested against four rejection criteria: (i) marketing claim, (ii) unverifiable within project scope, (iii) violates hard rules (new services, auth gating the demo, fabricated metrics), (iv) more than 2 days of effort with low demo payoff. Horizon: 3–4 minute investor demo first; marketplace later. Every evidence citation is drawn from the two source files; no external claims added.

## B. Top 5 improvements to execute NOW

Ordered by combined demo impact, credibility, and production path.

### 1. Verifier hardening: dosage-claim enforcement + refusal metrics

- **What to build:** Decompose the generated answer into claims. For any claim carrying a chemical name plus a number and a unit, run a rule-based entailment check against the retrieved passages (chemical, crop, number, and unit must all match). Strip unsupported dosage claims from the final answer and annotate them in the UI; log every verdict with its rationale. Record refusal behavior: over-responsiveness (answered an unanswerable query) and excessive refusal counts per TRUST-SCORE.
- **Where it plugs in:** Verifier Agent (pipeline step 4) via `backend/app/application/` (verifier port); audit log (`backend/app/logs/`); frontend per-claim badges on the answer card; optional `verifier_verdict` field on saved-history messages in Supabase.
- **Acceptance criteria:** On a 20-item dosage test set, every stripped or flagged claim carries a logged reason; zero false blocks on 10 fully grounded answers; the safety panel shows live checked / passed / flagged counts.
- **Strongest evidence:** Wallat et al., "Correctness is not Faithfulness in RAG Attributions," ICTIR 2025 (DOI 10.1145/3731120.3744592) — up to 57% of citations are post-rationalized even in Command-R+, a model trained for RAG. This is why the verifier must check claims against passages independently (VeriCite, arXiv:2510.11394, generate → NLI-verify → re-attach).

### 2. Dual-view metrics wired to the agent trace + safety panel

- **What to build:** Extend each audit-log entry with per-step validity fields: router category correctness, retrieval hit/miss and top-1 relevance, verifier pass/flag. Add a local `/safety-metrics` endpoint that reads the log and returns counts by category, refusal rate, and verifier pass rate. Render these on the existing agent-trace stepper and safety panel. No fabricated numbers: every panel number is a count from the actual log.
- **Where it plugs in:** Audit trail (`backend/app/logs/`, JSONL); FastAPI endpoint; frontend safety panel and agent-trace chips (already specified in AGENTS.md §4).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Each demo query updates the counts; refusal rate shown equals actual `low_confidence`/`unsafe` classification counts; stepper states match logged decisions 1:1.
- **Strongest evidence:** AgroTools (arXiv:2605.22366) — dual-view scoring (process-level tool/argument validity, outcome-level answer) is the current evaluation standard, and models fail most at tool planning and argument generation, so those fields are the ones to instrument. Scout risk #9: agent traces without real logging read as fake.

### 3. Hybrid RRF retrieval + dialect query expansion

- **What to build:** Rank-level reciprocal-rank fusion over the existing BM25 and dense (FAISS) channels in the Retrieval Agent. Before retrieval, expand the query by mapping dialect/colloquial terms through the 110-word map and AgriEnBn glossary to standard terms (e.g., "Magra" → "Stem Borer"). Surface the mapped query in the agent trace so the expansion is visible. Keep a BM25-only fallback flag.
- **Where it plugs in:** Retrieval Agent (pipeline step 2), `backend/app/application/`; input map: `dataset_release/safety/phase4_dialect_map.json` (already shipped).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Recall@5 and nDCG on the golden set improve over single-channel on at least 80% of dialectal queries; no regression on standard queries; the expanded query is visible in the trace.
- **Strongest evidence:** Agri-Query (arXiv:2508.18093) — hybrid RAG consistently beats single-channel and long-context prompting even on a 7B model; AHR-RAG (Smart Agriculture 2026) uses RRF for multi-hop fusion. TraSe (LM4UC 2025, aclanthology.org/2025.lm4uc-1.2) validates translative term mapping for Bangla RAG.

### 4. Expert-grounded golden benchmark with unanswerables

- **What to build:** 40–60 questions sampled from the existing 1,001 real farmer queries, covering dosage, timing, pest identification, off-topic, and at least 10 unanswerable items. Reference answers from extension sources; two evaluators score each answer (correct / partial / unsupported / refused). Precompute the stats and serve them from the research/benchmark panel. No synthetic generation — the verified 20,112-record corpus is the source.
- **Where it plugs in:** `dataset_release/benchmark/`; research/benchmark panel endpoint serving precomputed numbers (per hard rule 2, never computed live).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Inter-evaluator agreement recorded; per-category scores (dose and timing broken out separately) published on the panel; refusal rate on unanswerable items above 90% at demo time.
- **Strongest evidence:** PubMed 38341517 (Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems, 2024) — chatbot responses were preferred in 78% of cases but were worse exactly on planting time, seed rate, and fertilizer rate/timing, the claims this benchmark must measure. AIEP (arXiv:2601.11537) recommends golden Q&A sets with expert scoring; ACL 2025 Findings (2025.findings-acl.368) rules out pure LLM-as-judge, so two human evaluators are mandatory.

### 5. Voice: TTS read-aloud first, ASR input optional

- **What to build:** A read-aloud toggle on answer cards using Bengali TTS (ElevenLabs v3 Bengali, or Google TTS `bn-IN` — note the locale limitation; there is no `bn-BD` TTS locale). Optional mic input via ASR (Google STT `bn-BD` chirp_2, or a local Whisper-family model) that routes through the exact same text pipeline — no changes to the agent steps. Any ASR failure falls back to text input with a visible notice. Standard Bengali only at demo time; no voice-dialect claims.
- **Where it plugs in:** Frontend chat surface; optional backend `/asr` endpoint; zero changes to the pipeline itself.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Read-aloud renders within 2 seconds on demo hardware; the ASR path reproduces a text-typed result for three scripted phrases; the failure mode is a graceful text fallback, never a crash or a wrong-language answer.
- **Strongest evidence:** FarmSaarthi (JETIR2604936) — vernacular voice input gives 87.3% decision accuracy vs 62.1% for English text, with SUS 82.4. Read Ben-10 (IJCNLP-AACL 2025, 2025.ijcnlp-short.17) and AIEP (arXiv:2601.11537) before scoping: foundation ASRs fail on Bengali dialects and ASR errors are the top failure mode, which is why the demo constraint is standard Bengali with fallback.

## C. What NOT to do and why

1. **"AI front-end to the 16123 Krishi Call Center" integration.** REJECT. Dhaka Tribune (2024) and BSS (2026) describe a phone helpline — 180–200 calls/day, no 24/7 coverage, agents who can only answer Dhaka-dialect calls, 25 paisa/minute — with no public API and no integration surface. Building "triage + escalation" without an agreement produces a mock that violates the no-fabrication rule the moment it is presented as real. The honest slice is already shipped: the canned redirect with the 16123 number in the unsafe-response path. Keep it a story beat, not a feature.
2. **Entity KG + community summaries (GraphRAG-style) before the demo.** REJECT for this horizon. Scout risk #3 names the trap directly: Crop GraphRAG (DOI 10.3389/fpls.2025.1696872) reports gains on self-built test suites, and KrishiBot's 91.58% accuracy on 500 self-made questions is not cross-comparable. High effort, no visible delta in a 3-minute demo, and the accuracy claims are exactly the vendor-style numbers the project is forbidden from quoting. Revisit after the demo as a hallucination-suppression channel.
3. **DPO "learn to refuse" alignment of the local 4-bit model.** REJECT now. Sem-RAG (Applied Sciences 15(19):10850, 2025) measured LoRA gains of only 0.5–0.85% on grounding; scout risk #4: fine-tuning fixes register and formatting, while grounding comes from retrieval — KrishokBondhu and the project's own finding agree. TRUST-SCORE/TRUST-ALIGN (arXiv:2409.11242) show alignment works, but the refusal *metrics* are pipeline-side and cheap; retraining the demo model is a multi-day rabbit hole whose payoff the metrics already measure without it.
4. **Offline-first PWA with cached advisories and image queue.** REJECT for this horizon. The demo runs online; service-worker caching plus an image-diagnosis queue exceeds 2 days; and the local precedent cuts against it — the SAAO study (scirp.net, jss_2019031414520450) rates Krishoker Janala's offline pictorial app only 11.3% "highly effective." The Plantix critique (DOI 10.56975/ijcrt.v13i12.298178) targets diagnosis quality, not connectivity. Offline is a distribution lever, not a trust lever, and it cannot be demonstrated in a conference room.
5. **B2B dealer/SAAO dashboard layer.** REJECT now, keep as the post-demo lane. The monetization evidence is one-directional (PxD/Swiss Re: farmers are unwilling to pay for advisory; IDSS/Fosholi charges ACI business units for sales leads, €3.5M 2025 revenue target), but the build is role-based multi-tenancy with per-tenant data isolation, which hard rule 1 forbids at demo phase. In the demo it belongs on one roadmap slide, not in the product.

## D. Demo story angle

Open with a Chittagonian farmer's question and let the agent trace show each real step: the router classifying the query, retrieval expanding the dialect terms to standard vocabulary and fusing BM25 with dense results, and the verifier checking every claim in the answer against the retrieved passages — a dosage claim either green-flagged with its source or stripped and annotated as unsupported. Ask one out-of-scope or misuse question and show the graceful refusal with the 16123 escalation contact. Close on the safety panel: live counts of queries by category, verifier pass rates, and refusal accuracy, all drawn from the actual audit log — then flip to the research tab and show the pre-scored golden benchmark proving the system answers dose and timing questions correctly, the exact category where generic chatbots fail (PubMed 38341517). The story is: the only Bengali agri assistant where every claim is verified, every decision is traceable, and the safety numbers are real.
