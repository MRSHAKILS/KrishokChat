## Surviving Novelty Envelope

The defensible envelope is the combination of: Bengali agrochemical claims; stable evidence IDs and exact spans; typed relation and applicability judgments; deterministic normalization and matching as the primary verifier; calibrated selective release under asymmetric safety costs; and paired dialect/Banglish stress tests under the unchanged BM25 runtime. Each element has precedent. The paper can claim a new evaluated configuration and resource only after experiments establish it; it cannot claim that relation verification, selective certification, agricultural validation, or dialect RAG is new in isolation.
