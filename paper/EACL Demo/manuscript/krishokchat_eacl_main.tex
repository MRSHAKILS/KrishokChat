\begin{abstract}
Smallholder agriculture in linguistically diverse, low-connectivity regions needs advice that is safe, grounded in trusted sources, and easy to inspect. We present \textbf{KrishokChat}, an open, production-oriented multimodal platform for Bengali agricultural advisory. It combines lightweight safety screening, hybrid BM25+dense retrieval over 2,946 institutional manuals, fine-tuned Gemma-4 4-bit generation, on-device \textbf{classification}-only vision, and fail-closed verification in one auditable session with live traces, ``Why this advice?'' evidence, and one-touch escalation to the national Krishi Call Center (16123). Six FP32 ONNX models ship at per-crop resolution with 100\% agreement to PyTorch ($n$=1,237, $\delta\le5{\times}10^{-5}$); four INT8 variants are accepted at 3.7--3.9$\times$ compression with no accuracy loss (potato n=15/brassica n=51 held-out not reportable) (rice INT8 rejected; corn still unmeasured). Browser WASM runs at 26--265\,ms p50 single-thread, inference-only. Offline-first BM25 hits 0.94 at p50~26.8\,ms and retains $+$13.25\,pp delivery at rural-edge (15\% loss) and $+$30.0\,pp at severe-2G (30\% loss) over cloud-only RAG. Traces are fully ordered in all 100 test streams (p50~9 events) and the minimal deployment is 284\,MB (95.64\,MB unique ONNX, 40.59\,MB browser primary). On 210 live safety probes (420 total with baseline) the audited system shows 0.95\% overall attack success with 6.67\% on Bangla native injection. A live system and screencast are at \url{https://krishokchat.org}.
\end{abstract}
