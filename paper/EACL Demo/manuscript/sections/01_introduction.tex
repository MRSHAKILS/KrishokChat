\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

In the Global South, smallholder agriculture supports hundreds of millions of livelihoods. In Bangladesh, more than 16 million farming households manage diverse cropping systems under persistent pest, fungal, and climate pressure \citep{bari_handbook2023}. Conversational AI has shown clear promise for extension \citep{farmerchat2024, krishokbondhu2025}, yet field deployments remain brittle where reliability matters most: unconstrained models hallucinate chemical treatments, dialectal and Romanized Banglish inputs break retrieval, and opaque answers leave farmers and extension officers without evidence, provenance, or a safe next step \citep{ragvue2025}.

We present \textbf{KrishokChat}, an open, production-oriented multimodal Bengali advisory platform that treats these gaps as an integration problem. A KrishokChat session is a single auditable object---text, image, retrieved evidence, verification state, and escalation---rather than a stateless chat turn. Deterministic safeguards sit alongside grounded generation, and the decision process is observable by design.

\paragraph{Contributions.} This demo paper focuses on usefulness, integration, and reusability rather than beating a standalone benchmark:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{(C1) Integrated platform.} Safety screening, hybrid retrieval, grounded generation, classification-only vision, verification, and constrained delivery run as one observable workflow, not as disconnected pages. The system is provider-agnostic, offline-capable, and deployable from a minimal 284\,MB artifact.
    \item \textbf{(C2) Safety-aware, multimodal workflow with provenance.} A tiered resolution ladder gates every query; on-device classification narrows the retrieval space; a relational verifier checks dosage, formulation, and pre-harvest interval against retrieved sources; and every answer exposes ``Why this advice?'' evidence, a live trace, and a one-touch 16123 escalation path.
    \item \textbf{(C3) Open, reproducible artifact with honest system-level validation.} The stack (FastAPI~+ Next.js~14, PWA, \texttt{onnxruntime-web/wasm} 1.29.0 lazy-gated) is released under Apache-2.0 at \url{https://krishokchat.org} with frozen indexes, ONNX at correct per-crop resolution, and measured latency, vision, safety, observability, and resilience numbers that trace to frozen artifacts.
\end{itemize}
