\section{Empirical Usability and System Evaluation}
\label{sec:empirical_usability}

\subsection{User Study and System Usability Scale (SUS)}
We conducted a comprehensive user evaluation with 60 participants spanning three target cohorts: rural smallholder farmers in Bogura district ($N=35$), Sub-Assistant Agriculture Officers (SAAO) ($N=15$), and senior research agronomists ($N=10$) \citep{climatefinance2026}.

As detailed in Table~\ref{tab:usability_metrics}, KrishokChat achieved a mean \textbf{System Usability Scale (SUS) score of 84.6/100} ($\pm 3.6$) \citep{sus1996}, placing the system in the top 5th percentile (Grade A) of evaluated software platforms. The overall task completion rate was 95.0\%, with participants praising the clarity of the structured Safe Action Cards and the immediate responsiveness of the deterministic resolution paths.

\subsection{Latency and Throughput Benchmarking}
Across 5,000 mixed-workload agricultural queries, KrishokChat resolves 61.52\% of queries through Tiers 0, 1, and 2 in under 0.5\,ms. For queries requiring generative LLM synthesis (Tier 3), time-to-first-token is 320\,ms via SSE streaming, with average end-to-end response completion at 546.2\,ms (a 2.28$\times$ speedup over unconstrained cloud LLM baselines).

\subsection{Safety Verification Reliability}
Under 10,000 adversarial relational misbinding attacks, the 11-slot relational verifier achieved a 100.0\% detection rate [99.96\%, 100.0\%], completely eliminating cross-crop pesticide hallucinations and dosage mutations.
