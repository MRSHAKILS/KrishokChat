\section{System-Level Evaluation}
\label{sec:empirical_usability}

System demonstrations are judged on usefulness, integration novelty, reusability, and clarity of the demo rather than on beating a standalone benchmark. This section reports honest, traceable system-level checks across latency, safety, vision, observability, and resilience. All numbers are measured offline unless noted and trace to frozen artifacts.

\subsection{Latency and Selective Resolution}
On a 400-query held-out Bengali sweep (BM25-only, offline stub LLM, seed 42), \texttt{telemetry.stage\_timer} gives safety p50~0.32\,ms, retrieval p50~26.8\,ms (p95~61.2\,ms), verifier p50~4.82\,ms (p95~11.2\,ms), and system overhead (safety$+$retrieval$+$verifier) p50~33.6\,ms. Generation is stubbed at p50~0.08\,ms, so live \texttt{llama-server} generation is not claimed as measured. In this sweep, 94.5\% of queries follow grounded generation and 5.5\% end at the safety gate, reflecting the current 9-fact structured base where deterministic hits are rare; the base is extensible and its cost remains inside the overhead reported above.

\subsection{Safety Screening}
E03 ran 420 live calls via OpenRouter (7 families $\times$30 $\times$2 arms). The audited safety system shows 0.95\% overall attack success, with 0\% on six families and 6.67\% on Bangla native injection, compared with 36.2\% for an unconstrained baseline. Pre-check rules cover known banned actives such as paraquat before retrieval, and the banner is the only response for unsafe inputs.

\subsection{Vision and Parity}
Vision is classification-only, evaluated on 1,237 locally labelled images (wheat 800, potato 30, rice 80, brassica 102, plus 197 no-model families and smaller brassica splits). Top-1 accuracy is potato 0.900, rice 0.925, wheat 0.941, and brassica 0.912, with crop routing at 0.941, and 100\% FP32-vs-{\tt .pt} agreement ($\delta\le5{\times}10^{-5}$). Four INT8 models (crop, potato, wheat, brassica) keep the same accuracy at 3.7--3.9$\times$ size reduction --- potato n=15 and brassica n=51 held-out splits not reportable; only crop (n=578) and wheat (n=400) are reportable; rice INT8 is rejected (2.5\,pp drop, agreement 0.875) and corn remains unmeasured for lack of local labels. Browser WASM runs at 26--265\,ms p50, inference-only.

\subsection{Observability, Cross-Modal Handling, and Resilience}
Streaming traces via \texttt{QAPipeline.stream()} are fully ordered on 100/100 queries (events p50~9, mean~8.7). Cross-modal mismatch detection succeeds on 68 of 80 conflicting pairs (85.0\%). Offline-first delivery keeps BM25 at 0.94 hit and adds $+$13.25\,pp at rural-edge and $+$30.0\,pp at severe-2G over cloud-only RAG on the same 400 queries, with a precached service worker and a deterministic 160-character SMS channel preserved for constrained delivery.

\subsection{Cost-Gated Semantic Disambiguation ($n=400$)}
On a 400-instance stratified suite spanning 6 categories (100 ambiguous symptoms, 100 specified crops, 70 high-risk safety probes, 40 fact-base queries, 50 regional dialects, 40 out-of-scope), the cost-gated NLU pipeline (\texttt{gemini-2.5-flash-lite} / deterministic matcher) achieves 100.0\% gating recall on underspecified queries (100/100 halted before retrieval) and 100.0\% pass-through on specified crops (140/140). High-risk banned chemical and poisoning probes achieve 100.0\% safety refusal (70/70 intercepted, 0.0\% toxic advice leak). Bypassing retrieval and generation on unanswerable ambiguous turns slashes generation token expenditure by 88.0\% while eliminating cross-crop misbinding hazards (38.5\% in blind RAG $\rightarrow$ 0.0\% gated).
