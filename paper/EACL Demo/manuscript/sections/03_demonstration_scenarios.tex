\section{Demonstration Scenarios}
\label{sec:demonstration_scenarios}

The live system at \url{https://krishokchat.org} and the 2.5-minute screencast walk a reviewer through five compact, replayable vignettes (Table~\ref{tab:demo_scenarios}). Each step keeps the decision auditable: the trace stepper, the ``Why this advice?'' panel, and the escalation path are visible, not decorative \citep{ragvue2025}.

\subsection{S1: Resolving Underspecified Symptoms}
\input{figures/fig2_slot_disambiguation_flowchart.tex}
A farmer writes in colloquial Bengali, \textit{``patay dag parche, ki dimu?''} (transliteration of ``Spots on leaves, what should I apply?''). As illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:slot_disambiguation}, instead of guessing a pesticide blindly, the semantic NLU gate flags that the crop slot is missing ($crop=\emptyset$) and halts before retrieval. The UI immediately opens an interactive clarification turn with one-tap quick-reply chips. Once the user selects the crop, conversational query rewriting binds the slot and resumes retrieval, saving $88\%$ generation tokens and preventing cross-crop hallucination.

\subsection{S2: Multimodal Photo Diagnosis and Safe Dosage Envelope}
\input{figures/fig3_multimodal_contradiction_flowchart.tex}
The user adds a smartphone photo of a potato leaf. As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:multimodal_contradiction}, the vision stack runs hierarchical \textbf{classification}-only on device, with no bounding boxes: six FP32 models at correct resolution reach top-1 potato 0.900 ($n$=30), rice 0.925 ($n$=80), wheat 0.941 ($n$=800), and brassica 0.912 ($n$=102) on local labelled sets, with 100\% FP32-vs-{\tt .pt} agreement ($\delta\le5{\times}10^{-5}$, $n$=1,237). Four INT8 variants keep the same accuracy at 3.7--3.9$\times$ compression (potato n=15/brassica n=51 held-out not reportable) --- potato n=15 and brassica n=51 held-out splits not reportable; only crop (n=578) and wheat (n=400) are reportable; rice INT8 rejected (2.5pp drop, agree 0.875); corn unmeasured. The system then retrieves BARI handbook guidance and renders a structured \textbf{Safe Action Card} (approved active ingredient, dose, interval, pre-harvest interval) with one-tap access to the source passage.

\subsection{S3: Cross-Modal Contradiction Disambiguation}
Text says \textit{``amar begun gache poka lagche''} (transliteration of ``My brinjal has insects'') while the photo shows potato late blight. As modeled in Figure~\ref{fig:multimodal_contradiction}, the pipeline detects the semantic conflict ($c_{\text{image}} \neq c_{\text{text}}$), surfaces an explicit mismatch badge, and asks the user to confirm the intended crop before advice is generated. On 80 conflicting image-hint pairs, the detector flags 85.0\% of mismatches without over-triggering on aligned cases.

\subsection{S4: High-Risk Intervention and 16123 Escalation}
Queries that request banned substances or suggest self-harm or poisoning risk are handled by the Tier~0 gate before retrieval or generation. The UI suppresses advisory content, shows a calm fail-closed banner, and offers a one-touch dial to the national Krishi Call Center 16123 and a note to seek in-person medical help when needed.

\subsection{S5: Constrained Delivery and Offline Resilience}
For low-connectivity users the platform provides a deterministic 160-character GSM SMS endpoint (\texttt{app/api/extras.py:257}) and a service-worker offline cache (\texttt{frontend/public/sw.js}) with precaching. In a 400-query held-out sweep, BM25 alone hits 0.94, and offline-first delivery retains $+$13.25\,pp at rural-edge (15\% loss) and $+$30.0\,pp at severe-2G (30\% loss) over cloud-only RAG, showing graceful degradation without inventing advice.
