\section{Conclusion and Future Availability}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper demonstrated \textbf{KrishokChat}, an open, production-oriented multimodal Bengali agricultural advisory platform. By subordinating generative language models to an 11-slot fail-closed factual authority layer and a five-tier selective resolution ladder, KrishokChat eliminates agricultural hallucinations while achieving an 84.6/100 System Usability Scale rating. Future extensions will expand on-device speech synthesis and multi-country crop knowledge packs across South Asia.

\paragraph{Availability:}
KrishokChat is fully open-source under the Apache-2.0 license. The live platform, API documentation, pre-trained ONNX weights, and screencast video are available at \url{https://krishokchat.org} and \url{https://github.com/RaiyaanReza/KrishokChat-Agricultural-Advisory-System}.
