\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{KrishokChat}, an open, production-oriented multimodal Bengali advisory platform that brings safety screening, hybrid retrieval over 2,946 institutional sources, grounded generation, on-device classification-only vision, and fail-closed verification into one inspectable workflow. Its value for the demo track is integration and deployability: 100\% FP32-vs-{\tt .pt} parity with four INT8 models at 3.7--3.9$\times$ compression (rice INT8 rejected, corn unmeasured; potato/brassica INT8 not reportable for accuracy), 0.94 BM25 offline hit with $+$13.25\,pp rural-edge and $+$30.0\,pp severe-2G retention over cloud-only RAG, fully ordered streaming traces, and a 284\,MB minimal deployment. Deterministic and safety paths remain independent of the LLM, with escalation to 16123 where advice cannot be grounded.
