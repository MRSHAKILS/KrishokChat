# Reviewer Premortem

| Likely criticism | Why the reviewer is right | Minimum fix | Do | Don't do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. "The claimed novelty is already covered by agricultural verification, claim certification, and Bengali dialect RAG." | The fresh literature set directly occupies broad A and broad B. | Frame the paper around the combined evidence-span, agrochemical relation, selective-risk, BM25-runtime, and safety-invariance setting. Verify all related-work details before submission. | State overlap plainly and test the narrow distinction. | Claim first-of-kind relation verification, selective RAG, Bengali agri RAG, or dialect normalization. |
| 2. "The gold labels and source evidence are not trustworthy enough for safety conclusions." | Extracted PDF numbers are unreconciled; current corpora have multiple locations; relation and applicability labels require domain judgment. | Complete T05, freeze stable IDs/spans, run independent expert labeling, report agreement/adjudication, and release raw label audit where permitted. | Version every correction and disclose missingness. | Use an LLM judge as gold, average conflicting sources, or infer absent fields. |
| 3. "The method is a rules engine fitted to one dataset and may not generalize." | Deterministic dictionaries and field rules can overfit source wording and known units. | Group by source and intent, retain untouched test and adversarial sets, run field/parser ablations, report unseen alias/source errors, and bound claims to the evaluated domain. | Include oracle-field error decomposition and out-of-scope rates. | Market the verifier as universal or hide parser failures. |
| 4. "Abstention gains come from refusing nearly everything." | Risk falls trivially at zero coverage; current confidence is not calibrated. | Prespecify a coverage floor or target, report full risk-coverage curves, AURC, false abstention, dangerous non-abstention, and always-certify/always-abstain bounds. | Freeze thresholds on development data and show sensitivity. | Select the threshold after seeing test results or report risk without coverage. |
| 5. "Dialect normalization is synthetic, unsafe, and disconnected from the primary method." | Prior work covers dialect RAG; transformations can alter intent, negation, and dosage; small regional samples weaken inference. | Use paired intent lineages, native review, dictionary-first conditions, safety-slot invariance, subgroup sample gates, and E5 interaction analysis. | Demote B to a secondary protocol and omit learned normalization unless it passes all gates. | Claim dialect authenticity automatically, pool unsupported subgroups, or treat retrieval gain as safety gain. |

## Release Posture

A sixth predictable objection concerns implementation honesty: the vision fallback marks source-empty advice verified, the benchmark endpoint is a placeholder, and local Gemma performance is unverified. Fix or isolate these paths before an end-to-end demo claim. Do not use UI behavior as scientific evidence.
