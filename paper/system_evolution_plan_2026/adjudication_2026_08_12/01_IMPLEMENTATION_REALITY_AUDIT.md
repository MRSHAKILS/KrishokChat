## Adjudicated Runtime Baseline

The scientific baseline is the captured application pipeline: safety classification, BM25 retrieval, generation through a replaceable model port, normalized lexical dosage matching, local audit, and shared JSON/SSE execution. The proposed study replaces only the verifier adapter after offline qualification. It does not add a new agent, retriever, service, or planner loop.
