# 1 Introduction

A farmer types a Bengali description of yellowing leaves without naming the crop. The index returns sources from the dominant crop, and fluent generation presents wrong-crop advice with confidence. This failure motivates KrishokChat, a Bengali advisory demo built around three checks: halt before retrieval, fence retrieval with the photo, and check dosage claims before rendering.

C1 is halt before retrieval. A deterministic extractor maps treatment intent with empty crop to clarification with zero retrieval (A4), non-chemical guidance (A3), or refusal with the 16123 helpline (A5). On 200 farmer queries the det halt is 0.38 (N01b) (76 halted; single-reviewer labels; no gate-silent mappable set in real data). A pilot with 100 ambiguous and 140 specified prompts halted 100/100 and passed 140/140 (PILOT, E09). Clarification costs p50 89 tokens against retrieval-context p50 2146 tokens, with no blended saving claimed (N02).

C2 is fence and badge. A tri-state router sends the photo to per-crop disease models, and the predicted crop pre-binds the advisory query; text that disagrees with the photo triggers a badge and confirm-crop before any chemical. Fencing lowers wrong-crop advice from 0.3625 to 0.30 with 25 discordant pairs in one direction and 0 in the other (McNemar p 5.96e-08), as a perfect-gold simulation and upper bound, not classifier performance (N08). Badge capture is 53/54 farmer pairs and 400/400 PRISM pairs, 453/454 combined with 0 false halts, scoped to explicit-text contradiction only (N07; sole miss farmer_q_63, an implicit crop).

C3 is two walls around an untrusted generator with four renderings. An upstream gate (Tier-0 plus classifier) and a dosage-claim verifier (same-passage binding, annotate-and-drop, p50 4.815 ms) bound generation, and authorship badges keep evidence, model text, and dropped claims visible. Attack success falls to 0.95% [0.26, 3.41] from 36.19% [29.99, 42.88] over 420 live calls, with Bangla residual 0.10 [5.5, 17.4] (embedded 4/10, roleplay 2/10) reported as residual risk that motivates the verifier (E03+N12). The verifier catches 33/33 dose-x2, 30/31 dose-div2, 22/25 chemical-swap, and 29/29 unit-swap with 0/38 clean false positives on 38 answers, per-type with no pooling, dosage-claim sentences only (N03, SMALLN). SMS enforcement holds 299/300 offline and 100/100 live with 0 over-160 and 16/16 referral purity, while dose fields survive 0/84, so enforcement plus purity is claimed and packer work remains (N05).

Visitors can run five scenarios: S1 halt-chips-resume with zero retrieval, S2 photo fence to card, S3 mismatch badge, S4 refusal with 16123, and S5 SMS plus offline. Costs are MODELED, network deltas are SIMULATED, and live LLM latency is UNMEASURED throughout. The demo shows operating points with limits in-cell rather than best-case summaries.
