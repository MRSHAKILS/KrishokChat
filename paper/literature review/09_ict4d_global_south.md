## 1. Key papers

1. **Farmer.Chat** — Singh et al., arXiv:2409.08916 (v2 Oct 2024). 15,000+ farmers, 300,000+ queries, retrieval context precision 71%, ~80% query faithfulness, 67% response relevance; 14 FGDs (199 participants), usability tests, bi-weekly phone surveys. The canonical RAG-extension deployment paper.
2. **60 Decibels + Digital Green, "Farmer.Chat Endline Study"** (Nov 2025, 450 Kenyan farmers): NPS 63; 99% find recommendations trustworthy; 7 in 10 applied advice within 30 days; 61% "meaningful use" vs 27% Kenya benchmark; women NPS 75 vs 57. Behavior-change module cross-checks backend logs against farmer recall interviews (96% recall, 66% acted, 80% of actions aligned). **Over half of users hold university education — the system is not reaching lowest-literacy farmers.**
3. **AIEP Initiative (Gates, GIZ, CLEAR Global), "Disrupting Agricultural Advisory Utilizing Generative AI"** (Dec 2025) + arXiv:2601.11537 (2026). Five RAG/voice MVPs in Kenya/Bihar; 824-farmer study, NPS ~60 (women higher); "Golden Q&A" benchmark of 114 expert-validated questions withheld from public release over leakage risk; sub-5s voice latency the hard constraint; **only LLM-as-a-judge correlated (low-to-medium) with human agronomist ratings — n-gram and embedding metrics failed**.
4. **KrishokBondhu** — Ameen et al., arXiv:2510.18355 (Oct 2025, v2 Mar 2026). Bengali voice RAG over BARC/DAE/WorldFish texts, Gemma 3-4B; 72.7% high-quality; 4.53/5 vs KisanQRS 3.13; expert-referral text in 100% of responses; **no safety layer, no helpline integration, no farmer field evaluation**.
5. **KrishokChat benchmark (team's own)** — Reza, Nimi & Shahid, EACL 2026 (local copy in `paper/done papers/`). 85,979-instance, 4-track Bengali agri benchmark with citation-level provenance and chemical-trace audit; 1,000-query Real-World Farmer Benchmark, 300 of them collected via structured field interviews with smallholder farmers in Rajshahi and Natore districts. Closed-book Treatment QA Correct% <44% for every zero-shot baseline and a 4.05–7.00% chemical hallucination floor under oracle evidence — the benchmark's own verdict: standalone deployment is unsafe for chemical advice without retrieval and verification.
6. **High, Singh & Nemes, "Artificial Intelligence for Agricultural Extension"** — *Journal of Development Policy and Practice* 11(1):61–80 (2026). Farmer.Chat case study through an AKIS typology; extension ratios 1:1,000+ vs recommended 1:400; weak feedback loops; dependence on lead farmers/extension agents for onboarding older, less digitally literate users. The peer-reviewed critique template for "AI replaces extension" claims.
7. **Kabir, "Sowing trust in the machine"** — *AI & Society* (2025), DOI 10.1007/s00146-025-02788-8. LLM gave technically correct but generic cassava-weed-control advice unsuitable for resource-poor Nigerian farmers; epistemic injustice risk; FAIR/CARE data governance; hybrid human-AI advisory. The "correct-but-not-actionable" failure citation.
8. **Abate et al., "Improving smallholder agriculture with generative AI: Farmer.Chat in Kenya"** — AEA RCT Registry AEARCTR-17035, DOI 10.1257/rct.17035-1.2 (registered Jul 2026). Pre-registered cluster RCT, 600 villages, 3,000 households, Nakuru County. **The first causal-evaluation signal — ICT4D reviewers now expect pre-registration before causal language.**
9. **KisanQRS + Government of India KCC escalation** — arXiv:2411.08883 (2024, 34M KCC logs, 96.58% F1 mapping, 96.20% NDCG); PIB Dec 2025: AI/ML integration into KCC escalation (Farm Tele Advisor → Subject Matter Specialists → ICAR/KVK, 22 languages, 1800-180-1551). Validates KrishokChat's 16123 escalation design as institutionally grounded.
10. **Cannanure et al., "Applying Think-Aloud in ICTD"** — arXiv:2501.05840 (ICTD 2025). 20 think-aloud sessions; 75% navigated the chatbot but only 35% could use goal-setting; novices overwhelmed, slow typing. Method-adaptation note for usability evaluation with low-digital-literacy users.
11. **Rigby et al., "Mobile Phones in the Drylands"** — COMPASS '25, DOI 10.1145/3715335.3735464. WhatsApp ubiquity, elder/baraza-mediated information screening, women's reduced phone access; app-based services fail (~10,000 downloads Afriscout) while WhatsApp integrates. Channel choice drives uptake; intermediaries still curate information.
12. **GSMA Mobile Gender Gap Report 2025** (May 2025) + 2026: 2024 LMIC gaps — mobile ownership 8%, smartphone 14%, mobile internet 14%; South Asia internet gap 32% (330M women); 2025: 12% internet, 7% ownership, 13% smartphone gaps.
13. **Zhao et al., "Participatory Development Research in South Asia through LLM Agents"** — ACL IndoNLP Workshop 2025, pp. 108–121. Multi-agent LLM pipeline for participatory data collection in Sinhala-speaking Sri Lanka. South-Asia agentic-LLM ICT4D precedent.
14. **Background anchors**: Tzachor et al., *Nature Plants* 2023 (PMID 37932438); "Digital agro-advisory tools in the global south: a behavioural analysis," *Discover Agriculture* (2025), DOI 10.1007/s44279-025-00190-y (trust as the foundation of adoption); "Agricultural data governance from the ground up," *Big Data & Society* (2025), DOI 10.1177/20539517251330182; IFPRI/AGX AI blog (May 2026) three-level benchmarking (model/system/process) with gender-disaggregated indicators.

## 2. Findings & insights

- **Trust is high but sample-selection-biased.** 99% trust / NPS 63 / 70% application yet over half the users hold university education. Trust numbers from self-selected digital users do not transfer to the general farming population.
- **Application ≠ correct application.** The 60dB backend-log cross-check (66% acted; 80% aligned) is the only published attribution method; "applied" self-reports overstate harm-free adoption by up to 20 points and should be the floor for any impact claim.
- **Automated metrics fail without human correlation.** Across five AIEP MVPs, only LLM-as-judge correlated with agronomist judgment, and weakly; the Golden Q&A benchmark was withheld over leakage fears. **There is no public, multi-dialect, low-resource-language safety benchmark for agri-advisory — the seed's 20,112-record dataset occupies this vacancy.**
- **Voice latency dominates feasibility.** Sub-5s voice response consistently unmet; fixes were in-country hosting and audio minimization; Bengali ASR/TTS chains add dialectal morpheme risk; translation errors cascade (Hindi "mushroom"/"mosquito" confusion documented in AIEP).
- **Correct-but-generic advice is the signature harm** (Nigeria cassava case; over-reliance studies link AI path-dependency to weakened autonomous decision capacity).
- **Women: paradoxical signal.** Women reach higher satisfaction in deployed chatbots (NPS 75 vs 57) while South Asia's mobile internet gender gap is 32% — the women reached are already digitally included. Gender-disaggregated application outcomes are unmeasured in every paper reviewed.
- **Helplines are capacity-constrained public infrastructure.** Bangladesh's Krishi Call Center (16123): ~180–200 calls/day, 9:00–17:00, Fridays excluded, 25 paisa/min, Dhaka-centric staffing. India's KCC handles the same constraints with a three-level escalation protocol now absorbing AI/ML. A chatbot redirecting to 16123 extends an existing state service; **no reviewed system measures whether such referrals are followed.**

## 3. Research gaps

1. **No measured evaluation of helpline escalation from an AI advisory system.** KrishokBondhu emits "expert referral" text in 100% of responses but no referral-uptake measurement; Farmer.Chat has no escalation layer; India's KCC AI/ML integration is announced policy, not evaluated. Referral acceptance, recall, and farmer adherence are unknown. The seed's audit-trail + helpline design is positioned to produce the first such measurement.
2. **No literacy-stratified or over-reliance field evaluation exists for low-resource-language agri-chatbots.** Every 2025–2026 study either samples digitally included users (Farmer.Chat) or evaluates offline (KrishokBondhu). Application-rate claims rest on self-report with documented selection bias; no instrument measures automation bias or dosage-misapplication harm in low-AI-literacy users. The 1,000-query Farmer Benchmark (350-query held-out eval split) + 20,112-record multi-dialect corpus enables literacy-stratified recall/application audits no competitor can replicate.
3. **No public multi-dialect safety benchmark for low-resource agri-NLP, and no published data-governance protocol for farmer query audit trails.** Competitors withhold golden Q&A sets (AIEP) or lack dialect coverage; no agri-chatbot paper operationalizes accessible consent and farmer-legible data practices. The local-only audit log is itself a governance artifact.

## 4. Conventions (what ICT4D/COMPASS/CHI reviewers benchmark against)

- **Mixed-methods field evidence is mandatory**: platform analytics + FGDs with gender-balanced recruitment + phone surveys + usability observation + shadowing, triangulated with conversational logs; lab-only evaluation is a desk rejection risk at COMPASS/ICTD.
- **Third-party or pre-registered evaluation for impact claims**: 60dB-style independent phone surveys with NPS + meaningful-use composite; AEA RCT Registry pre-registration before causal language.
- **Gender-disaggregated reporting on every access/trust/application metric** (GSMA gaps as context).
- **Human expert (agronomist) evaluation as ground truth; automated metrics only as supplements** after correlation with human ratings.
- **Honest constraint reporting**: voice latency figures, ASR/MT error examples, language-coverage limits, hardware reachability. Concealing these reads as hype.
- **Institutional embedding narrative**: partnerships with state extension (DAE/AIS 16123, ICAR/KCC), lead-farmer onboarding, WhatsApp/phone channels over proprietary apps.
- **Ethics section covering data governance + automation bias** (Kabir 2025; data-justice literature; GAIA Ethics Toolkit 2025).

## 5. Positioning recommendations for KrishokChat

- **Differentiate from KrishokBondhu explicitly**: same language, same RAG, same call-centric modality — the seed wins on the two things KrishokBondhu lacks: a measured safety pipeline and a national-helpline escalation protocol tied to real 16123 capacity constraints (180–200 calls/day, office hours).
- **Ship the first behavior-change attribution study for Bengali agri-AI**: replicate the 60dB backend-log cross-check (query→response→recall→action-alignment) in a small field pilot, with literacy-stratified sampling to expose Farmer.Chat's selection bias directly.
- **Position the 20,112-record multi-dialect safety dataset as the public benchmark the field lacks** — with a documented LLM-as-judge → agronomist correlation pass (AIEP protocol), and the 16123 escalation category as a novel safety class.
- **Frame the safety pipeline as institutional integration, not novelty theater**: cite India's KCC AI/ML escalation policy (PIB 2025) and Bangladesh's AIS infrastructure; report audit-trail metrics (per-category counts, referral rates) as a governance contribution answering the data-justice literature.
- **Match ICTD evaluation conventions**: mixed methods, gender-disaggregated trust/application metrics, voice-latency and ASR error disclosure, a limitation paragraph declining causal claims — pre-registration or a 60dB-style independent survey is the cheapest credibility purchase available.
