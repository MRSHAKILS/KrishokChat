## 2. Findings & insights

- **Scale is the wrong axis for differentiation.** Deployed systems report monotonically growing counts (15k/300k â†’ 1.5M queries â†’ 670k farmers/2.5M questions); a capstone paper cannot compete on this axis. The same papers disclose no released evaluation data.
- **Factuality baselines are low and only fine-tuning/grounding moves them.** Zero-shot Llama-3 factuality 29%; 51 LLMs average 41.27% on AgriEval vs 70.62% PhD experts; LoRA SFT lifts recall 26.2%â†’50.3%. Any measured improvement over these numbers is publishable.

- **Over-advising is the dominant failure mode**: pest-management high-recall/high-FP profile; amateur-gardening study (MDPI *Sustainability* 17(23):10466, 2025) found models over-diagnose healthy plants and recommend unnecessary treatments; LLM-as-judge correlates weakly with humans in agri QA (2507.16974).
