## 2. Findings & insights

**Verifier architecture has converged.** 2025–26 claim-level verifiers share one shape: decompose answer into atomic claims → verify each claim against retrieved context via NLI → label entailed/contradicted/baseless → aggregate with an AND-join. RT4CHART (F1 0.776 vs 0.424, +83% relative) shows local claim-level checking is the main performance driver, global re-verification adds selective gains; VeriCite shows verification is best placed *before* final generation. MedRAGChecker adds KG consistency signals because text-only NLI misses drug–disease contraindications — the analogue for KrishokTech is a chemical-whitelist check on dosage claims.

**Selective refusal is a measurable, trainable, currently-failing capability.** RefusalBench: frontier models drop below 50% refusal accuracy on multi-document tasks; refusal scales independently of answer accuracy and improves with targeted alignment. Zhou et al. (AAAI 2026) report the failure mode KrishokTech's design avoids: irrelevant retrieved contexts cause over-refusal on answerable queries, and refusal fine-tuning (R-tuning) worsens calibration while ICFT mitigates it. Their two-stage mechanism (uncertainty threshold on the base model, then context-utility signal ΔU) is a published version of "decide before you retrieve."

**Production abstention is deployed with latency budgets.** Huang et al. mask 29.9% of responses at precision 0.95 in a financial customer-support RAG; Shankar et al. report AUROC 0.961 on hard near-distribution abstention using a single energy score over corpus embeddings. Both demonstrate that abstention is a first-order feature, not a guardrail add-on.

**Adaptive retrieval has moved from reflection tokens to calibrated confidence interfaces.** "Know Before You Fetch" (arXiv:2606.29959) reformulates adaptive RAG as graded retrieval-budget allocation (k=0/1/5/abstain) with calibrated probabilities (ECE 0.643→0.009 on NQ); BeyondUncertainty (arXiv:2607.25600) routes on black-box verbalized confidence, cutting passages 20.4% at equal F1; hidden-state confidence-shift reranking (arXiv:2509.06472) reports +4.70% end-to-end accuracy and −7.10% retrieval cost. KbPO (ACL 2026) is the RL instantiation. Common finding: raw uncertainty signals are poorly calibrated absolutely; the calibrated decision interface is the reusable artifact.

**Trace UX is validated but risk-prone.** CHI '26 studies show users audit stepwise rationales and calibrate trust on certainty framing; incorrect rationales paired with strong certainty disproportionately damage trust. LinkQ's state-diagram + live-action-pill study (14 practitioners) found trace displays improved mental models but could induce overtrust — trace visibility is not automatically safety-positive.

**Bengali/agriculture competitors share one blind spot.** Farmer.Chat, KrishokBondhu, and the cross-lingual Bengali system all stop at retrieval + generation. KrishokBondhu's post-processing is a coherence check; the cross-lingual system's safety story is OOD rejection. None implements claim-level verification, refusal metrics, or a logged escalation path. The medical systems (ClinicBot, atomic fact-checking) do, but only for English clinical content.

## 3. Research gaps

**Gap A — No agricultural advisory system performs claim-level verification with numeric-dosage drift detection.** Medical verifiers exist (MedRAGChecker: drug–disease KG relations; ClinicBot: numeric string-match on clinical thresholds; atomic fact-checking: catches incorrect drug dosages), and generic verifiers exist (RT4CHART, VeriCite). No agriculture-domain verifier exists, and no verifier anywhere targets agrochemical dose/rate units (e.g., mL per 15L water, gm/decimal). The team's own benchmark paper supplies the empirical requirement: its chemical-provenance audit shows a persistent chemical hallucination floor of 4.05–7.00% across six architecturally different systems even when the gold evidence is handed to the model (§5.3, EACL 2026), and its strongest zero-shot baseline still produces a verifiably correct treatment recommendation on fewer than half of instances (43.64%). Dosages must therefore be retrieved-and-verified, never generated.

**Gap B — No refusal/selective-answering benchmark exists for low-resource-language, safety-critical domain content.** RefusalBench (NQ, GaRAGe), AbstentionBench, and RAGREFUSE are English, generic-domain (RAGREFUSE includes chemical domains but in English and not agricultural practice). No benchmark perturbs Bengali agrochemical queries across RefusalBench's six uncertainty categories (ambiguity, contradiction, missing info, false premises, granularity, epistemic mismatch). KrishokTech's 20,112-record safety dataset is positioned to fill this.

**Gap C — No system-track paper evaluates user-facing agent traces with a farmer population.** CHI '26 evidence (rationale auditability, certainty-framing effects, trace-induced overtrust) is lab-based with generic tasks. My Climate CoPilot evaluates with 50 domain experts but not the trace UI; LinkQ is a 14-practitioner workshop study on knowledge-graph querying. Nobody has measured whether a stepper-style agent trace changes trust and follow-through in low-literacy extension-service users, where the overtrust risk is highest.

## 4. System/application-track conventions observed

- **ACL System Demonstrations** (e.g., My Climate CoPilot, 2025.acl-demo.7): 8–10 pages, system-architecture figure, transparency/privacy emphasis, expert evaluation (50 experts), automatic evaluation, live demo at conference, demo URL + code.
- **EMNLP Industry** (DecEx-RAG, PrismRAG): production framing, benchmark + deployment evidence, released code; PrismRAG reports +5.4% factuality over 12 benchmarks and "deployed in production."
- **ACL Industry** (Faithful Industrial RAG): offline eval + 2-week A/B test (92.7% hallucination reduction; latency 2.5s→3.1s) + multi-month deployment; reviewers expect honest cost/latency accounting.
- **Evaluation expectations**: claim-level metrics over response-level (RagChecker, RT4CHART, AwF precision/recall), abstention metrics (AbstainAccuracy/AbstainF1, risk–coverage curves), LLM-judge + human verification (RefusalBench reports 93.1% human agreement), and explicit negative results (AB-RAG, BeyondUncertainty report failed signals openly).
- **Artifacts**: code + benchmarks are now table stakes (RefusalBench, VeriCite, DecEx-RAG, MAO-ARAG all release code); dataset releases with licenses (KrishokTech dataset on HF `RaiyanKhaan/krishokChat` is CC-BY-4.0); demo videos and live URLs for demo tracks.

## 5. Positioning recommendations for KrishokTech

1. **Lead with the verifier.** Position KrishokTech as the first agricultural advisory system with claim-level verification (RT4CHART-style entailed/contradicted/baseless labeling) plus a numeric-dosage whitelist check — the agrochemical analogue of ClinicBot's string-match and MedRAGChecker's KG check. This is the defensible novelty Gap A.
2. **Report refusal metrics explicitly** (AbstainAccuracy, AbstainF1, risk–coverage) on the 6-way router, and cite RefusalBench's "<50% multi-document refusal accuracy" finding to frame selective refusal as an engineered capability, not an LLM default.
3. **Differentiate against the two January–October 2026 Bengali competitors by citation**: 2601.02065 (translation bottleneck, no claim verification, no audit log) and 2510.18355 (no safety routing, no refusal). The 2,120-node national-institution corpus plus the 16123 escalation path is your coverage argument.
4. **Anchor prior work on the team's two papers**: the KrishokTech benchmark (EACL 2026 — Reza, Nimi & Shahid) and the AgRiTrust retrieval benchmark (Reza, Maria & Nimi), local copies in `paper/done papers/`. Frame the system paper as the pipeline-level extension of the benchmark paper: retrieval is already measured (AgRiTrust: Hybrid RRF R@10 0.539 over 2,882 nodes), generation is already measured (KrishokTech-4B General QA Token F1 0.314), and the unmeasured layer is exactly the verifier between them.
5. **Structure per EMNLP Industry conventions**: architecture + agent-trace figure, benchmark on authentic farmer queries with claim-level and abstention metrics, expert human evaluation, latency/cost table, and release artifacts (code, dataset, demo video). Add a small farmer-panel evaluation of the trace stepper — Gap C is currently unoccupied.
