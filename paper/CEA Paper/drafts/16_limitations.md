# Section 16: Limitations & Threats to Validity (Draft Skeleton)

## 16.1 Scope of Crop & Pathology Coverage
- Current fact base covers major national staple and commercial crops (Potato, Rice, Brinjal, Tomato, Wheat, Mango, Chili); expansion to secondary minor crops requires further agronomic validation.

## 16.2 Absence of Longitudinal Yield Outcomes
- Empirical evaluation measures advisory correctness, fail-closed safety, and systems resilience; it does not measure multi-season field yield impact.

## 16.3 Simulation Boundaries
- Network profiles simulated via standard cellular traffic parameters; physical field trials subject to dynamic local telecom variations.
