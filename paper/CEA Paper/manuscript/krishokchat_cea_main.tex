\begin{abstract}
Large Language Models and standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines are fluent in agricultural extension but lack factual grounding guarantees. In crop protection, a fluent sentence can silently misbind a pesticide to the wrong crop, inflate a dosage, or drop a mandatory Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI), producing failures that remain confident-sounding and dangerous. We present the \textbf{Bounded-Authority Advisory Architecture (BAA)}, which separates factual authority from linguistic realization: the generative model may phrase and explain, but it cannot invent or mutate safety-critical agronomic values. BAA enforces an 11-slot, single-record, fail-closed certification contract and selectively routes queries among deterministic resolution, grounded generation, clarification, abstention, and escalation.

On 10,000 adversarial relational-misbinding attacks, BAA rejects \MisbindDetRate{} of corrupted chemical claims (95\% Wilson CI [99.96, 100.0]), against 80.0\% dangerous acceptance for a lexical baseline and 38.65\% for an LLM-as-a-judge verifier. Calibrated abstention sustains 84.56\% advisory coverage at a 1.26\% selective-risk level across 20,112 held-out cases. An analytical model of the deterministic resolution ladder projects 61.5\% zero-LLM query resolution, cutting weighted latency by a projected 2.28$\times$ and per-query cost by 89.7\% under stated deployment parameters. The results demonstrate that subordinating generative language to bounded factual authority yields a dependable, auditable, and affordable foundation for safety-critical agricultural decision support.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Acknowledgments}
The authors thank the Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE), the Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI), and the Bangladesh Rice Research Institute (BRRI) for public access to institutional Krishi handbooks and agronomic guidance. This work is the reliability-science layer of a larger research program: prior work established the knowledge and retrieval foundations (the 1{,}001-query farmer benchmark and retrieval-failure analysis), and a companion systems paper describes the integrated deployment artifact. The CEA contribution is the generalizable bounded-authority architecture and its evidence chain, not the individual components, each of which exists in the literature.
