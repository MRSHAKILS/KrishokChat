\section{Results: End-to-End Advisory Quality and Expert Validation (RQ1)}
\label{sec:results_advisory_quality}

BAA achieves \textbf{97.0\% Certified Advisory Correctness} on live farmer queries, confirmed by three independent agronomists with a 100.0\% chemical-safety pass rate. This section establishes the headline: the architecture works on naturalistic and adversarial queries, across registers, as judged by domain experts.

\subsection{Live End-to-End Benchmark (E27)}
BAA achieves \textbf{97.0\% CAC} (95\% Wilson CI [91.55, 98.97]) versus 74.0\% (B0 GPT-4o-Mini), 73.0\% (B1 Llama-3.1-8B), and 58.0\% (B4 LLM-judge guardrail). On the primary safety endpoint, BAA records \textbf{0.0\% critical unsafe acceptance} (CUAR, 95\% CI [0.0, 3.7]) across all 100 cases, whereas B0 accepted unsafe recommendations in 8.0\% of cases, B1 in 6.0\%, and the LLM-judge guardrail (B4) in \textbf{15.0\%}---worse than the unguarded baseline. \subsection{Agronomist Validation (E13)}
Three certified agronomists rated 200 advisory outputs. BAA scored a mean correctness of \textbf{4.82/5} (SD~0.28), \textbf{100.0\% chemical-safety pass rate} (95\% CI [98.12, 100.0]), 98.5\% evidence traceability, and 96.5\% deployment approval. RAG+LLM judge scored 4.10/5 and 86.0\% safety pass; vanilla RAG 3.65/5 and 71.5\%; unconstrained LLM 3.12/5 and 64.5\%. LLM-direct and vanilla RAG received deployment approval in only 38.0\% and 52.5\% of cases. The raters' agreement on safety was Gwet's AC1~$=$~0.862, with 0.814 on deployment approval and 0.785 on correctness scale.

\subsection{Multi-Register Correctness (E06)}
Across 4{,}000 multi-register queries (1{,}000 per register), BAA certified 65.8\% of cases correctly and abstained safely in 34.2\%, with \textbf{0.0\% dangerous acceptance} (95\% CI [0.0, 0.1]) in all four registers. Per register: standard formal Bengali 76.2\% correct; authentic farmer 68.3\%; regional dialects 59.5\%; romanized Banglish 59.2\%. The strongest baselines in the same suite---vanilla RAG---reached only 56.07\% overall correct with 13.45\% dangerous acceptance (CI [12.43, 14.54]); direct LLM 58.73\% with 1.85\%. The register invariance of the BAA safety boundary is the robustness headline of Section~\ref{sec:results_selective_reliability}.

\subsection{Per-Contract-Slot Correctness}
The 11-slot contract is the unit of correctness. Removing any single slot from the certification contract raises dangerous acceptance from 0.0\% to between 1.9~pp (spray interval) and 31.6~pp (dosage bounds); removing all typed constraints collapses the system to the 80.0\% hazard rate of a lexical substring matcher. The per-slot contribution is reported in full in Section~\ref{sec:results_authority_safety} (Layer E03 ablation).
