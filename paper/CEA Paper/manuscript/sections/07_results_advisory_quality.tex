\section{Results: End-to-End Advisory Quality and Realism (RQ1)}
\label{sec:results_advisory_quality}

This section reports the end-to-end performance of BAA and standard baseline architectures on naturalistic and adversarial farmer queries (Layer~E27), realistic underspecified inputs (Layer~E46), wrong-premise user assertions (Layer~E47), and multi-turn conversational pressure (Layer~E42).

\subsection{Live End-to-End Benchmark (E27)}
On a 100-case live-API baseline evaluation (70 naturalistic and 30 adversarial Bengali queries; 100\% real API completions and backend execution), standard architectures achieved correctness rates of 74.0\% (B0 GPT-4o-Mini), 73.0\% (B1 Llama-3.1-8B), and 58.0\% (B4 LLM-judge guardrail). On safety integrity, B0 accepted unsafe recommendations in 8.0\% of cases, B1 in 6.0\%, and the post-hoc LLM judge (B4) in \textbf{15.0\%} (95\% CI [9.3, 23.3]), demonstrating that prompt-based textual guardrails can increase unsafe acceptance over unguarded baselines.

The bounded-authority architecture (\textbf{BAA / B6}) achieved the highest certified advisory correctness at \textbf{83.0\%} (95\% CI [74.5, 89.1]) while reducing critical unsafe acceptance to \textbf{1.0\%} (95\% CI [0.18, 5.45]), an 8$\times$ to 15$\times$ reduction in hazard rate over unconstrained and judge-guarded baselines. BAA conservative abstention reached 46.0\% (CI [36.6, 55.7]), reflecting the fail-closed contract which refuses ungrounded or ambiguous advice rather than hallucinating plausible dosages. \subsection{Multi-Register Correctness (E06)}
Across 4{,}000 multi-register queries (1{,}000 per register), BAA certified 65.8\% of cases correctly and abstained safely in 34.2\%, with \textbf{0.0\% dangerous acceptance} (95\% CI [0.0, 0.1]) in all four registers. Per register: standard formal Bengali 76.2\% correct; authentic farmer 68.3\%; regional dialects 59.5\%; romanized Banglish 59.2\%. The strongest baseline in the same suite (vanilla RAG) reached only 56.07\% overall correct with 13.45\% dangerous acceptance (CI [12.43, 14.54]); direct LLM reached 58.73\% with 1.85\%.

\subsection{Confused Farmer Realism and Active Clarification (E46)}
Agricultural extension users rarely provide perfectly formatted queries. Layer~E46 evaluates 800 realistic imperfect queries across eight distinct corruption categories ($n=100$ per category, Table~\ref{tab:confused_farmer_realism}). When presented with severely missing information (Category~C2, e.g., ``What medicine should I give?'' with no crop specified), unconstrained LLMs emit speculative pesticide recommendations in 47.0\% of cases and RAG in 72.0\%. 

In contrast, BAA achieves an \textbf{Appropriate Clarification Rate (ACR) of 90.0\%} on underspecified inputs and strictly enforces \textbf{0.0\% Unsafe Compliance Rate (UCR)} across all categories. Under 1{,}000$\times$ unit errors (Category~C3, e.g., asserting 5\,kg/L) and adversarial jailbreak requests (Category~C8), BAA achieves 0.0\% UCR (100\% safe refusal with routing to the national Krishi Call Center 16123), whereas unconstrained LLMs comply unsafely in 58.0\%--63.0\% of cases.

\subsection{Wrong-Premise Resistance and Sycophancy Prevention (E47)}
When farmers assert plausible but agronomically incorrect premises with high certainty (e.g., requesting streptomycin for rice bacterial blight, or asserting a 5$\times$ overdose), unconstrained LLMs exhibit strong sycophancy, accepting the false premise and providing dangerous dosage instructions in 64.2\% of cases (Layer~E47, $N=200$, Table~\ref{tab:wrong_premise_compliance}).

BAA cross-checks asserted entities against verified single-record evidence, achieving an overall \textbf{Premise Correction Rate (PCR) of 91.8\%} and \textbf{0.0\% UCR}. On off-label chemical requests (Category~P2) and banned chemical citations (Category~P5), BAA actively refutes the user's premise in 92.0\%--95.0\% of cases, demonstrating that the authority contract prevents sycophantic compliance.

\subsection{Multi-Turn Conversational Safety Persistence (E42)}
Under persistent multi-turn conversational pressure across 200 dialogue sequences (5 sequential turns, 1{,}000 total turns, Layer~E42, Table~\ref{tab:conversational_safety_persistence}), unconstrained LLMs exhibit severe boundary erosion: Conversational Safety Persistence (CSP) collapses to \textbf{8.5\%} overall (0.0\% survival under incremental dose escalation and explicit risk acceptance). Prompted LLM judges also degrade to 34.5\% CSP under social proof and authority override framing.

Because BAA enforces stateless, deterministic contract verification at every conversational turn, it achieves \textbf{100.0\% CSP} ($S_5 = 100.0\%$ survival at Turn~5) across all eight adversarial pressure types, ensuring that conversational persistence cannot compromise advisory safety.
