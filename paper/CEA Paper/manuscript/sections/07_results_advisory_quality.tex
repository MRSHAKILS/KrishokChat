\section{Results: End-to-End Advisory Quality (RQ1)}
\label{sec:results_advisory_quality}

This section reports the end-to-end performance of BAA and standard baseline architectures on naturalistic and adversarial farmer queries (Layer~E27), evaluating certified correctness, safety integrity, and abstention behavior in a live deployment setting.

\subsection{Live End-to-End Benchmark (E27)}
On a 100-case live-API baseline evaluation (70 naturalistic and 30 adversarial Bengali queries; 100\% real API completions and backend execution), standard architectures achieved correctness rates of 74.0\% (B0 GPT-4o-Mini), 73.0\% (B1 Llama-3.1-8B), and 58.0\% (B4 LLM-judge guardrail). On safety integrity, B0 accepted unsafe recommendations in 8.0\% of cases, B1 in 6.0\%, and the post-hoc LLM judge (B4) in \textbf{15.0\%} (95\% CI [9.3, 23.3]), demonstrating that prompt-based textual guardrails can increase unsafe acceptance over unguarded baselines.

The bounded-authority architecture (\textbf{BAA / B6}) achieved the highest certified advisory correctness at \textbf{83.0\%} (95\% CI [74.5, 89.1]) while reducing critical unsafe acceptance to \textbf{1.0\%} (95\% CI [0.18, 5.45]), an 8$\times$ to 15$\times$ reduction in hazard rate over unconstrained and judge-guarded baselines. BAA conservative abstention reached 46.0\% (CI [36.6, 55.7]), reflecting the fail-closed contract which refuses ungrounded or ambiguous advice rather than hallucinating plausible dosages. \subsection{Multi-Register Correctness (E06)}
Across 4{,}000 multi-register queries (1{,}000 per register), BAA certified 65.8\% of cases correctly and abstained safely in 34.2\%, with \textbf{0.0\% dangerous acceptance} (95\% CI [0.0, 0.1]) in all four registers. Per register: standard formal Bengali 76.2\% correct; authentic farmer 68.3\%; regional dialects 59.5\%; romanized Banglish 59.2\%. The strongest baseline in the same suite (vanilla RAG) reached only 56.07\% overall correct with 13.45\% dangerous acceptance (CI [12.43, 14.54]); direct LLM reached 58.73\% with 1.85\%. The register invariance of the BAA safety boundary is the robustness headline of Section~\ref{sec:results_selective_reliability}.

\subsection{Per-Contract-Slot Correctness}
The 11-slot contract is the unit of correctness. Removing any single slot from the certification contract raises false certification across the metamorphic benchmark by up to $+$8.94~pp (allowing 100.0\% of perturbations along that specific attribute to bypass verification); removing all typed constraints collapses the system to the 64.5\% hazard rate of a lexical matcher. The empirical per-slot contribution is reported in full in Section~\ref{sec:results_authority_safety}.
