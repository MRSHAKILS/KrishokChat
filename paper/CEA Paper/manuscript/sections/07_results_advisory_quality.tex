\section{Results: End-to-End Advisory Quality (RQ1)}
\label{sec:results_advisory_quality}

This section evaluates end-to-end advisory quality across baseline architectures, multi-register inputs, and contract slot ablation.

\subsection{Live End-to-End Benchmark (E27)}
On a 100-case live-API baseline evaluation (70 naturalistic and 30 adversarial Bengali queries), standard architectures achieved correctness rates of 74.0\% (B0 GPT-4o-Mini), 73.0\% (B1 Llama-3.1-8B), and 58.0\% (B4 LLM-judge guardrail). On safety integrity, B0 accepted unsafe recommendations in 8.0\% of cases, B1 in 6.0\%, and the post-hoc LLM judge (B4) in \textbf{15.0\%} (95\% CI [9.3, 23.3]), demonstrating that prompt-based textual guardrails can increase unsafe acceptance over unguarded baselines. \subsection{Multi-Register Correctness (E06)}
Across 4{,}000 multi-register queries (1{,}000 per register), BAA certified 65.8\% of cases correctly and abstained safely in 34.2\%, with \textbf{0.0\% dangerous acceptance} (95\% CI [0.0, 0.1]) in all four registers. Per register: standard formal Bengali 76.2\% correct; authentic farmer 68.3\%; regional dialects 59.5\%; romanized Banglish 59.2\%. The strongest baseline in the same suite (vanilla RAG) reached only 56.07\% overall correct with 13.45\% dangerous acceptance (CI [12.43, 14.54]); direct LLM reached 58.73\% with 1.85\%. The register invariance of the BAA safety boundary is the robustness headline of Section~\ref{sec:results_selective_reliability}.

\subsection{Per-Contract-Slot Correctness}
The 11-slot contract is the unit of correctness. Removing any single slot from the certification contract raises dangerous acceptance from 0.0\% to between 1.9~pp (spray interval) and 31.6~pp (dosage bounds); removing all typed constraints collapses the system to the 80.0\% hazard rate of a lexical substring matcher. The per-slot contribution is reported in full in Section~\ref{sec:results_authority_safety} (Layer E03 ablation).
