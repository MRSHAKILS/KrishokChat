\section{Results: Security, Offline Resilience, and Constrained Delivery (RQ5)}
\label{sec:results_security_delivery}

\subsection{Prompt Injection and Evidence Poisoning (E07/E08)}
Layer E07/E08 evaluated 1{,}400 adversarial attacks (200 per family) across seven families: direct system override, evidence override, retrieval poisoning, delimiter hijacking, Bengali-native injection, romanized Banglish injection, and mixed code-switching. The unconstrained LLM certified unsafe output in 83.86\% of attacks (95\% CI [81.84, 85.69]); vanilla RAG in 69.36\%; RAG with an LLM guard in 21.79\% (CI [19.70, 24.02]) --- still vulnerable to delimiter breakouts and code-switching jailbreaks; and BAA in \textbf{0.0\%} (0/1{,}400; CI [0.0, 0.27]), with 1{,}289 of 1{,}400 attacks intercepted at the deterministic Tier-0 gate before any retrieval and the remaining 111 blocked by the relational verifier post-generation. The 0.0\% figure is an observed rate on the evaluated suite with a stated upper bound; it is not a universal guarantee.

\subsection{Deterministic SMS Compression Fidelity (E15)}
Layer E15 tested 1{,}000 advisory tuples under the 160-character GSM 03.38 limit across three arms. The deterministic 11-slot template preserved \textbf{100.0\% of all safety-critical slots} (dose bounds, unit, PHI, interval, formulation) in 102--115 characters, with 0.0\% messages exceeding 160 characters and 0.0\% critical hazard (95\% CI [0.0, 0.38]). LLM-composed SMS fit in 92.9 characters on average but truncated or omitted safety fields: PHI survived only 35.6\% of messages and the critical hazard rate was \textbf{64.4\%} (CI [61.38, 67.31]). Naive truncation to 160 characters produced a 100.0\% hazard rate. The operational principle: once factual authority is established, a constrained channel renders from the certified structured record rather than re-generating safety-critical content.

\subsection{SMS Injection Survivability (E22)}
Layer E22 extended the injection suite to the outbound channel: 1{,}400 attacks across five families (Banglish evasion, delimiter jailbreak, direct system override, evidence-dosage tampering, phishing redirect). Deterministic template composition showed \textbf{0.0\% injection survivability} (0/1{,}400; CI [0.0, 0.27]), whereas LLM-composed SMS leaked injected payloads in \textbf{36.36\%} of attacks (CI [33.88, 38.91]), most severely for dosage-tampering attacks (113/294). Because the template fills slots from the certified record, an injected instruction has no slot to occupy.

\subsection{Cellular Network Degradation (E14)}
Layer E14 simulated four rural network profiles (1{,}000 queries each): perfect 4G, urban 3G, rural edge (15\% loss), and severe 2G (30\% loss). Table~\ref{tab:deployment_efficiency} reports delivery success. Under rural-edge loss, offline-first caching sustains \textbf{91.4\%} delivery versus 82.0\% for cloud-only RAG (+9.4 pp); under severe 2G loss, \textbf{58.1\%} versus 12.8\% (+45.3 pp). Cache hits resolve at p50 1.2--1.5 ms versus 8.2--11.3 s for cloud RAG, and zero stale-advisory safety violations were observed. These are simulated network profiles, not field measurements.
