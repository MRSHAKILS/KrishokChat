\section{Deployment Implications}
\label{sec:deployment_implications}

\subsection{Rural Connectivity and Offline Evidence}
The E14 network results translate directly to deployment reality in Bangladesh's rural grid: at 15\% packet loss, an offline-first cache sustains 91.4\% delivery versus 82.0\% for cloud-only RAG, and at 30\% loss the gap widens to 58.1\% versus 12.8\%. The offline fact pack (SHA-256-chained, 92.8\% bandwidth-reduced differential updates, E23) means deterministic resolution and verification require no network at all; the network is needed only for the generative tier.

\subsection{Low-End Devices}
The on-device profile shows lightweight ONNX and TFLite edge models (including the 10-class YOLO26s crop gate at $20.82$\,MB, $99.55\%$ test top-1, the 8-class Chilli specialist at $5.91$\,MB ONNX with $97.50\%$ field accuracy, and the 10-class Rice disease specialist covering Tungro virus at $41.48$\,MB ONNX / $10.78$\,MB INT8, $96.25\%$ field accuracy across $n$=80) operating at 26--48\,ms inference on mobile CPU and completely eliminating cross-crop toxic pesticide hazards ($0.0\%$ residual routing). Together with the 1.25\,MB intent router and the deterministic resolver ($\le$0.94\,ms p95), the safety-critical perception and verification core runs entirely on commodity Android hardware; the LLM tier remains optional and cloud/API-based where available.

\subsection{Extension-Service Escalation}
Abstention is not abandonment: every fail-closed refusal redirects to the national Krishi Call Center (16123) or an agricultural extension officer. In the KrishokChat deployment, abstention is never a dead end. Every abstention message includes: (i)~the specific missing information, (ii)~a suggested clarification question, and (iii)~the 16123 helpline number. The farmer experience is not ``the system refused'' but ``the system asked for more detail and offered a human backup.'' For a national extension service, the practical implication is a division of labor: the machine handles the certifiable majority, and the human network absorbs the abstained minority at a bounded, measurable rate.

\subsection{Knowledge Maintenance and Regulatory Updates}
The E24 loop shows knowledge maintenance is a data operation, not a code operation: 18 minutes closed a real coverage gap. Regulatory changes (e.g., a newly banned active ingredient) are handled by the polarity registry and temporal validity fields; the E30 result shows obsolete records cannot be certified once the manifest is updated. An operator can therefore keep the system current without touching the model.

\subsection{Auditability and Monitoring}
Every certified answer carries a provenance chain (record hash, source, version, date) and every interaction writes an audit event (query, risk class, route, resolution tier, evidence version, model, verification, final action, latency). This makes the system auditable per-answer --- a requirement for public-sector deployment and a differentiator from chatbot-only advisory tools.

\subsection{What Is Not Claimed}
We have not conducted countrywide field deployment; we report simulated network profiles rather than field connectivity, and the vision modality is classification-based (task: classify) rather than detection-based. The deployment implications above are architectural and model-based, not deployment measurements.
