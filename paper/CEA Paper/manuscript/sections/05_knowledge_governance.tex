\section{Knowledge Base Construction and Governance}
\label{sec:knowledge_governance}

\subsection{Institutional Corpus and Source Provenance}
The factual foundation of KrishokChat is a curated corpus of 2{,}946 institutional source documents published by accredited Bangladeshi agricultural institutions, including:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{BARI Krishi Projukti Hatboi (9th Edition, 2023):} crop technology manuals covering field crops, tubers, and horticultural varieties \citep{bari_handbook2023}.
    \item \textbf{BRRI Adhunik Dhaner Chas (22nd Edition, 2024):} canonical rice production and pathology guidelines \citep{brri_handbook2024}.
    \item \textbf{DAE/MoA agrochemical governance registries:} current lists of approved, restricted, and banned pesticides in Bangladesh (regulatory polarity source).
\end{itemize}
Every node in the fact store carries provenance: source document, source version, effective date, expiry date, verifier identity, and a cryptographic evidence hash.

\subsection{The 11-Slot Evidence Schema and Authority Stratification}
As detailed in Table~\ref{tab:knowledge_schema}, the knowledge base decouples the agronomic decision contract (Layer A: the 11 semantic slots) from the evidence governance layer (Layer B: source authority, publication/effective dates, regulatory polarity, provenance hash, verifier signature). Sources are stratified into a strict authority hierarchy:
\begin{align}
    \text{MoA Regulatory} &\succ \text{Institutional (BARI/BRRI)} \nonumber \\
    &\succ \text{Legacy Institutional} \succ \text{Secondary}
\end{align}
When multiple sources overlap, the resolver enforces precedence by authority rank then by publication timestamp, and records that have been superseded or revoked are invalidated. A record must be both authoritative (by source/jurisdiction) and current (by date/version/regulatory state) to satisfy Eq.~\eqref{eq:certification_rule}.

\subsection{Cryptographic Hash-Chaining and Tamper-Evident Packs}
Structured facts are compiled into an offline SQLite fact pack for edge and offline deployment. Each record is hashed with SHA-256 and incorporated into a Merkle-style chain; updates are distributed as differential delta packs rather than full downloads. In Layer E23, 1,000 single-bit and swap mutations were injected into the chain; the client detected 100.0\% of them (95\% CI [99.62, 100.0]), with a delta payload of 791 bytes versus a 10{,}985-byte full pack (92.8\% bandwidth reduction) and sub-millisecond chain verification (p95 0.1919 ms). We frame this experiment as a deployment-integrity result, not a cryptography contribution: it ensures the offline evidence base cannot be silently altered or replayed.

\subsection{Knowledge Ingestion and Maintenance Loop}
No model output is ever written directly into the fact store. The ingestion pipeline is a controlled chain: authoritative source $\rightarrow$ document staging $\rightarrow$ fact extraction $\rightarrow$ schema validation $\rightarrow$ conflict detection $\rightarrow$ expert review $\rightarrow$ versioning/hash/signature $\rightarrow$ release to the fact store. When recurring queries fail at Tier 4 because a knowledge gap exists, the failures are aggregated into a prioritized authoring queue. Layer E24 evaluated the marginal cost of closing one gap: a targeted authoring session for chili anthracnose (BARI Krishi Projukti Hatboi 2021, p.~148) added two validated facts (azoxystrobin+difenoconazole 325 SC; copper oxychloride 50 WP) in 18 minutes, closing the cluster gap completely (55/55 queries, 100.0\%), lifting system coverage from 58.7\% to 64.2\% (95\% CI [61.18, 67.11]) --- a 5.5 pp absolute gain at 3.06 newly resolvable queries per authoring minute. We report this as a single-cluster case study, not a productivity distribution.

\subsection{Ingestion Economics, Schema Constraints, and Cross-Region Scalability}
A crucial practical consideration for deploying bounded-authority systems is the human-in-the-loop cost of knowledge curation. Extracting 11 highly specific agronomic slots from unstructured government PDFs requires semi-automated regex/pattern extractors coupled with agronomist double-blind validation, taking approximately 18--25 person-minutes per crop-disease profile. The automated schema compiler rejects invalid or out-of-bound numerical ranges, preventing typographical errors from entering the hash-chain.

Furthermore, regarding packaging constraints, while \texttt{dose\_min} and \texttt{dose\_max} are modeled as continuous floating-point variables for strict mathematical interval bounding, the schema natively handles discrete packaging formats (e.g., ``1 sachet (5\,g) per 10\,L water'') via the solvent volume denominator ($v_s$) and formulation ($f$) fields. Expanding the system to new crops or neighboring agro-climatic zones (e.g., West Bengal, Assam) does not require retraining or fine-tuning underlying neural models; it requires only compiling the new jurisdiction's official pesticide gazette into the 11-slot relational schema. The bounded authority architecture is thus bounded by deterministic gazette curation rather than by machine learning retraining bottlenecks.
