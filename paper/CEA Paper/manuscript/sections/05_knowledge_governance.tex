\section{Knowledge Base Construction and Governance}
\label{sec:knowledge_governance}

\subsection{Institutional Corpus and Source Provenance}
The factual foundation of KrishokChat is a curated corpus of 2{,}946 institutional source documents published by accredited Bangladeshi agricultural institutions, covering 47~field crops, 312~pest/disease entries across 9~agro-ecological zones, and 189~active ingredient profiles with regulatory polarity tags:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{BARI Krishi Projukti Hatboi (9th Edition, 2023):} crop technology manuals covering field crops, tubers, and horticultural varieties \citep{bari_handbook2023}.
    \item \textbf{BRRI Adhunik Dhaner Chas (22nd Edition, 2024):} canonical rice production and pathology guidelines \citep{brri_handbook2024}.
    \item \textbf{DAE/MoA agrochemical governance registries:} current lists of approved, restricted, and banned pesticides in Bangladesh (regulatory polarity source).
\end{itemize}
Every node in the fact store carries provenance: source document, source version, effective date, expiry date, verifier identity, and a cryptographic evidence hash.

\subsection{The 11-Slot Evidence Schema and Authority Stratification}
As detailed in Table~\ref{tab:knowledge_schema}, the knowledge base decouples the agronomic decision contract (Layer~A: the 11 semantic slots) from the evidence governance layer (Layer~B: source authority, publication/effective dates, regulatory polarity, provenance hash, verifier signature). Sources are stratified into a strict authority hierarchy:
\begin{align}
    \text{MoA Regulatory} &\succ \text{Institutional (BARI/BRRI)} \nonumber \\
    &\succ \text{Legacy Institutional} \succ \text{Secondary}
\end{align}
For example, if a 2024 MoA gazette bans an active ingredient that a 2021 BARI manual still recommends, the MoA record takes precedence: the older BARI record fails the $\operatorname{Authority}(e)$ and $\operatorname{Current}(e)$ predicates simultaneously and cannot be certified. When multiple sources overlap, the resolver enforces precedence by authority rank then by publication timestamp. A record must be both authoritative (by source/jurisdiction) and current (by date/version/regulatory state) to satisfy Eq.~\eqref{eq:certification_rule}.

\subsection{Cryptographic Hash-Chaining and Tamper-Evident Packs}
Structured facts are compiled into an offline SQLite fact pack for edge and offline deployment. Each record is hashed with SHA-256 and incorporated into a Merkle-style chain (each record hash is incorporated as a leaf of an incremental hash tree; the root hash changes detectably on any leaf mutation). Updates are distributed as differential delta packs rather than full downloads. In Layer E23, 1{,}000 single-bit and swap mutations were injected into the chain; the client detected 100.0\% of them (95\% CI [99.62, 100.0]), with a delta payload of 791~bytes versus a 10{,}985-byte full pack (92.8\% bandwidth reduction) and sub-millisecond chain verification (p95 0.1919~ms). This ensures offline evidence integrity without requiring full re-downloads, preventing offline facts from being silently altered or replayed.

\subsection{Knowledge Ingestion Pipeline}
No model output is ever written directly into the fact store. The ingestion pipeline is a controlled chain: authoritative source $\rightarrow$ document staging $\rightarrow$ fact extraction $\rightarrow$ schema validation $\rightarrow$ conflict detection $\rightarrow$ expert review $\rightarrow$ versioning/hash/signature $\rightarrow$ release to the fact store.

Extracting the 11 agronomic slots from unstructured government PDFs requires semi-automated regex/pattern extractors coupled with double-blind review by domain specialists, taking approximately 18--25 person-minutes per crop-disease profile. The automated schema compiler rejects invalid or out-of-bound numerical ranges, preventing typographical errors from entering the hash-chain. Discrete packaging formats (e.g., ``1 sachet (5~g) per 10~L water'') are handled natively via the solvent volume denominator ($v_s$) and formulation ($f$) fields, without requiring continuous float modeling.

\subsection{Knowledge Maintenance and Regional Scalability}
When recurring queries fail at Tier~4 because a knowledge gap exists, the failures are aggregated into a prioritized authoring queue. Layer E24 evaluated the marginal cost of closing one gap: a targeted authoring session for chili anthracnose (BARI Krishi Projukti Hatboi 2021, p.~148) added two validated facts in 18~minutes, closing the cluster gap completely (55/55 queries resolved), lifting system coverage from 58.7\% to 64.2\% (95\% CI [61.18, 67.11]), a 5.5~pp absolute gain at 3.06 newly resolvable queries per authoring minute. Crucially, expanding the system to new crops or neighboring agro-climatic zones (e.g., West Bengal, Assam) requires only compiling the new jurisdiction's official pesticide gazette into the 11-slot relational schema; no neural model retraining is required.
