\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented the Bounded-Authority Advisory Architecture (BAA), a decision-support framework that separates factual authority from linguistic realization in agricultural AI. By binding every crop-protection recommendation to an 11-slot, single-record, fail-closed contract, BAA prevents large language models from inventing, mutating, or misbinding safety-critical chemical dosages, pre-harvest intervals, and application guidelines.

Across 36 experimental layers spanning $\approx$60,000 cases, the architecture demonstrates that factual authority can be rigorously bounded without sacrificing conversational utility. BAA achieves 100.0\% rejection on 10,000 adversarial misbindings and 11,000 metamorphic mutations, provides 97.0\% certified correctness on live farmer queries with zero critical safety hazards, and sustains 84.56\% coverage at 1.26\% selective risk. The five-tier selective resolution policy resolves 61.5\% of queries deterministically with zero LLM invocation, achieving a 2.28$\times$ latency speedup, 89.7\% serving cost reduction, and 100.0\% safety-slot fidelity on constrained SMS delivery.

By providing verifiable provenance chains and tamper-evident offline execution, BAA demonstrates that reliable agricultural computing in low-resource environments does not require trusting black-box generative parameters. Future work will extend this framework to longitudinal agronomic field trials in partnership with national extension services, evaluating real-world crop yield preservation and applicator safety.
