\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced and evaluated the Bounded-Authority Advisory Architecture (BAA), an agricultural decision-support architecture in which factual authority is explicitly separated from generative language realization. BAA certifies every recommendation through an 11-slot, single-record, fail-closed contract: the LLM may phrase and explain, but it may not invent or mutate safety-critical agronomic values. The architecture selectively routes each query among deterministic resolution, grounded generation with verification, clarification, abstention, and escalation to the national Krishi Call Center (16123).

The evidence shows the mechanism holds across the causal chain. Typed single-record binding rejects 100.0\% of 10{,}000 relational misbindings and 100.0\% of 11{,}000 metamorphic mutations, where lexical, dense, citation, and judge baselines certify 42--82\% of corrupted claims. On live end-to-end evaluation, BAA certifies 97.0\% correct advice with 0.0\% observed critical unsafe acceptance (95\% Wilson CI [0.0, 3.7]), confirmed by a three-agronomist validation study (4.82/5; Gwet's AC1~$=$~0.862; 100.0\% chemical-safety pass), at a 33.0\% abstention cost. Calibrated abstention delivers 84.56\% coverage at 1.26\% risk. The deterministic resolution ladder eliminates LLM invocation for 61.5\% of queries, cutting modeled latency 2.28$\times$; deterministic SMS preserves 100\% of safety slots where LLM-composed SMS loses the PHI in 64.4\% of messages; and offline-first caching sustains 91.4\% delivery at 15\% packet loss.

What remains unproven is equally clear. There is no farmer-outcome data, no national-scale deployment, and no same-model replication of the escalation layer. Several newer layers lack full registry verification blocks; two exploratory layers show an unresolved CUAR inconsistency; and the small-$n$ layers cannot support the effect sizes they hint at. These define the next experimental cycle: larger independent annotation, hardened verification blocks for the live-API layers, and---ultimately---field pilots with real extension officers, where the ultimate metric is not CUAR but whether a farmer's crop survives the season.
