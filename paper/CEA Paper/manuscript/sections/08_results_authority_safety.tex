\section{Results: Safety Verification and Authority Enforcement (RQ2)}
\label{sec:results_authority_safety}

BAA rejected \textbf{100.0\% of 10,000 adversarial relational-misbinding attacks} (95\% CI [99.96, 100.0]) and achieved complete rejection across all 88 metamorphic mutation cells. The safety boundary holds across every attack family evaluated. This section traces the structural mechanism behind that result and stress-tests it from every angle.

\subsection{Relational Misbinding Defence (E02)}
Layer E02 evaluated 10{,}000 adversarial cases (10 corruption families $\times$ 1{,}000 cases), each presenting juxtaposed valid facts from different records designed to trigger relational misbinding. BAA rejected \textbf{100.0\%} (0/10{,}000 false certifications; 95\% CI [99.96, 100.0]). A lexical substring matcher certified 80.0\% of corrupted claims as safe (CI [79.20, 80.77]); dense retrieval 37.56\%; citation-alignment 43.14\%; LLM-as-judge 38.65\%; vanilla RAG 72.65\%; the partial 8-slot matcher (B5) 24.5\%. Single-record joint binding makes cross-record misbinding impossible by construction: a claim whose 11 slots cannot all be satisfied from a single accredited record hash fails certification regardless of how the slots individually appear in the retrieved pool. Table~\ref{tab:authority_verification} reports the full comparison.

\subsection{Metamorphic Authority Testing: Exhaustive Coverage of 11 Mutation Classes (E28)}
We evaluated the 11-slot certification predicate exhaustively against all 11 mutation classes across 8 base agronomic crop-protection records, yielding 88 class$\times$record coverage cells. The certification rule is deterministic; we verified empirically that the verdict is constant within each cell (zero within-cell variation across all 88 cells), consistent with the deterministic design.

\textbf{BAA (B6) rejected every case in every coverage cell} --- complete rejection across all 88 cells. This is a coverage result, not a statistical estimate: every combination of mutation class and base record was evaluated, and the predicate rejected all of them.

\textbf{The B5-vs-B6 isolation.} B5 shares all architecture with BAA except four contract slots: water volume ($v_s$), spray interval ($I_s$), pre-harvest interval (PHI), and provenance-hash integrity. B5 failed on \emph{exactly} those four mutation classes --- water volume mutation (mu\_07), interval shortening (mu\_08), PHI shortening (mu\_09), and provenance-hash corruption (mu\_11) --- and passed on the remaining seven. This one-to-one correspondence between the absent slots and the failure classes is causal, not coincidental: the missing slot is the missing check. No baseline confound can explain it.

\subsection{Counterfactual Evidence Binding (E05)}
Layer E05 tested certification under counterfactual mutations (dose scaled 5$\times$, PHI shortened to 3~days): 2{,}000 evidence pairs per system. BAA certified 0.0\% of mutated claims (95\% CI [0.0, 0.19]) while certifying 100.0\% of true evidence, giving a Counterfactual Binding Consistency (CBC) of 1.0000. Vanilla RAG certified 72.65\% of mutated claims (CI [70.65, 74.56]; CBC~0.2735), a lexical matcher 59.55\% (CBC~0.4045), and an LLM-as-judge 38.65\% (CBC~0.5705). The verifier is evidence-bound rather than prior-bound.

\subsection{Parametric-vs-Evidence Conflict (E29)}
In Layer E29, each of five LLMs was evaluated when retrieved evidence conflicted with the model's parametric prior: direct generation showed parametric intrusion rates of 30--56\%, standard RAG still leaked 8--30\%, and a prompted judge leaked 2--16\%, illustrating that generative and prompted baselines remain susceptible to parametric priors even in the presence of conflicting evidence. The BAA arm was not included in this layer's traces and is excluded from this characterisation. Note the effective sample granularity caveat reported in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\subsection{Temporal Source Authority (E30)}
In the temporal conflict benchmark (100 cases per system), standard baselines exhibited substantial vulnerability to obsolete regulatory records: the unconstrained LLM cited the obsolete record in 39.0\% of cases, lexical mixed RAG in 39.0\%, and the LLM-judge guardrail in \textbf{75.0\%} (95\% CI [65.7, 82.5]), showing that textual judges can actively prefer older, detailed text over current gazettes. The BAA arm is excluded pending controlled re-measurement.

\subsection{Cross-Modal Perception Uncertainty (E31)}
When text and visual crop identity conflicted in a 100-case suite, the unconstrained multimodal LLM delivered the wrong chemical recommendation in \textbf{54.0\%} of cases, and the LLM judge triggered clarification in only 26.0\% of conflicts. The BAA arm is excluded from this layer. Per the project's artifact constraints, perception inputs are classification labels, not detection bounding boxes.

\subsection{Slot Ablation: What Each Contract Field Buys (E03)}
Layer E03 ablated each of the 11 contract slots individually (10{,}000 cases per configuration, 13 configurations). Removing each slot raises dangerous acceptance as follows: dosage bounds $+$31.6~pp, regulatory polarity $+$17.8~pp, active ingredient $+$14.2~pp, host crop $+$11.4~pp, target pathogen $+$8.2~pp, solvent volume $+$7.1~pp, formulation $+$5.8~pp, unit $+$5.4~pp, growth stage $+$4.9~pp, PHI $+$2.7~pp, spray interval $+$1.9~pp. Removing all typed constraints collapses the system to the 80.0\% hazard of a lexical substring matcher. The dosage-bound and regulatory-polarity slots are individually load-bearing: their ablation alone creates the two largest hazard surges in the battery.
