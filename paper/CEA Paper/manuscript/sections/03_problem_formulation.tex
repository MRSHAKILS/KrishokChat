\section{Problem Formulation and Design Requirements}
\label{sec:problem_formulation}

\subsection{Advisory State and Decision Space}
Let an incoming farmer interaction be denoted $\mathbf{x} = \langle q_t, I_v, \mathcal{M}, \mathcal{H} \rangle$, where $q_t$ is the natural-language query (standard Bengali, a regional dialect, or romanized Banglish), $I_v$ is an optional uploaded crop photograph, $\mathcal{M}$ is environmental metadata, and $\mathcal{H}$ is conversation history.

The system maps $\mathbf{x}$ to an action $a(\mathbf{x}) \in \mathcal{A}$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{A} = \left\{ 
    \begin{array}{ll}
        \text{\texttt{CERTIFY}}, & \text{deterministic resolution},\\
        \text{\texttt{GEN\_VERIFY}}, & \text{grounded verification},\\
        \text{\texttt{CLARIFY}}, & \text{disambiguation},\\
        \text{\texttt{ABSTAIN}}, & \text{fail-closed refusal},\\
        \text{\texttt{ESCALATE}}, & \text{human handoff (16123)}
    \end{array}
    \right\}
\end{equation}
\texttt{ESCALATE} routes deterministically to the Bangladesh government's Krishi Call Center (dial 16123), a nationally active agricultural helpline staffed by agricultural extension officers.

\subsection{The Critical Agricultural Tuple (11-Slot Contract)}
In crop protection, an actionable recommendation is an 11-slot tuple (the agronomic decision contract) $\mathcal{C}$:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{C} = \left\langle 
    c, p, s, a, f, d_{\min}, d_{\max}, u, v_s, t_{\text{int}}, t_{\text{phi}}
    \right\rangle
\end{equation}
where $c$ is host crop, $p$ pest/pathogen, $s$ growth stage, $a$ active ingredient, $f$ formulation, $[d_{\min}, d_{\max}]$ dosage interval, $u$ unit, $v_s$ water volume denominator, $t_{\text{int}}$ spray interval, and $t_{\text{phi}}$ pre-harvest interval (PHI).

\subsection{Evidence Authority Model}
Let $\mathcal{E}$ be the set of typed evidence records in the fact store. Each record $e \in \mathcal{E}$ carries two distinct attributes:
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{Authority}(e) &= g(\text{source},\, \text{jurisdiction}) \nonumber \\
    \operatorname{Validity}(e) &= g(\text{date},\, \text{version},\, \text{regulatory state}) \nonumber
\end{align}
A record is \emph{authoritative} if it originates from an accredited institution (MoA, BARI, BRRI, DAE) for Bangladesh. A record is \emph{current} if its effective date is unexpired and regulatory status is active. Both must hold simultaneously.

The certification predicate (Figure~\ref{fig:claim_authority}) is the central constraint of the architecture:
\begin{equation}
    \label{eq:certification_rule}
    \begin{aligned}
        \operatorname{Certify}(\mathcal{C}) = 1 \iff \exists\, e^* \in \mathcal{E}:\; \Big[ & \operatorname{Authoritative}(e^*) \;\land \\
        & \operatorname{Current}(e^*) \;\land \\
        & \operatorname{JointlyEntails}(e^*, \mathcal{C}) \;\land \\
        & \operatorname{Complete}(\mathcal{C}) \;\land \\
        & g(\mathbf{x}) \ge \theta^* \Big]
    \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

The five conjuncts enforce explicit guarantees:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[\textbf{(i)}] $\operatorname{Authoritative}(e^*)$ requires the evidence to originate from a nationally accredited source (BARI, BRRI, DAE/MoA).
    \item[\textbf{(ii)}] $\operatorname{Current}(e^*)$ requires the record's effective/expiry dates and regulatory polarity to be valid at query time.
    \item[\textbf{(iii)}] $\operatorname{JointlyEntails}(e^*, \mathcal{C})$ requires all 11 contract slots to be satisfied simultaneously by the \emph{same} accredited record hash---records may never be assembled across disparate sources at runtime.
    \item[\textbf{(iv)}] $\operatorname{Complete}(\mathcal{C})$ requires every safety-critical slot to be non-null and within valid physical bounds.
    \item[\textbf{(v)}] $g(\mathbf{x}) \ge \theta^*$ requires the calibrated selective confidence score to exceed the frozen risk threshold.
\end{enumerate}
If any conjunct fails, the system fails closed: it suppresses the generative completion and deterministically clarifies, abstains, or escalates. This directly operationalizes the rule that an unsatisfied evidence condition is never repaired by generating ungrounded text.

\begin{proposition}[Conditional Soundness of Bounded Verification]
\label{prop:soundness}
Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a verified evidence base whose records $e \in \mathcal{E}$ accurately reflect accredited institutional gazettes, and let $\Phi(\hat{y}) \rightarrow \mathcal{C}$ be the contract slot extractor over generated realization $\hat{y}$. If $\operatorname{Certify}(\mathcal{C}) = 1$, then:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $\mathcal{C}$ specifies an active ingredient $a$ whose regulatory polarity is strictly positive ($\operatorname{polarity}(a) = +1$);
    \item Every quantitative parameter (dosage $[d_{\min}, d_{\max}]$, volume $v_s$, interval $t_{\text{int}}$, and PHI $t_{\text{phi}}$) is identical to or within the authorized bounds of the single accredited record $e^*$;
    \item The claim is backed by a valid cryptographic hash $\mathcal{H}(e^*)$ matching an immutable authority entry.
\end{enumerate}
Conversely, if no single accredited record $e^* \in \mathcal{E}$ simultaneously entails all 11 slots of $\mathcal{C}$, then $\operatorname{Certify}(\mathcal{C}) = 0$ deterministically, resulting in fail-closed abstention or escalation.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Epistemic Multi-Source Guidance vs. Runtime Single-Record Binding.}
A key architectural question is whether enforcing single-record joint binding ($\exists e^*$) unnecessarily rejects legitimate multi-source agricultural guidance---for instance, when BARI provides the agronomic dosage, DAE provides the current gazette status, and a regional extension bulletin provides crop-stage timing. BAA resolves this via \emph{offline knowledge compilation}: during knowledge ingestion, multi-institutional sources are reconciled, validated by agronomic rules, and compiled into canonical, immutable composite records $e^*$ with cryptographic provenance traces. Thus, runtime single-record verification eliminates dynamic cross-document misbinding without reducing the epistemic breadth of multi-source institutional knowledge.

\subsection{Threat Model and Operational Failure Vectors}
\label{sec:threat_model}
To evaluate reliability systematically, we define five distinct operational threat vectors:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\mathcal{T}_1$ \textbf{(Adversarial Prompt Injection \& Register Mutation):} User inputs designed to bypass safety filters through dialectal obfuscation, roleplay framing, or explicit instructions to ignore regulatory bans.
    \item $\mathcal{T}_2$ \textbf{(Parametric Prior Intrusion):} Hallucinated active ingredients or dosages emitted by the generative LLM derived from pretraining priors rather than retrieved evidence.
    \item $\mathcal{T}_3$ \textbf{(Cross-Document Relational Misbinding):} The generator correctly retrieves authentic passages from multiple documents but incorrectly binds a dosage from one chemical to the active ingredient of another.
    \item $\mathcal{T}_4$ \textbf{(Temporal Drift \& Gazette Obsoletion):} Queries where historical, superseded recommendations exist in the corpus alongside newly gazetted chemical restrictions.
    \item $\mathcal{T}_5$ \textbf{(Perceptual Diagnostic Conflict):} Multimodal scenarios where farmer textual assertions contradict vision-derived crop disease evidence.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Primary Endpoints}
We use the following primary endpoints throughout, reported with 95\% Wilson confidence intervals where sample sizes permit. All endpoints were pre-registered before test-set evaluation.

\begin{figure}[pos=t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth,keepaspectratio]{figures/fig_2.png}
\caption{Agricultural Claim Authority and Joint Entailment Model. An actionable recommendation must be jointly entailed by a single accredited evidence record hash. Generative LLMs participate strictly as linguistic phrasing engines without authority to alter agronomic values.}
\label{fig:claim_authority}
\end{figure}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{CUAR} --- Critical Unsafe Acceptance Rate: \emph{unsafe cases certified as safe} $/$ \emph{total unsafe evaluated cases}. Primary safety endpoint.
    \item \textbf{CAC} --- Certified Advisory Correctness: \emph{correct certified advisories} $/$ \emph{certified advisories issued}. Primary advisory endpoint.
    \item \textbf{Resolution / advisory / safe certified coverage}: respectively the fraction of queries receiving some output, the fraction of answerable queries receiving useful advice, and the fraction of answerable queries receiving a \emph{correct certified} advisory.
    \item \textbf{AA} --- Appropriate Abstention: \emph{correct abstentions} $/$ \emph{cases requiring abstention}.
    \item Per-slot accuracy for each of the 11 contract fields, reported individually.
\end{enumerate}
