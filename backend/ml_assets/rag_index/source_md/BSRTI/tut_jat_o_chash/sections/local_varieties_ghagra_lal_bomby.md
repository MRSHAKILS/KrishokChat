# Local (Old) Varieties — Ghagra & Lal Bomby

*Source: Bengali_Batch2_CropGuides.pdf (batch batch 2b), BSRTI (Bangladesh Sericulture Research & Training Institute), original pages 307-307*


---

|  Pictures | Important Characteristics  |
| --- | --- |
|  **Figure: Ghagra** | - ➤ This variety is not now using by the farmers. - ➤ Leaf size is very small, slightly coarse, green and highly lobed and rarely unlobed. - ➤ Suitable for traditional bush plantation. - ➤ Rooting ability 80% - ➤ Nutritional quality viz, leaf moisture content 70.98%, moisture retention capacity 24.58%, total minerals 8.85% , total sugar 4.85%, reducing sugar 3.47%, protein 18.50%. - ➤ Leaf yield 12.50 mt/ha/yr.  |
|  **Figure: Lal bomby** | - ➤ This variety is also rarely found in the farmer's level but now maintaining in the germplasm of BSRTI. - ➤ Suitable for traditional bush plantation. - ➤ Rooting ability 80% - ➤ Nutritional quality viz, leaf moisture content 72.99%, moisture retention capacity 36.85%, total minerals 8.10% total sugar 3.54%, reducing sugar 3.43% protein 16.99%. - ➤ Leaf yield 12.25 mt/ha/yr.  |
