# REQUIREMENTS OF ANIMAL OWNER/FARM MANAGER/WORKER AND COMPONENTS OF ANIMAL FARM/ANIMAL REARING FACILITY

# 3.1. Skills & Competencies Required for Animal Owner/Farm Manager/Worker

a) The farm owner, manager and farm workers should undergo training on the necessary knowledge and skills about the basic procedures of the farm management including-
1) proper animal handling and welfare,
2) feeding management,
3) primary animal health management,
4) medicine and biologics dispensing, administration and application,
5) animal breeding and artificial insemination (AI),
6) usage of farm tools and equipment,
7) quarantine and isolation practices,
8) biosecurity,
9) pasture and forage development and management,
10) waste management,
11) other routine farm management practices,
12) recording and record keeping, and
13) updated laws, policies and guidelines regarding animal farming.
b) Farm manager and workers should be competent in the tasks that they are required to do and their competencies should be reviewed on a regular basis.
c) The farm should have sufficient number of necessary staffs.
