# **3-weeks Heat Calendar for Livestock**

[tbl-36.md](tbl-36.md)

Make columns with 21 rows next to each other. Start with writing down the first day on the calendar and continue to write down the first 21 days on the calendar, then go to the top of the next column and continue writing down again. When a cow is seen in heat, write down her number in the cell with the date you saw the cow in heat. For example, on day 9 of the month. Then three weeks later on day 30 look to the left column and see which cows were in heat three weeks ago, so extra attention should be given to these cows when observing them.
