# Phytophthora root and stem rot
*Phytophthora drechsler*

![img-95.jpeg](sections/images/63_p81_img-95.jpeg)

Photo: Paul Bachi, University of Kentucky Research and Education Center, Bugwood.org

- Internal rotting of the stem often with few external symptoms.
- Unlike many other common Phytophthoras this attacks the roots and the middle of the stem.
