# Bacterial soft rot on cabbage

*Erwinia carotovora* subsp. *carotovora*

|   | Prevention | Monitoring | Direct Control | Direct Control | Restrictions  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Bacterial soft rot on cabbage (Photo: Bernard Sakwa, MoALF, Kenya) | - Avoid water logging by planting on raised beds or ridges. - Rotate with leguminous crops such as beans and cereal crops such as maize for three seasons to avoid build-up of the disease inoculum - Avoid injury of crop while carrying out field operations as these will be form entry points of the disease - Avoid overhead irrigation to minimize soils splash that spreads disease - Avoid harvesting during warm and moist weather | - Additional relevant crops: turnip, rutubaga, broccoli and cauliflower. - Monitor the crop daily by looking for: - Water soaked lesions which spread rapidly over and into the head, becoming soft, mushy and dark in colour emitting an offensive smell - Take action after one plant is affected | - Harvest the healthy crop first and store in a cool, airy and disinfected store - Remove and destroy by ploughing-in all diseased debris - Uproot and carry away infected plants in a container to avoid spilling the affected soil. - Apply wood ash in the affected holes after uprooting plants | - When using pesticides, wear protective gear and follow the instructions on the product label and safely dispose empty containers - Always consult recent list of registered pesticides (PCPB) - Disinfect all farm tools by dipping in 10% jik solution (3.85% sodium hypochlorite) at 50ml per litre of water for at least 5 minutes - Spray with copper based fungicides e.g. Copper oxychloride (Amicop 50WP and Cobox 50WP at 60gms/20L of water) and Copper Hydroxide (Cuprocaffaro Micro 37.5 WG and Kocide DFat 50gms/20L of water) to minimize bacterial infection. | - WHO II (Moderately hazardous) PHI 7 days  |
