# Black spot Grey leaf spot Target spot

*Alternaria* spp. commonly *Alternaria brassicicola*

![img-72.jpeg](sections/images/40_p58_img-72.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Holmes, California Polytechnic State University at San Luis Obispo, Bugwood.org

- Seen in the seedbed as leaf spots and damping off.
- Small dark brown/black leaf spots, which enlarge and have a target ring pattern.
- Dusty black (spores) material may be seen on fingers after touching the spots.
