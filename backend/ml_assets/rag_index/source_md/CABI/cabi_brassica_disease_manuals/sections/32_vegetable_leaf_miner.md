# Vegetable leaf miner
*Liriomyza sativae*

![img-63.jpeg](sections/images/32_p50_img-63.jpeg)

Photo: Central Science Lab, Bugwood.org

- Tiny yellow and black fly.
- Middle section is shiny black.
- Not active fliers and will only take short jerky flights from one leaf to the next; often seen walking around on leaf surfaces.
