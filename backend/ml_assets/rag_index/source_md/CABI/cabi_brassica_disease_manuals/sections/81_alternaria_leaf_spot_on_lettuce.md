# Alternaria leaf spot on lettuce

*Alternaria brassicae* and *A. brassicicola*

|   | Prevention | Monitoring | Direct Control | Direct Control | Restrictions  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Lettuce leaf showing Alternaria symptoms (Sandra Jensen, Cornell University, Bugwood.org) | - This is a seed borne disease plant certified disease free seeds - Transplant only healthy seedlings. - Mulch plants with straw soon after transplanting to reduce spread of spores already in the soil through rain splash - Control weeds of all kinds including volunteer crops to reduce sources of inoculum - Practise crop rotation after two seasons of lettuce with crops such as sorghum, millet or cassava - Bury old plant debris shortly after harvest to reduce inoculum level available in the next season. - Seeds can be treated with a fungicides before sowing to prevent the disease (see details of fungicides in the yellow column) | - Monitor fields weekly looking for dark brown to black lesions with yellow halos on leaf surfaces - Look for wilting and leaf drop - Spots affect quality of leaves, therefore early control action is required when first observed - Apply direct control as soon as first symptoms are observed to reduce spread of the disease OR use a protectant fungicide as a preventative measure | - When the disease is at a low incidence, rogue out all infected plants as they are seen and destroy by burning - Collect fallen leaves regularly in the season and destroy by burning to reduce availability of spores | - When using a pesticide or botanical, always wear protective clothing and follow the instructions on the product label - Do not use chemicals with the same mode of action year after year as this can lead to resistance - Always consult the most recent list of registered pesticides of MOFA, Ghana - Apply Mancozeb (e.g. Agrithane 80WP, Benco 80WP, Damazeb 80WP, Dizcozeb 80WP) at a rate of 75g/15L of water. Multi-site broad spectrum fungicide, FRAC group: M3 - Apply Captan (Merpan 50WP) at 75-120g/15L of water. Multi-site broad spectrum fungicide, FRAC group: M 4 - Treat seeds with Mancozeb at 3-4g/kg of seed before sowing. FRAC code: M3 - Treat seeds with Folpet (Folpan 50 WP) at 3-4g/kg of seed. FRAC code: M4 | - WHO Class U - Unlikely to present acute hazard in normal use. Apply 3 times at 7-10 day intervals. REI-24hrs; PHI-14 days. Very toxic to aquatic organisms - do not apply near waterways - WHO Class U - Unlikely to present acute hazard in normal use. Apply 3 times in a season at 7-10 day intervals. REI-24hrs; PHI-14 days. Very toxic to aquatic organisms - do not apply near waterways - WHO Class U (Unlikely to present any hazard under normal use) - WHO Class U (Unlikely to present any hazard under normal use)  |
