# Edema

![img-84.jpeg](sections/images/52_p70_img-84.jpeg)

Photo: David B. Langston, University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Swellings, usually on the underside of leaves; some cultivars more susceptible than others.
- Non-pathogenic disorder that will not spread from one plant to another.
- It is caused by a combination of high humidity and high soil moisture content, might also be the result of some chemical sprays.
