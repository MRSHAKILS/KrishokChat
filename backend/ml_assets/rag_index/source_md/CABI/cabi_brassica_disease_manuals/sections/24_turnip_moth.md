# Turnip moth
*Agrotis segetum*

![img-55.jpeg](sections/images/24_p42_img-55.jpeg)

Photo: T. Morris, Flickr

- Shows various colour forms, although usually the forewings show a neat, pale fringe with a narrow inner dark line and the rear wings are white.
- Wingspan is 32–42 mm.
- Flies at night.
