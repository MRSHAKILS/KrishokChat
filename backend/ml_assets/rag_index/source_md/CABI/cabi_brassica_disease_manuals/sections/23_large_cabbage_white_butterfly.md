# Large cabbage white butterfly

*Pieris brassicae*

![img-54.jpeg](sections/images/23_p41_img-54.jpeg)

Photo: Hania Berdys, Bugwood.org

- Wingspan 55-70 mm, with upper sides usually very white, with a pronounced black tip to the forewing.
- The head, and body are black with grey hair-like scales.
