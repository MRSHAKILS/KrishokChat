# Onion thrips
## *Thrips tabaci*

![img-77.jpeg](sections/images/45_p63_img-77.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Holmes, California Polytechnic State University at San Luis Obispo, Bugwood.org

- Silvering of leaves which may become brown and hard (corky) as the plant responds to the damage (by this time the thrips may have moved on).
- Thrips are often associated with oily droplets of frass (excrement) deposited on the leaf surface (seen with a handlens).
