# Cabbage looper
*Trichoplusia ni*

![img-39.jpeg](sections/images/08_p26_img-39.jpeg)

Photo: Distant Hill Gardens, Flickr

- Initially larvae are greyish/white, later pale green and marked with distinct white stripe on each side.
- Larvae have three pairs of prolegs; crawl by arching back to form a loop and then projecting the body forward.
- Eggs yellowish, white or greenish, 0.6 mm diameter and 0.4 mm tall, laid singly or in small clusters.
