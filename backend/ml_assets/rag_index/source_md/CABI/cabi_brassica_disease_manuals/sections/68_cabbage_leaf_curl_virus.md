# Cabbage leaf curl virus
## CaLCuV

![img-100.jpeg](sections/images/68_p86_img-100.jpeg)

Photo: David B. Langston, University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Symptoms on leaves are yellow spots, vein clearing, mosaic, curling, and wrinkling.
- Transmitted by whitefly.
