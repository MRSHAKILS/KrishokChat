# Whiptail of crucifers
Molybdenum deficiency

![img-87.jpeg](sections/images/55_p73_img-87.jpeg)

Photo: P. Taylor, CABI

- Leaves develop a boat-like appearance if the plant is short of molybdenum.
- If very severe the leaf edges are so reduced that the mid rib is all that remains, with the leaf appearing as though it's been eaten.
