# Root rot damping off
*Rhizoctonia* spp. and others

![img-88.jpeg](sections/images/56_p74_img-88.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Holmes, California Polytechnic State University at San Luis Obispo, Bugwood.org

- May prevent emergence of the seedling.
- If they emerge the base of the young stem is partially or totally destroyed by brown or black rot.
- They generally will fall over due to the rot and die.
