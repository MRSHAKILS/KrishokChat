# Heart rot
Calcium deficiency

![img-94.jpeg](sections/images/62_p80_img-94.jpeg)

Photo: S. Nelson, Flickr

- “Decay” only seen in the central stem and does not spread to leaf stalks.
- Can be mild with a small cavity and browning or can be severe and appear similar to bacterial infection.
- No bad smell is associated with heart rot.
