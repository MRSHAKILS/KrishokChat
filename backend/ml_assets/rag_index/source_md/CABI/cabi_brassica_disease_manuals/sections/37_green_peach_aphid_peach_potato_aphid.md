# Green peach aphid (Peach potato aphid)
*Myzus persicae*

![img-69.jpeg](sections/images/37_p55_img-69.jpeg)

Photo: C. Quintin, Flickr

- Can be many colours from yellow green or pink but any one aphid is the same colour all over.
- Winged forms are usually black.
- Usually found on lower sides of leaves in sparse colonies.
- Transmits a lot of viruses.
