# Boron deficiency

![img-92.jpeg](sections/images/60_p78_img-92.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Holmes, California Polytechnic State University at San Luis Obispo, Bugwood.org

- Symptoms are generally seen in the young leaves first. They don't expand properly and snap easily.
- Stems are hollowed out.
- Occurs most frequently on coarse or sandy acid soils. Boron becomes less available during drought.
- Easily confused with Calcium deficiency.
