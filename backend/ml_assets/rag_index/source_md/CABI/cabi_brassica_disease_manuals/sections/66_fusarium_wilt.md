# Fusarium wilt

*Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. conglutinans*

![img-98.jpeg](sections/images/66_p84_img-98.jpeg)

Photo: Ontario MoA, Food and rural affairs

- Yellowing of the lower leaves, often initially on one side of the plant they will later turn brown and drop off.
- Brown discolouration inside the stem is characteristic.
- Eventually the entire plant may yellow, wilt, and collapse.
