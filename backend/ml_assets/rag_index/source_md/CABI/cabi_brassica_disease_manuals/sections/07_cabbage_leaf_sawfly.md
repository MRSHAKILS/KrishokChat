# Cabbage leaf sawfly
*Athalia proxima*

![img-38.jpeg](sections/images/07_p25_img-38.jpeg)

Photo: Merle Shepard, Gerald R.Carner, and P.A.C Ooi, Insects and their Natural Enemies Associated with Vegetables and Soybean in Southeast Asia, Bugwood.org

- Milk white eggs 0.6-0.9 mm high.
- The lava is NOT a true caterpillar (there are more than 4 false feet on the body of the larvae).
- Early instars are grey/dark grey, as they age the head turns black.
- Tough silken yellow cocoon.
