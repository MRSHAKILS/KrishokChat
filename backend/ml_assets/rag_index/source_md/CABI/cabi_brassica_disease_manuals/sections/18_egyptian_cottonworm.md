# Egyptian cottonworm
*Spodoptera littoralis*

![img-49.jpeg](sections/images/18_p36_img-49.jpeg)

Photo: O. Heikinheimo, Bugwood.org

- Grey brown body, wingspan 30-38 mm.
- Forewings grey to reddish brown.
- Rear wings greyish white with grey edges.
