# Cabbage whitefly
*Aleyrodes proletella*

![img-64.jpeg](sections/images/33_p51_img-64.jpeg)

Photo: Rasbak, Wikimedia.org

- Adults 1.5 mm long; white wings with four grey spots – wings white due to a powdery wax. Head dark with yellow underside; red eyes.
- Often seen on leaf underside, flying only when disturbed.
- Nymphs are yellow and scale-like.
- Can transmit viruses.
