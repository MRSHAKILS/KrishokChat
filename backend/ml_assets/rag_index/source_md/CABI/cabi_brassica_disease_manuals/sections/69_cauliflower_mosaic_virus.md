# Cauliflower mosaic virus
## Ca MV

![img-101.jpeg](sections/images/69_p87_img-101.jpeg)

Photos: Horticultural Research International

- Vein clearing and mild leaf distortion.
- Transmitted by aphids.
- If the weather is warm the symptoms are not seen.
