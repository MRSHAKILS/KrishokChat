# Cabbage looper
*Trichoplusia ni*

![img-58.jpeg](sections/images/27_p45_img-58.jpeg)

Photo: Joseph Berger, Bugwood.org

- Forewings are mottled grey-brown in colour with silvery white spots centrally, with a U-shaped mark and a circle or dot that are often connected (which is characteristic of this pest).
- Rear wings light brown at the base with ends dark brown. Wingspan 33-38 mm.
