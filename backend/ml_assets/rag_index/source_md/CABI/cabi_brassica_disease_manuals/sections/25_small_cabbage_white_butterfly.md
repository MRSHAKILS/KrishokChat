# Small cabbage white butterfly

*Pieris rapae*

![img-56.jpeg](sections/images/25_p43_img-56.jpeg)

Photo: Forrest and Kim Starr, Starr Environmental, Bugwood.org

- Wingspan 32–47 mm; upper side is creamy white with black tips on the forewings. Females also have two black spots in the centre of the forewings. Underwings are yellowish with black speckles.
- Looks like a smaller version of the Large cabbage white butterfly (*Pieris brassicae*).
