# Cotton leafworm, Tobacco cutworm
*Spodoptera litura*

![img-51.jpeg](sections/images/20_p38_img-51.jpeg)

Photo: D. Hobern, Flickr

- Adult moths 15-20 mm, with wingspan of 30-38 mm.
- Forewings grey to reddish-brown, with a complex pattern of creamy streaks and paler lines along the veins. Rear wings are greyish-white with greyish-brown margins.
