# Black Rot

# *Xanthomonas campestris*

![img-74.jpeg](sections/images/42_p60_img-74.jpeg)

Photos: J. Mulema, CABI

- Very common disease.
- Characteristic V-shaped lesions spread in from the edge of older leaves or from insect damage.
- The spreading edge of the lesion is yellow whereas the older areas are brown.
