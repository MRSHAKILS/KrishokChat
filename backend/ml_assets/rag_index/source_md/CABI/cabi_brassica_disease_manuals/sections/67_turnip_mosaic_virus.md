# Turnip mosaic virus
## TuMV

![img-99.jpeg](sections/images/67_p85_img-99.jpeg)

Photo: Horticultural Research International

- Symptoms vary with host and environment.
- Mosaic and or browning is common. Tips of leaves bending out and down is common.
- Necrotic spotting (black necrotic spots and ringspots) may develop in field or in store.
- Aphid transmitted but with limited direct plant to plant spread.
