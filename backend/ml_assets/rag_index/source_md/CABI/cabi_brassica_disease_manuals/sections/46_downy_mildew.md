# Downy Mildew
*Hyaloperonospora brassicae*

![img-78.jpeg](sections/images/46_p64_img-78.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Holmes, California Polytechnic State University at San Luis Obispo, Bugwood.org

- Usually a leaf disease but can attack seedling leaves, stems of seedlings and the white region of cauliflower.
- Can be vein limited in some cases.
- In cool weather there may be little yellowing (initially) but with time or in hot dry weather the plant material will become yellow and then brown.
