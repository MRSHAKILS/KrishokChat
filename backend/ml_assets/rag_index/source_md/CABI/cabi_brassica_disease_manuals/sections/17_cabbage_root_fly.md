# Cabbage root fly

*Delia radicum*

![img-48.jpeg](sections/images/17_p35_img-48.jpeg)

Photos: Rasbak, Wikimedia.org

- Larvae (up to 6 mm) white and pointed at one end; burrow into roots (main picture).
- Flies look like small house flies, 6 mm long (insert left).
- The fly lays white, elongated eggs at the base of plant.
- Pupae are brown and are also found in the soil (insert right).
