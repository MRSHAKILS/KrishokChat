# White rust
*Albugo candida*

![img-83.jpeg](sections/images/51_p69_img-83.jpeg)

Photo: P. Taylor, CABI

- Various amounts of browning can occur; green islands can remain after the leaf has yellowed.
- Often associated with blistering and distortion of the leaves or shoot.
- Can be seen on all parts of the plant.
