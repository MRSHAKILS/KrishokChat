# Cabbage cluster caterpillar

# *Crocidolomia pavonana*

![img-53.jpeg](sections/images/22_p40_img-53.jpeg)

Photo: Goldentakin, Flickr

- Wingspan about 30 mm; black thorax and a reddish brown body.
- They emerge at night.
- Larvae initially feed on the underside of the leaves but then eat the whole leaves and finally the stem.
