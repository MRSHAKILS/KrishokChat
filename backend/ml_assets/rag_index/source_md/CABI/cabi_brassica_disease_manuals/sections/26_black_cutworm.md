# Black cutworm

*Agrotis ipsilon*

![img-57.jpeg](sections/images/26_p44_img-57.jpeg)

Photo: Whitney Cranshaw, Colorado State University, Bugwood.org

- Forewings have small, black dagger-like markings extending towards wing tip and a whitish band extending across the wing; wingspan 40-55 mm.
- Eggs about 0.5 mm in diameter, ball shaped, with a slightly flattened base; laid in clusters on leaves.
