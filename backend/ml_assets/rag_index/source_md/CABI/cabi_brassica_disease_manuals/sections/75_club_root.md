# Club root

# *Plasmodiophora brassicae*

![img-107.jpeg](sections/images/75_p93_img-107.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Holmes, California Polytechnic State University at San Luis Obispo, Bugwood.org

- Above ground the plants are stunted and wilt readily, often have a blue tinge.
- Below ground large bulbous swellings on the roots are extremely characteristic.
- Initially these are white but they darken with age and may start to decay.
