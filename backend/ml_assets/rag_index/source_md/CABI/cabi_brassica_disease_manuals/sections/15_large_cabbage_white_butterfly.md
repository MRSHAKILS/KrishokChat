# Large cabbage white butterfly

*Pieris brassicae*

![img-46.jpeg](sections/images/15_p33_img-46.jpeg)

Photos: P. Taylor, CABI

- Larvae up to 30 mm long; yellow and black with obvious hairs.
- Often initially on the underside of leaves or hidden among young leaves.
- They produce large amounts of black frass (excrement).
