# White leaf spot

*Mycosphaerella brassicicola* / *Pseudocercosporella capsellae*

![img-81.jpeg](sections/images/49_p67_img-81.jpeg)

Photos: David B. Langston, University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Appears as tan or light brown spots, they may join together and may drop out.
- Can appear similar to Paraquat damage.
- Stem lesions are elongated and brown at first, turning ashy grey to white with a brownish margin.
