# Mustard aphid
*Lipaphis erysimi*

![img-70.jpeg](sections/images/38_p56_img-70.jpeg)

Photo: A. N. Sparks Jr University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Olive-green to brown, with ridges on the back.
- Found on leaf underside, young shoots, growing points, and when severe on both sides of the leaf.
- Causes leaf rolling, yellowing and stunting.
- Severe infestations prevent head formation and flowering.
