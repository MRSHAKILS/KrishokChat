# Turnip moth
*Agrotis segetum*

![img-37.jpeg](sections/images/06_p24_img-37.jpeg)

Photo: F. L. Martin GNU licence

- The larvae are generally grey sometimes looking a little purple.
- Attacks the lower stems often results in cutting off of seedlings.
- Often found curled up near the base of plants.
