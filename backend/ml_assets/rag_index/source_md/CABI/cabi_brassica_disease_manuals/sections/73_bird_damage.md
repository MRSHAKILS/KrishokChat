# Bird damage
(Pigeons)

![img-105.jpeg](sections/images/73_p91_img-105.jpeg)

Photo: P. Taylor, CABI

- May appear similar to locust or caterpillar damage.
- Leaves are torn rather than nibbled.
- No frass is seen.
