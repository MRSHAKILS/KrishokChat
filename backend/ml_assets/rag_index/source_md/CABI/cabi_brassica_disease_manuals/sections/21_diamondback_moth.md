# Diamondback moth

*Plutella xylostella*

![img-52.jpeg](sections/images/21_p39_img-52.jpeg)

Photo: IITA, Flickr

- Adult small, slim, greyish-brown moth with pronounced feelers (antennae).
- Marked with a broad cream or light brown band along the back.
- When viewed from the side, the tips of the wings can be seen to turn upward slightly.
