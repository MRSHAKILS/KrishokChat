# Chinese kale - Downy mildew

*Peronospora brassicae*

|   | Prevention | Monitoring | Direct Control | Direct Control | Restrictions  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  Symptom of Downy Mildew (www.bugguide.net) | - Ensure proper land preparation to make sure that your soil is well drained - Transplant only healthy seedlings - Provide adequate plant spacing to reduce the density of the canopy and improve aeration. - Thin plants and prune infected shoots also helps improve plant's aeration and reduce disease inoculum. - Plough-under all the plant debris after harvest - Practice crop rotation with non-brassica crops - Alter planting dates to avoid rainy periods so as to avoid high disease pressure period | - Inspect the crop regularly - Scouting should begin when the plants are young until harvest time | - Remove infected plants and prune infected leaves/shoots - Properly dispose of collected diseased-parts either by burning or burying them - Spray garlic bulb extract | - Note: for all pesticides usage to follow instructions on product label; Note: to avoid the development of resistance to pesticides, pesticides in the same FRAC Group should not be used continuously - Carbendazim; 2-3g/lit; Contact action - Difenoconazole (e.g. Score); Use 5ml/10lit water; Systemic action - Mancozeb Use 2-3g/1lit water; Contact action | - FRAC Group 1; WHO Class U (Unlikely to present acute hazard); Follow PHI on label - Triazoles FRAC Group 3; WHO Class II (Moderately hazardous) - FRAC Group M3; WHO Class U (Unlikely to; present acute hazard)  |
