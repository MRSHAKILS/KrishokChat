# Cabbage webworm
*Hellula rogatalis*

![img-41.jpeg](sections/images/10_p28_img-41.jpeg)

Photo: A. N. Sparks Jr. University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Older larvae have 5 dark stripes from head to tail and yellowish or brownish hairs.
- Larvae produce a lot of silk, for protection.
- Eggs laid singly or in small masses on the youngest leaves, oval and flattened in shape, greeny grey.
