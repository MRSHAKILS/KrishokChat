# Ring spot

*Mycosphaerella brassicicola*

![img-79.jpeg](sections/images/47_p65_img-79.jpeg)

Photo: M. Gammelgaard, platesygdomme.dk

- Well defined spots 10-20 mm, usually tan coloured and covered in black dots (fruiting bodies).
- The border of the spots is grey/olive colour and this may remain after the rest of the leaf has yellowed.
