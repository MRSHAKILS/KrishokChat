# Mango Powdery Mildew

Recognize the problem

Mango powdery mildew is a disease that attacks flowers and leaves causing a white powdery appearance (see top and bottom pictures).

Background

- The disease is caused by a fungus.
- The fungus grows on the plant and produces spores that appear as white powder on the surface.
- It is spread through the air and is more serious in dry but humid weather conditions.
- It causes leaves and flowers to drop hence no or very little fruit formation. Young leaves are usually more diseased than old ones.
- The disease can be controlled using cultural methods and chemicals.

Management

- Cut the mango tree branches to keep them short for easy management and air flow.
- Remove diseased leaves and flowers and bury or burn them to reduce the source of the disease.
- Spray the trees with plant penetrating chemicals such as those ending in "azole" or "strobin", changing them regularly with surface chemicals such as those containing sulphur.
- Spray on weekly intervals, following the instructions on the label.
- Start spraying just before flowering and continue throughout the flowering period.
