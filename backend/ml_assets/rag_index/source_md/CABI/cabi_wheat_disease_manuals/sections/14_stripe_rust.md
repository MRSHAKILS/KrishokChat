# Stripe Rust

## Recognize the problem

Stripe rust disease is a serious fungal disease of cereals, especially wheat, which causes yield loss. Even if the number of kernels from an infected plant is high, yield will still be lower as the kernels will be smaller than those from a healthy plant.

This disease has 5 life stages. The main symptom of this disease is the development of slim lines beside each other on the blades of the leaves. The length of the lines is not always the same but the colour of the lines is always yellow to red, which is why the disease is called stripe rust.

## Background

The main host for this disease is the Barberry bush. The disease is carried from one plant to another by wind, rain and humans.

This disease is favoured by temperatures of 10-20°C. If the conditions get hotter than this then the growth of the disease will stop. The disease will attack when the wheat is flowering and when the wheat is mature, the yellow lines turn black.

## Management

- Remove barberry plants from the field
- Remove and destroy unwanted weeds
- Avoid from more water or less irrigation
- Cultivate the wheat in lines
- Cultivate improved seeds like wheat Gul, Solh, Pamir, Lalmi-2, Lalmi-3, Darulaman-07, Moqawom09, Chont10, etc.
- Less wheat seed cultivation
- Crop rotation with other plants like beans and vegetables.
- Chemical usage should be used as a last option. Apply sulphur powder in the first stage of the disease.
