# *Pythium* seedling and root rot
*Pythium* spp.

Worldwide

![img-225.jpeg](sections/images/90_p204_img-225.jpeg)

Photo: A. E. Robertson, Iowa State University

- Common under prolonged waterlogged conditions.
- Seeds fail to emerge.
- Discoloured leaves develop.
- Roots rot and develop dark reddish spots, other diseases will often take over once the plant is affected.
