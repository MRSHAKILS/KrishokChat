# Maize dwarf mosaic virus

MDMV

Worldwide

![img-200.jpeg](sections/images/65_p179_img-200.jpeg)

Photo: International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center

- Symptoms depend on many factors, environment, cultivar, strain of virus etc.
- Symptoms can include “mosaic” “red leaf” “tan stripe” and “red stripe”.
- Mottle may develop on young leaves, chlorotic dots enlarge to form stripes.
- In cool climates, red streaks turn brown.
- Stunting is a common symptom.
