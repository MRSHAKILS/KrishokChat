# Stunt nematode

Tylenchorhynchus spp.

Worldwide

![img-241.jpeg](sections/images/106_p220_img-241.jpeg)

Photo: Dept of Plant Path, NCSU, Bugwood.org

- Root feeder. Few stubby roots, swollen brown tips.
- Plants, small, yellow, less vigorous.
- Looks like drought and nutrient deficiencies.
- Makes other root diseases such as charcoal rot more likely to occur.
