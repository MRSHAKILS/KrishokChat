# Charcoal root and stalk rot

*Macrophomina phaseolina*

Worldwide

![img-224.jpeg](sections/images/89_p203_img-224.jpeg)

Photo: University of Nebraska, Lincoln Extension

- Stalks are soft, the plant material inside the stem is missing, only the fibers remain, small dots of fungi giving a charred “burnt” appearance.
- Plants are likely to fall over.
