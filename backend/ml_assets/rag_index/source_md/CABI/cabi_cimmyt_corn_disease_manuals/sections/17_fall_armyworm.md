# Fall armyworm
*Spodoptera frugiperda*

America and Africa

![img-150.jpeg](sections/images/17_p131_img-150.jpeg)

Photos: Frank Peairs, Colorado State University, Bugwood.org; M. Cock, CABI

- Young larvae are greenish with a black head. Later the body becomes brownish, white lines develop down the sides.
- Raised spots are on the back; they are usually dark, and bear spines.
- The face of the mature larva is marked with a white upsided down “Y”.
- Adult moth: Triangular white spots at the tip and centre of the wings, rear wing is shiny silver-white with a narrow dark border round the edge.
