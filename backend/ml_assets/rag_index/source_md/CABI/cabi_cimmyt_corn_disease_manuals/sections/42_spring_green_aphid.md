# Spring green aphid

*Schizaphis graminum*

Worldwide

![img-177.jpeg](sections/images/42_p156_img-177.jpeg)

Photo: Frank Peairs, Colorado State University, Bugwood.org

- Feed on underside of leaf. Female 1.5-2.0 mm and typically pale green often with a stipe down the back.
- Fully grown they have the classic pear shaped body of aphids but young ones are smaller and with parallel sides.
- Winged forms develop when population are high or nearing the end of the season.
