# Oriental armyworm

*Mythimna separate*

Asia and SE Asia

![img-144.jpeg](sections/images/13_p127_img-144.jpeg)

Photos: Takahashi, Wikimedia commons; Shipher Wu, Flickr

- Young larvae are white, they turn green when older, head orange or brown.
- Mature larvae are variable but are greenish to light brown marble orange brown with narrow white line along the back.
- Mature larvae bite off the grain stalks and let them fall.
- Adults about 20 mm long wingspan 40 mm forewings have two pale round spots.
