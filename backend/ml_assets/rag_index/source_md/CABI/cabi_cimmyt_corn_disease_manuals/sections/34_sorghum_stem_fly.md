# Sorghum stem fly

*Atherigona soccata*

Africa, Asia, SE Asia

![img-169.jpeg](sections/images/34_p148_img-169.jpeg)

Photo: G. Goergen, Ecoport.org

- White eggs are long and thin, 0.8 x 0.2 mm 1-3 eggs laid per leaf.
- Larvae (maggots) crawl down into funnel and eat the youngest leaves of seedlings: Deadheart develops maggot up to 10 mm.
- Leaf easily removed and gives off bad smell.
- Fly (4 mm) looks like a small house fly.
- Older plants are more resistant.
