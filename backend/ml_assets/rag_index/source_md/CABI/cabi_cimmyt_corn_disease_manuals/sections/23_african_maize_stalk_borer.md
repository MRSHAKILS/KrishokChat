# African maize stalk borer

*Busseola fusca*

Africa

![img-158.jpeg](sections/images/23_p137_img-158.jpeg)

Photos: D. Cugala ICIPE

- Eggs (white but turn darker with age) laid under leaf in a long column stretching up the stem.
- Young larvae are deep purple or black.
- Larvae featureless, up to 40 mm. The body is creamy-white with grey or pink hint, head dark brown.
- Adult wing-span 25-35 mm. The colour is variabe depending on place and time of year.
