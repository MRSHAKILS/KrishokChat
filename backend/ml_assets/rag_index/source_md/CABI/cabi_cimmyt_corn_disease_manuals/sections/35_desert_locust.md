# Desert locust
*Scistocerca gregaria*

Africa and Asia

![img-170.jpeg](sections/images/35_p149_img-170.jpeg)

Photo: H Hubert, Flickr

- Solitary desert locusts colour is greyish or brownish, but males may become yellow when they mature. Males are 40-50 mm, females are 50-60 mm long.
- When they cluster together they turn black and develop orange spots.
