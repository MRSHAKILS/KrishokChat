# Cotton bollworm

*Helicoverpa armigera*

Worldwide except N America

![img-147.jpeg](sections/images/15_p129_img-147.jpeg)

![img-148.jpeg](sections/images/15_p129_img-148.jpeg)

Photos: Paolo Mazzei, Bugwood.org; W. Billen, Pflanzenbeschaustelle, Weil am Rhein, Bugwood.org

- Up to 40mm long the head is yellow with several spots. Three dark stripes extend along the back and a yellow one along the side.
- Underside is pale.
- Larvae often eat each other; the older larvae are less common.
- When disturbed they drop from the plant and curl up.
