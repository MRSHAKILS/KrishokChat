# Crazy top

*Sclerophthora macrospora*

Worldwide

![img-205.jpeg](sections/images/70_p184_img-205.jpeg)

Photo: Paul Bachi, University of Kentucky Research and Education Center, Bugwood.org

- Characteristic 'crazy top'.
- Early infection: leaf mosaic Severe: lower leaves, strap-like, leathery, roughened underside, with translucent chlorotic streaks and twisted.
- Stunted roots, plant pulls up easily.
- Related to Downy mildew.
