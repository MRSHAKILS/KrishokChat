# Shortage of Sulphur
## Sulphur deficiency

Worldwide

![img-198.jpeg](sections/images/63_p177_img-198.jpeg)

Photo: Dr Prakash Kumar and Dr Manoj Kumar

- General yellowing of the plant similar to nitrogen shortage but in the case of sulphur shortage the tip of the plant also turns pale yellow whereas when nitrogen (N) is short it will remain green.
