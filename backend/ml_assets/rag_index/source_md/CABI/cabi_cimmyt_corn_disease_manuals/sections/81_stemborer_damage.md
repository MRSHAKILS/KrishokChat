# Stemborer damage

Many species

Worldwide

![img-216.jpeg](sections/images/81_p195_img-216.jpeg)

Photo: A Nallusamy and C Karthikeyan, Tamil Nadu Ag University

- Exit holes in the stem often surrounded by frass.
- Almost always associated with holes in leaves.
- Cut open the stem to find the eaten cavity inside, and there may be other larvae within.
- It is necessary to see the larvae to identify the pest.
- Stems may break due to weakness caused by boreholes.
