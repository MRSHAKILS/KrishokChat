# Anthracnose foliar blight

*Colletotrichum sublineolum*

Worldwide

![img-181.jpeg](sections/images/46_p160_img-181.jpeg)

Photo: Clemson University – USDA Cooperative Extension Slide Series, Bugwood.org

- Circular dark spots with red edges and a lighter middle.
- Older spots get a brown centre, (2–20 mm).
- Black fruiting bodies within the spots are an important thing to look out for.
- Whole leaves can be killed and plant may die before harvest.
