# Witchweed

*Striga asiatica*

Africa and Asia

![img-242.jpeg](sections/images/107_p221_img-242.jpeg)

Photo: P. Taylor, CABI

- Parasite or hemi-parasitic reddish flowered plant causes scorching early drying and often the plant will fall.
- Plants will show poor vigour and fail to yield.
