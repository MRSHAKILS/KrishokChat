# Wireworms, and false wireworm

*Agriotes* spp. *Gonocephalum* spp. and many others

Worldwide

![img-154.jpeg](sections/images/20_p134_img-154.jpeg)

Photo: Kansas State University

- Larvae are long and thin, small yellow brown, shiny hard-bodied and live in the soil.
- Can be move quickly with three pairs of well-developed legs near the head.
- Cannot curl into a tight C-shape as cutworms and chafer grubs do.
