# Corn planthopper

*Peregrinus maidis*

Worldwide

![img-166.jpeg](sections/images/31_p145_img-166.jpeg)

Photo: ARS Corn And Soybean Research Unit – ARS-USDA

- The nymphs have no wings and are green to light brown about 3 mm long.
- They run rapidly when disturbed. Both adults and nymphs like to feed inside the funnel of the plant, particularly in young plants. As the plants grow, they move out onto the underside of the leaves.
