# Lesser grain weevil

*Sitophilus oryzae*

Worldwide

![img-172.jpeg](sections/images/37_p151_img-172.jpeg)

Photo: Udo Schmidt, Flickr

- 2 mm long with long snout, brown/black with four orange spots on wing cases.
- Usually a pest of stored products but can attack plants in the field.
- Female chews hole in stored grain and lays an egg the larvae develop inside the stored seed.
