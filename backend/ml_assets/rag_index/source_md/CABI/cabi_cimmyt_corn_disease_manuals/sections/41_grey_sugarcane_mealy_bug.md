# Grey sugarcane mealy bug

*Saccharicoccus sacchari*

Worldwide

![img-176.jpeg](sections/images/41_p155_img-176.jpeg)

Photo: CABI

- Pink, of typical mealybug appearance, often covered with white wax up to 7 mm long.
- Egg shaped, fleshy and with a slightly segmented body no obvious head or bottom end.
- For most of their life cycle they live in the highly protected environment behind the leaf sheath.
- May attack underground as well above ground.
