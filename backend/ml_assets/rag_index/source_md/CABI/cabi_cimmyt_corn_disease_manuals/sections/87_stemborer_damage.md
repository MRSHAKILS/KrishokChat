# Stemborer damage

Many species

Worldwide

![img-222.jpeg](sections/images/87_p201_img-222.jpeg)

Photo: P. Kelly, CABI

- Plants likely to fall over due to weakness in the stem.
- Plants likely to be suffering from drought stress and will mature early if they mature at all.
- Stem borer larvae are easily found once the stem is split open.
