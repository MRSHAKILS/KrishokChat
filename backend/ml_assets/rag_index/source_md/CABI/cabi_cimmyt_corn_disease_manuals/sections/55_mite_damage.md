# Mite damage

Mite damage (on maize)

Worldwide

![img-190.jpeg](sections/images/55_p169_img-190.jpeg)

Photo: S. Zukoff, Kansas State University, Department of Entomology

- Silvering of the leaf associated with webbing.
- Death of the leaf soon follows but mites are not be seen on the dead leaves.
- Mites are commonly found on the back of the leaf near the midrib and near the leaf sheath.
