# Spring green aphid
*Schizaphis graminum*

Worldwide

![img-232.jpeg](sections/images/97_p211_img-232.jpeg)

Photo: P Sloderbeck and L Brooks, Texas A&M University

- Reddish yellow spots develop 2-4 days after feeding.
- These spots join together and can discolour the whole leaf which will die is feeding was severe.
- Seedlings may be killed and older plants may fall over in severe cases.
