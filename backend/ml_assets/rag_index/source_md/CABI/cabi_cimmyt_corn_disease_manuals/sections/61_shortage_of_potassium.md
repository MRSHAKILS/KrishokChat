# Shortage of Potassium
## Potassium deficiency

![img-196.jpeg](sections/images/61_p175_img-196.jpeg)

Photo: Dr Prakash Kumar

- The edges of the lower leaves are the first to show symptoms.
- Symptoms first seen in the lower leaves, tip of the plant remains green.
- Unlike nitrogen (N) shortage where the area closest to the centre of the leaf turns yellow in the case of potassium (K) shortage the edges of the leaf turn yellow and then brown.
