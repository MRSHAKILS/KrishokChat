# African sugarcane borer

*Edlana saccharina*

Africa

![img-155.jpeg](sections/images/21_p135_img-155.jpeg)

![img-156.jpeg](sections/images/21_p135_img-156.jpeg)

Photos: David Agassiz (top); ICIPE (lower)

- Oval yellow eggs. Larvae light-brown to dark-grey with small dark spots. Head reddish to dark brown.
- Often a great deal of frass hanging from exit holes.
- Moths vary considerably but have long forewings with two distinct dark spots at the top. When settled the sides of the wings are parallel to each other, 35 mm wingspan.
