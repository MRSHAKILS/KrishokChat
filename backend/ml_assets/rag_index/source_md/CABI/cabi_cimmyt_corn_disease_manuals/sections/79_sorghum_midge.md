# Sorghum midge
*Stenodiplosis sorghicola*

Worldwide

![img-214.jpeg](sections/images/79_p193_img-214.jpeg)

Photo: G Cronholm, Texas A&M University

- Maggots feeding on milky stage grains will make empty chaffy spikelets as shrivelled grains fail to develop.
- Empty pupae cases (ringed) remain at top of spikelet once the damage has been done and the adult has flown.
