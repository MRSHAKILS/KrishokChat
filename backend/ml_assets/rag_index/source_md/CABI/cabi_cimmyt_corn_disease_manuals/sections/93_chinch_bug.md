# Chinch bug
*Blissus leucopterus*

N and S America

![img-228.jpeg](sections/images/93_p207_img-228.jpeg)

Photo: Gerald Wilde, CABI CPC

- Mostly attacks behind leaf sheaths but occasionally from roots and leaves.
- Severely damaged plants may die or be badly stunted (failure of leaf sheaths and stems to properly extend).
- Reddish feeding marks behind leaf sheaths and a reddish or yellowish streaking on the leaves.
