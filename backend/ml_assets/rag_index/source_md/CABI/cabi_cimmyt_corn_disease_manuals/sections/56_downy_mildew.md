# Downy mildew

*Peronosclerospora sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-191.jpeg](sections/images/56_p170_img-191.jpeg)

Photo: Thomas Isakeit, Texas A&M University

- White downy growth on underside of leaves. Later, narrow yellow striping which turn brown.
- White areas dry, brown, die appear shredded.
- Plants become stunted, fail to produce grain.
