# Corn planthopper

*Peregrinus maidis*

![img-193.jpeg](sections/images/58_p172_img-193.jpeg)

Photo: ARS Corn And Soybean Research Unit

- Small (5 mm) and look little bit like aphids.
- Waxy nymphs and adults often seen in plant funnel, on leaf sheaths and underneath leaves.
- Large numbers (looking like aphid colonies) will stunt the plant, leaves become twisted, brown and the ear will fail to emerge.
- Transmits Maize mosaic virus.
