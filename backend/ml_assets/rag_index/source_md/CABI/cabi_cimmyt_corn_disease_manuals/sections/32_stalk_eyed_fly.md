# Stalk eyed fly

*Diasemopsis* spp.

Africa

![img-167.jpeg](sections/images/32_p146_img-167.jpeg)

Photo: Ton Rulkens Flickr

- The maggots feed inside the stems or tillers of seedlings results in “dead hearts”.
- Adults small, 6 mm and come out in the evenings.
- Several different types all with eyes on stalks.
- Damage can be mistaken for the Sorghum shoot fly.
