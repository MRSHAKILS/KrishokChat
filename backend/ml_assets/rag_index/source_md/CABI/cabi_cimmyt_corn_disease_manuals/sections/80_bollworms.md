# Bollworms
*Helicoverpa* spp.

Worldwide

![img-215.jpeg](sections/images/80_p194_img-215.jpeg)

Photo: K O Bell, Texas A & M University

- In addition to the damage to leaves the caterpillars attack the developing grains.
- Often a lot of frass is seen.
- These large caterpillars are never hidden within the stems or seeds.
