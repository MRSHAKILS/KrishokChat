# Ergot, sugary disease
*Claviceps africana*

Worldwide

![img-213.jpeg](sections/images/78_p192_img-213.jpeg)

Photo: G Odvody, Texas A&M University

- Clear sticky honeydew, oozes from grain and drips on leaves grain heads often compacted.
- May become blackened with sooty mould or may develop a whitish bloom on the honey dew surface.
- Black fungal survival structures produced later in place of the seed.
