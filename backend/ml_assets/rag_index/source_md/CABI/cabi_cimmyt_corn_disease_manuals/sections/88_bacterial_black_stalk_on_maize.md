# Bacterial black stalk (on maize)

*Dickeya zeae*

Worldwide

![img-223.jpeg](sections/images/88_p202_img-223.jpeg)

Photo: Howard F. Schwartz, Colorado State University, Bugwood.org

- Lower regions of stem turn brown and become water soaked soft and slimy.
- There may be a foul smell and plants frequently fall over.
- Upper leaves wilt and soft rot may occur at the base of the funnel and this may spread down rapidly. Outer leaves generally unaffected.
- Often seen in poorly drained soils.
