# Mite damage

## Mite damage

Worldwide

![img-233.jpeg](sections/images/98_p212_img-233.jpeg)

Photo: Kansas State University

- Extensive mite damage leads to wholesale death of leaves which gain a scorched appearance.
- Mites cannot be found on the dead leaves they are present hidden on the young leaves.
- Detailed examination of the youngest leaves is required.
