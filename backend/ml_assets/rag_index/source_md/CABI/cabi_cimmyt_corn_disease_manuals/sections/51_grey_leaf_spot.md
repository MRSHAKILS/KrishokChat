# Grey leaf spot
*Cercospora sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-186.jpeg](sections/images/51_p165_img-186.jpeg)

Photo: P. Taylor, CABI

- Rectangular narrow tan/brown spots appear on mature leaves (12-50 mm) x (3-6 mm) turn grey with time.
- Individual lesions may coalesce, and under severe disease conditions, blight entire leaves.
