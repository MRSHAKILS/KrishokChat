# Grain mould

Various fungi

Worldwide

![img-206.jpeg](sections/images/71_p185_img-206.jpeg)

Photo: Tom Allen, Mississippi State University Extension

- Variable symptoms due to the nature of the problem.
- Severe infection results in grains with pink, white, or black fungal mycelium and sporulating structures.
- May lead to toxin production making the sorghum dangerous to eat and storage problems.
- Compact ear heads are highly susceptible in wet weather.
