# Mites

## Tetranychus urticae (and others)

Worldwide

![img-175.jpeg](sections/images/40_p154_img-175.jpeg)

Photo: F.C. Schweissing, Bugwood.org

- Mites are extremely small and difficult to see (0.3 mm x 0.4 mm).
- There are many types but all create similar damage and characteristic webbing.
- Do not look for mites without a hand lens and do not look for mites on the dead areas of the plant.
