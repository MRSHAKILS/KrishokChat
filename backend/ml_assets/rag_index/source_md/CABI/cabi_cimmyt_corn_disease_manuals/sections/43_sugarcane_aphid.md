# Sugarcane aphid

*Melanaphis* spp.

Worldwide

![img-178.jpeg](sections/images/43_p157_img-178.jpeg)

Photo: D Sekula Flickr

- Live on the lower surface of leaves.
- 1.8 mm long pale yellow or orange with black tail pipes and black feet
- Later in the season the aphids will leave the leaves move up onto the filling grain.
- Winged forms develop when population high.
- Sooty mould is extremely common with this pest.
