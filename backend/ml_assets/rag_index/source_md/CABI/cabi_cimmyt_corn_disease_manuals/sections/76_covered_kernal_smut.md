# Covered kernal smut

*Sporisorium sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-211.jpeg](sections/images/76_p190_img-211.jpeg)

Photo: University of Georgia Plant Pathology, University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Infected grains turn white, uninfected ones remain brown.
- In this case seed head is only partially infected but usually all the seeds in the head are affected.
