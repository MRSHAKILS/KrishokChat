# African pink stem borer

*Sesamia calamistis*

Africa

![img-161.jpeg](sections/images/26_p140_img-161.jpeg)

Photos: IRD Lab Evolution, Génomes et Spéciation; CIMMYT

- Eggs creamy white but darken with age, 1 mm wide.
- Larvae lack hairs and look creamy-white smooth and shiny. Often a distinctive pink colour under the skin.
- Hooks on the ends of short stubby legs are arranged in rows (hand lens needed).
- Unlikely to cause holing as it doesn't feed on the top leaves.
