# Beet armyworm
*Spodoptera exigua*

Worldwide except S America

![img-142.jpeg](sections/images/11_p125_img-142.jpeg)

Photos: M. Young, Rural Agricultural Development Authority, Jamaica; P. Harris, Bugwood.org

- The larvae are pale green or yellow when small but gain pale stripes later.
- The body is practically without hairs or spines.
- Older larvae are generally darker but variable in colour, pink or yellow on the underside.
- The adults are moderately sized mottled grey brown moths, the wing span of 25-30 mm.
