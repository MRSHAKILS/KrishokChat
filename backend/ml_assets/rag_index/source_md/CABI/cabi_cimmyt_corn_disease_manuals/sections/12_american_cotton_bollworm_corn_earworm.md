# American cotton bollworm/Corn earworm

*Helicoverpa zea*

Americas and China

![img-143.jpeg](sections/images/12_p126_img-143.jpeg)

Photos: Clemson University – USDA Cooperative Extension Slide Series, Bugwood.org; Seabrooke Leckie, Flickr

- Usually with orange heads and a black region just behind head.
- Bodies usually blackish but can be brown green/yellow covered with many thorny micro spines.
- Young larvae feed together but older ones eat each other; larger larvae are not so common.
- Adults variable colour (yellowish brown) 32-45 mm wingspan.
