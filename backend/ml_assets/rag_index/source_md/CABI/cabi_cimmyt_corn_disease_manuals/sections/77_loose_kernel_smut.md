# Loose kernel smut

*Sphacelotheca cruenta*

Worldwide

![img-212.jpeg](sections/images/77_p191_img-212.jpeg)

Photo: P Sajjabbi, www.runetwork.org

- Plants become infected as seedlings, plants are often stunted.
- Infected heads emerge before uninfected ones.
- Individual seeds are replaced by pointed galls (25 mm or longer), with a thin grey membrane.
- This membrane soon breaks after ear emergence and black dusty material is released and will blow away, long black pointed structures remain.
