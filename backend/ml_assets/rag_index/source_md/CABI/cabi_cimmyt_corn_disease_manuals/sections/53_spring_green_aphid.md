# Spring green aphid

*Schizaphis graminum*

Worldwide

![img-188.jpeg](sections/images/53_p167_img-188.jpeg)

Photo: Alton N. Sparks, Jr., University of Georgia, Bugwood.org

- Extensive red brown spotting of leaves develops and remains even once the aphids have been controlled.
- Whole plants at seedling stage can be destroyed and feeding on older plants causes lodging.
