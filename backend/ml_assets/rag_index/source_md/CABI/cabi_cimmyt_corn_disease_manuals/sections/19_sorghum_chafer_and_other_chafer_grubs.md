# Sorghum chafer and other Chafer grubs

*Pachnoda interrupta*

Worldwide

![img-152.jpeg](sections/images/19_p133_img-152.jpeg)

Photos: P. Taylor, CABI; Udo Schmidt, Wikipedia

![img-153.jpeg](sections/images/19_p133_img-153.jpeg)

- Large fleshy body with brown head.
- Grubs always live underground and are characteristically C-shaped when disturbed.
- Three pairs of well-developed legs.
- Adults are typical beetle shaped, usually brown but may be patterned.
