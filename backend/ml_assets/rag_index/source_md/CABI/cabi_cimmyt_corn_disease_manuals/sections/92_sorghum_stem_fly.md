# Sorghum stem fly
*Atherigona soccata*

Africa, Asia, SE Asia

![img-227.jpeg](sections/images/92_p206_img-227.jpeg)

Photo: ICRISAT

- Maggot eats out the centre of the plant giving dead heart, older leaves are unaffected.
- Youngest leaf is easily removed and gives off bad smell.
- Plants may recover and send out side shoots these also may be attacked but are more able to stand the attack, papery leaves are produce in this case.
