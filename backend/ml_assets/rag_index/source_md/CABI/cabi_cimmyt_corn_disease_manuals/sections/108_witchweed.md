# Witchweed
*Striga hermonthica*

Africa

![img-243.jpeg](sections/images/108_p222_img-243.jpeg)

Photo: IITA image library

- Parasite or hemi-parasitic purple flowered plant causes scorching early drying and often the plant will fall.
- Plants will show poor vigour and fail to yield.
