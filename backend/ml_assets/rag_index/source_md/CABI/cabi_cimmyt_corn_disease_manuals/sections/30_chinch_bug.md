# Chinch bug
*Blissus leucopterus*

N and S America

![img-165.jpeg](sections/images/30_p144_img-165.jpeg)

Photo: Kansas State University

- Eggs are yellow but turn red found at the base of plants behind lower leaf sheaths and on soil.
- Nymphs, dark red and have a white band across their bodies. Adults 5 x 1.5 mm They are mostly black with reddish legs and milky white wings.
- Adults produce a familiar foul odour when crushed.
