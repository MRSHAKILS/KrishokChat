# Earhead bug

*Calocoris angustatus* and other

India and Africa

![img-163.jpeg](sections/images/28_p142_img-163.jpeg)

Photo: Entomart, commons.wikipedia.org

- Eggs (blue) cigar shaped 1.5 mm laid under the scales covering the grain.
- Nymphs and adults attack developing grain (adults 5 mm long yellowish green).
- Nymphs and adults suck juice from grains when they are at the milky ripe stage.
- Grains fail to fill and heads become chaffy.
