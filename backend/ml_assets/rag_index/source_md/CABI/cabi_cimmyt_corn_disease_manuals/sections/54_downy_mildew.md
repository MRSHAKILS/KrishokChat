# Downy mildew

*Peronosclerospora* spp. (on Maize)

Worldwide

![img-189.jpeg](sections/images/54_p168_img-189.jpeg)

Photo: Thomas Isakeit, Texas A&M University

- Leaves have yellow to white stripes from the base.
- Leaves are often narrower than usual, erect, and shredded. A white, downy growth may develop on both surfaces of leaves.
- Plants may be stunted, and have an irregular seed set.
