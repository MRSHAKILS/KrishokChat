# Lesion nematode

Pratylenchus spp. (on banana)

Worldwide

![img-239.jpeg](sections/images/104_p218_img-239.jpeg)

Photo: University of Nebraska

- Causes black dead areas on root surface and holes inside roots.
- Above ground only general symptoms develop, reduced growth, with stunting.
