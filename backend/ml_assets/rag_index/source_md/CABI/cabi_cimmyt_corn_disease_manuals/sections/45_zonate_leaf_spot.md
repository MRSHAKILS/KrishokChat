# Zonate leaf spot
*Gloeocercospora sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-180.jpeg](sections/images/45_p159_img-180.jpeg)

Photo: L Claflin, Kansas State University

- Red spots on lower leaves are the first symptoms.
- These spots develop into circles (mid leaf) or semi-circular if on the edge of leaves but they then elongate and join together.
- It can become severe in times of high temperature and humidity.
