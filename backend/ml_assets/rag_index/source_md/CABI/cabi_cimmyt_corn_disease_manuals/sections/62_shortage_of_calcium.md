# Shortage of Calcium

## Calcium deficiency

Worldwide

![img-197.jpeg](sections/images/62_p176_img-197.jpeg)

Photo: Dr Prakash Kumar

- The plants are stunted and the leaves fail to expand properly and they are often partially trapped by the lower leaf.
- The plant may develop a ladder like appearance with each leaf holding the one above it.
- In this photo ignore the leaf borer damage and slight necrosis.
