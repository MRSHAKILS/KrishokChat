# Shortage of Phosphate
## Phosphate deficiency

Worldwide

![img-194.jpeg](sections/images/59_p173_img-194.jpeg)

Photo: Dr Prakash Kumar

- Severe stunting of the plant associated with dark green colour and purpling of the lower leaves.
- Other stress factors can produce similar symptoms but this is very characteristic of phosphate (P) shortage.
