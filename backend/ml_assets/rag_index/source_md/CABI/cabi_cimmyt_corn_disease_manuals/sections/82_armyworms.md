# Armyworms

*Spodoptera* spp. and others

Worldwide

![img-217.jpeg](sections/images/82_p196_img-217.jpeg)

Photo: J. Mulema, CABI

- Leaves only feeders they never bore into stems.
- Larvae feed on leaf funnels, and emerging leaves will have extensive areas of damage.
- Some species feed on leaves leaving mid-rib.
- It is necessary to see the larvae to identify the pest.
