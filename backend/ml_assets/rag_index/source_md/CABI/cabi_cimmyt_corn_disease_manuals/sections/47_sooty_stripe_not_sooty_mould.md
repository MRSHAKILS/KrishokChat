# Sooty stripe (NOT Sooty mould)

*Ramulispora sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-182.jpeg](sections/images/47_p161_img-182.jpeg)

Photo: David Nowell, Ecoport.org

- Small yellow spots are the first symptom.
- These become oval and develop a brown centre with a wide yellow edge.
- Spots can get really large and join up.
- Older spots have a lot of black spores appear as a “soot” covering the surface of the brown area.
