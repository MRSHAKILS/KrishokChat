# Rough leaf spot

*Ascochyta sorghi*

Asia, Africa, N America

![img-187.jpeg](sections/images/52_p166_img-187.jpeg)

Photo: Clemson University Extension slide series, Bugwood.org

- Spots first appear 2-3 mm x 2 mm as reddish areas.
- Fruiting bodies are usually produced rapidly sometimes before the discolouration (on both sides of the leaf).
- Spots enlarge until they become 8-14 x 4-8 mm. Usually the centres become tan with a purple or red border but this depends on the variety.
