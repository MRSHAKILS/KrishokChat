# Downy mildew

*Peronosclerospora sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-201.jpeg](sections/images/66_p180_img-201.jpeg)

Photo: International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center

- Early symptoms, pale leaves, white downy growth on underside.
- On older leaves wide yellow or white striping, and often develop brown spots.
- White areas dry, brown, die and appear shredded.
- Plants are stunted and fail to produce grain.
