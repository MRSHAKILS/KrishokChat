# Bacterial top and stalk rot

*Dickeya zeae*

Worldwide

![img-226.jpeg](sections/images/91_p205_img-226.jpeg)

Photo: University of Nebraska, Lincoln Extension

- The growing point of the plant disintegrates.
- Leaves are easily pulled out of the whorl and the lower end has a bad smell.
- Often basal rots accompany this symptom and the plant is likely to fall over.
