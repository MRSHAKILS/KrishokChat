# Anthracnose

*Colletotrichum sublineolum*

Worldwide

![img-235.jpeg](sections/images/100_p214_img-235.jpeg)

Photo: Uni Georgia Dept Agricultural and Environmental sciences

- Spots on the stem are circular with a red to black border and grey centre. They penetrate into the stem and red marbling can be seen inside the stem.
- Fruiting bodies often seen within the spots on the stems.
- The disease can kill crops that have poor resistance.
