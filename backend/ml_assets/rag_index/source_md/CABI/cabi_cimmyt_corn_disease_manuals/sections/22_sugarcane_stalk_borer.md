# Sugarcane stalk borer

*Diatraea saccharalis*

N and S America

![img-157.jpeg](sections/images/22_p136_img-157.jpeg)

Photos: Aldo Norberto Bonaveri; William White, Bugwood.org

- Egg mass 25-50, whitish-yellow.
- Larvae 25 mm, red-brown head, yellowish-white body, brown spots.
- At low temp lacks spots and becomes yellower.
- Adults, straw-colour, V-shape black dots on forewing, 25 mm wingspan.
