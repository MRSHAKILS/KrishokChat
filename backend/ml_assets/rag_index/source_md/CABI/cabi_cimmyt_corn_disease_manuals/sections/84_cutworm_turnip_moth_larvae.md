# Cutworm, Turnip moth larvae

*Agrotis segetum*

Worldwide

![img-219.jpeg](sections/images/84_p198_img-219.jpeg)

Photo: W.M. Hantsbarger, Bugwood.org

- Severe damage can be done to seedlings.
- Larvae chew stems at or just above the soil line and plant the falls over.
- It looks as though the plant has been cut off.
- Larvae hide in soil during the day.
