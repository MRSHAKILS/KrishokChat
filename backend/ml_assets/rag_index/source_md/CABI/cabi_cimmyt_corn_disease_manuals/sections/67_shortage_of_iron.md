# Shortage of Iron
Iron deficiency

Worldwide

![img-202.jpeg](sections/images/67_p181_img-202.jpeg)

Photo: Dr Prakash Kumar and Dr Manoj Kumar

- Long and continuous stripes on the leaves.
- Usually seen in the younger leaves first.
- Leaves can become almost totally yellow in extreme cases.
- Stripes caused by viruses are usually not continuous and downy mildew stripes are much wider.
