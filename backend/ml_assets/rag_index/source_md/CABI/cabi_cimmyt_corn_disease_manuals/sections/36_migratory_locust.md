# Migratory locust
*Locusta migratoria*

Africa, Asia, SE Asia

![img-171.jpeg](sections/images/36_p150_img-171.jpeg)

Photo: P Rowley, Flickr

- Body length varying from 35 to 50 mm for males and from 45-55 mm for females.
- Mandibles (mouth parts) are blue. Wing cases are shining and long: 45-55 mm (males), 50-60 mm (females).
- Wings are colourless, except smoky tint and black veins.
