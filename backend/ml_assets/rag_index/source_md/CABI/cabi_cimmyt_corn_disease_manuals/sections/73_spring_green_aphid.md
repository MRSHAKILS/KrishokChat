# Spring green aphid
*Schizaphis graminum*

Worldwide

![img-208.jpeg](sections/images/73_p187_img-208.jpeg)

Photo: D Mock, Texas A&M University

- Once the plant begins to produce seed the aphids may move up onto the developing stalks of the grain.
- Seed head becomes splayed.
- Feeding on the developing grains prevents them from filling and they will die.
- Many aphids can produce similar symptoms and the individuals need to be examined.
