# *Puccinia purpurea*

Worldwide

Photo: S Nelson, Flickr

- The leaves bearing the reddish powdery pustules will dry up.
- Plants will show poor head development and this will lead to shrivelled grain.
- Under ideal conditions the whole plant will be affected.
