# Shortage of Nitrogen

Nitrogen deficiency

Worldwide

![img-195.jpeg](sections/images/60_p174_img-195.jpeg)

Photo: Dr Prakash Kumar

- General yellowing of the plant usually associated with stunting.
- Symptoms first seen in the lower leaves, tip of the plant remains green.
- Yellow V-shape spreads down the centre of the leaf from the tip of the leaf to the stem.
