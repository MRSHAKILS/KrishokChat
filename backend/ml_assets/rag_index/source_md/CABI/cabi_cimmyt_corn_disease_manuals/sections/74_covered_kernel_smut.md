# Covered kernel smut

*Sporisorium sorghi*

Worldwide

![img-209.jpeg](sections/images/74_p188_img-209.jpeg)

Photo: L Claflin, Kansas State University

- Plants are infected at the seedling stage but show no symptoms until near maturity.
- Dark brown powdery masses are covered by a tough greyish white or brown membrane, this usually breaks at harvest time.
- Usually all the seeds in the head are affected.
