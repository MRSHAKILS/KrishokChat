# Cutworm, Turnip moth larvae
*Agrotis* sp.

Worldwide

![img-151.jpeg](sections/images/18_p132_img-151.jpeg)

Photos: Frank Peairs, Colorado State University, Bugwood.org; Donald Hobern, Flickr

- The larvae are generally grey, sometimes tinged with purple.
- Live in the soil rarely climb plants.
- On each segment, 2 small black spots at the front and two at the back, each bearing a small bristle.
- Generally active at night will curl up if discovered during the day.
