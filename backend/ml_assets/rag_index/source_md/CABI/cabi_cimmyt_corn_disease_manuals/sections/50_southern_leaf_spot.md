# Southern leaf spot
*Cochilobolus heterostrophus*

Worldwide

![img-185.jpeg](sections/images/50_p164_img-185.jpeg)

Photo: CIMMYT, Flickr

- Tan spots 2-6 mm wide and 3-22 mm long.
- Spots have yellow green or chlorotic border.
- When the seed head emerges a black felty mould may cover the affected seeds and ear rot may occur.
