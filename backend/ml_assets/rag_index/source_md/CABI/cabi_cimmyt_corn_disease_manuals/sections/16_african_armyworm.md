# African armyworm
*Spodoptera exempta*

Worldwide except S America

![img-149.jpeg](sections/images/16_p130_img-149.jpeg)

Photos: M Powell, ARC-PPRI; P. Taylor, CABI

- Two types of larvae: those which like to live together and those which live alone.
- Grouped larvae have a velvety-black upper surface with pale lines on their sides, a green or yellow under surface, and no hairs on the body.
- There are three parallel lines on the back of the first body segment. The head is always shiny-black.
- Larvae living alone are fat and extremely sluggish and variable in colour.
