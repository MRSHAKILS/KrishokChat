## 4.8 Recording Weeds of Fruit Crops

Fruit plants /trees are long-lived. Various types of weeds may infest the fruit garden. Grassy weeds are not competing for nutrient because most tree plants are deep-rooted. But some parasitic plants are directly sucking nutrient from the trunk or branch of the infested plants like Loranthus sp. The fruit plant strawberry is sensitive to weed infestation. Polythene mulch/ straw mulch reduce weed infestation and maximize its yield (Karim et al., 2009). The major and minor weeds of fruits crops are shown in Table 34.
