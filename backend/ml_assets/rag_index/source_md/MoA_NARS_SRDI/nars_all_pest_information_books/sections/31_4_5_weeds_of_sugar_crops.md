### 4.5 Recording Weeds of Sugar Crops

Sugarcane and sugar beets are the sugar crops cultivated in Bangladesh. Sugarcane remains in the field about 12 months. So, many weeds may infest the crop and about 20% yield reduction may occur (SRTI, 1978). Therefore, it needs one early weeding to minimize yield reduction for both sugarcane and sugar beet.
