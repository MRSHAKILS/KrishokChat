## 2.7 Recording Insect and Mite Pests of Vegetable Crops

Insect and mite pests of 13 different vegetable crops such as brinjal, cabbage, cauliflower, knokhol, radish, turnip, lady's finger, tomato, cucurbits, bean, kangkong, lettuce and amaranthus are included under the pests of vegetable crops. Altogether 192 insect and mite pests of vegetable crops are listed in Table 7 of which 62 pests were in the range of medium to high with common to wide distribution in the country. Plant parts affected include seedling, root, leaf, stem, twig, bud, inflorescence, flower and fruit.
