## 2.13 Recording Insect and Mite Pests of Medicinal Plants

Insect and mite pests of 24 different medicinal plants are included under this category. Altogether 133 insect and mite pests of medicinal plants are listed in Table 13 of which 47 pests were in the range of medium to high with common to wide distribution in the country. Plant parts affected include seedling, root, leaf, stem, twig, bud, flower, inflorescence, fruits and seed.
