## 2.8 Recording Insect and Mite Pests of Fruit Crops

Insect and mite pests of 28 different fruit crops are included under the pests of fruit crops. Altogether 313 insect and mite pests of fruit crops are listed in Table 8 of which 108 pests were in the range of medium to high with common to wide distribution in the country. Plant parts affected include seedling, leaf, stem, twig, bud, flower, inflorescence, fruits and seed.
