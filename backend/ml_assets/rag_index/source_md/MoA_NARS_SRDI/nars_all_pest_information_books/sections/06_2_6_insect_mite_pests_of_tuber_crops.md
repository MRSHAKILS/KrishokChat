## 2.6 Recording Insect and Mite Pests of Tuber Crops

Insect and mite pests of 4 different tuber crops such as potato, sweet potato, aroids and yam are included under the pests of tuber crops. Altogether 39 insect and mite pests of tuber crops are listed in Table 6 of which 9 pests were high with common to wide distribution in the country. Plant parts affected include seedling, root, leaf, stem, twig, bud, inflorescence, flower and fruit.
