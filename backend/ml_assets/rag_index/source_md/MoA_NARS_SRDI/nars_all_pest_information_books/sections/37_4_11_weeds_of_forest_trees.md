#### 4.11 Recording Weeds of Forest Trees

Forest crops consist of perennial plants. Weeds like grassy plants/sedges grown in the ground are not harmful to forest trees as they are not competed for nutrient with them. Some herbs/creeping weeds hindering photosynthesis and make infested tree weak. Table 37 is showing some weeds of forest plants.
