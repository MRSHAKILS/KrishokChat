#### 4.2 Recording Weeds of Pulse Crops

There are different pulses are cultivated in Bangladesh. Of them except grass pea, the other pulses like lentil, mung bean, chickpea and black gram are sensitive to weed infestation and crop loss also remarkable. Bhuiyan et al. (2018) also reported that yield loss of chickpea caused by early weed infestation may be about 40-50%. The weed found in the field of pulse crops are illustrated in Table 28.
