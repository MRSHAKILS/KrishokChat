#### 4.10 Recording Weeds of Flower and Ornamental Plants

In Bangladesh, many kinds of flowers and ornamental plants are cultivated in open field, especially, in winter season. Some flowers are grown under shade to protect these from rain in the summer-rainy season. There are various types of weed infestation in the field. To suppress weeds farmers generally use hand weeding. Following Table 36 is showing a list of weeds of flowers and ornamentals in Bangladesh.
