## 2.11 Recording Insect and Mite Pests of Forest trees

Insect and mite pests of 52 different forest trees are included under the pests of forest trees. Altogether 546 insect and mite pests of forest trees are listed in Table 11 of which 255 pests were in the range of medium to high with common to wide distribution in the country. Plant parts affected include seedling, root, leaf, stem, twig, bud, flower, inflorescence, fruits and seed.
