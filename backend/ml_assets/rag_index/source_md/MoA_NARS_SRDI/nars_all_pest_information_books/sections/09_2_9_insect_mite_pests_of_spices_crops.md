## 2.9 Recording Insect and Mite Pests of Spices Crops

Insect and mite pests of 15 different spices crops are included under this category. Altogether 75 insect and mite pests of spices crops are listed in Table 9 of which 46 pests were in the range of medium to high with common to wide distribution in the country. Plant parts affected include seedling, leaf, stem, twig, bud, bulb, flower, inflorescence, fruits and seed.
