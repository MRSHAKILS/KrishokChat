### 4.3 Recording Weeds of Oilseed Crops

Among the oilseeds crops like mustard, ground nut, sesame, sunflower, safflower and soybean, mustard is the most important among those cultivated in Bangladesh. Although oilseeds crops are sensitive to weed infestation but the farmers pay less attention on it. As a result, sometimes drastic yield reduction occurs.
