## GROUP SESSION PLANNING

Day 01

Time – 11.00

Duration: 30 minutes

Target group : Women famers of Household based pond aquaculture and homestead gardening

Title of session : Understanding of Household based fish culture

Aim : To give the training participants a basic understanding on 'Household Based Pond Aquaculture'

Objective : At the end of this session, the participants -
- Will be able to describe household-based pond fish cultivation
- Will know how to use household-based resources for pond fish cultivation
- Will know about the role of women in pond fish culture and how they can be empowered
