## Fish culture training course

### Course evaluation format (mood meter)

Training

location...date:...
...

Objective is to take opinion from participants about everyday's sessions

Guidelines:

1. Reviewing drawbacks and advantages of the training sessions critically
2. Get an overall feeling about the sessions by getting the participants to mark the symbols on the mood meter
3. Before taking opinion using the mood meter, care should be taken to elucidate what needs to be considered in evaluating the training sessions e.g. Approach, delivery, and use of training material. This does not include residential arrangements, food and personal feelings
4. Participants should listen carefully to trainer's explanation before evaluating
