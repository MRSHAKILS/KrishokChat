### Cautions during fry transport

- Care should be taken so that the fry bags do not get pressure from any thing.
- Transport patil (pot) should be covered with wet cloths or chat (jute mat).
- Bag / patil should always be kept in a shadowy place during transport.
- Care should be taken so that the fry bags do not get pressed from any hard objects.
- It is better to add 10 g ice / L water/ hr transport distance to keep the water cool.
