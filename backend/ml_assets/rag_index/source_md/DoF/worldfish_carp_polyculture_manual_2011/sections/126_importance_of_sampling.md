### Importance of sampling

To know the growth rate of fish

To determine the total biomass of stocked fish in the pond

To verify the appropriateness of the quantity of applied feed

To redetermine the quantity of applied feed

To know if fish reach harvestable size

To examine fish health, diagnose disease and take necessary actions
