## Measurement method of pond and water area

### What is meant by pond measurement?

Pond measurement means calculating area of the pond. This area can be calculated 2 ways - with dyke or without dyke. Usually, pond area is measure in decimal.

**Water area measurement:** Calculating water area of the pond means water area measurement. Before stocking fish in any pond, its water area should be calculated. Generally average water area of a pond is calculated.
