### Floating of fish after rain

Sometimes, fish start floating on the pond surface just after rain. It happens due to the drop of pH level and toxicity from harmful hydrogen sulfide gas.

### Preventive measures

Water pH should be measured after rain.

Every time, after heavy rain per decimal 75 – 80 g quick lime / dolomite should be used.
