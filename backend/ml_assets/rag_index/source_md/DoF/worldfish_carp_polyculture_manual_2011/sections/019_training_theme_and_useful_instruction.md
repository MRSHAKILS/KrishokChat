### **Training Theme and useful instruction**

The topics of the training have been determined in the light of practical need of the farmers at the field level and on the basis of evaluation and recommendation from the experts. In the different session planning, subject-oriented handouts are given in this manual. By reading the handouts, the trainers will enrich themselves, which in turn assist the trainees in participating in thematic discussion. Different subject matters are arranged chronologically. Running the session becomes easy if the trainers prepare themselves by assessing the session planning thoroughly well-ahead the training commences.
