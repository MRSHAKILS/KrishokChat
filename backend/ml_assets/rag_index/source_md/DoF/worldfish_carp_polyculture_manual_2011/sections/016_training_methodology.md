### **Training Methodology**

Most of the learning matters should be accomplished using participatory approach. Here, ample opportunities would be given to farmers to exchange their own experience, so farmers can easily take part and achieve effective learning. The following experience-based participatory approaches will be used in each of the sessions -

1. Brain-storming,
2. Group Discussion,
3. Open Discussion,
4. Event Analysis,
5. Real Objects and Practical Demonstration,
6. Speech-Discussion, and
7. Question and Answer
