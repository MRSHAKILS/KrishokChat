### Other aspects

**Embankment:** In general, the embankment of a medium to large pond is 5 – 6 feet. This should be higher (elevated) than level of flood or flush flood (due to excessive rain).

**Berm:** A level space (raised barrier) between the edge of the top layer of pond and embankment should be kept unfilled. The area is known as berm. In a medium sized pond, generally 3-4 feet space towards inner side from the embankment should remain unfilled

**Slope:** The angular line starting from the pond embankment towards the pond bottom is known as pond slope. Suppose the slope of a pond is 1 : 2, this indicates if someone digs a pond 1 foot vertically, to ensure 1 : 2 slope, the pond should be dug horizontally 2 feet towards the inner side. That will be the design of the bottom of the main gully of the pond. This way, earth excavation should be started for a 8 feet deep pond along the depth, after fixing a spot on the embankment, another spot should be fixed at the 16 feet distance both length and breadth-wise from the first spot. As a result, the slope created from the embankment channel along the main gully will be 1 : 2 (1 : 2 means vertically 1 and horizontally 2).
