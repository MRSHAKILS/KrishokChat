# **Risk in dry season**

During dry season, water level may drop and pond water depth may go down as well. In this situation, the water temperature may rise quickly and results in the drop in dissolved oxygen content. As a result, all fish in the pond might die. Therefore, before this situation, all marketable fish should be harvested.
