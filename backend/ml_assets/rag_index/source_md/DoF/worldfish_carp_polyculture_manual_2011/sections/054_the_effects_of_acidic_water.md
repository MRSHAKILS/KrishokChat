### The effects of acidic water

- If pH of water drops below 5 sodium and chloride of fish blood come out through osmosis. As a result, weak fish die. This becomes even more serious if calcium content is low in water.
- Huge quantity of slimes is created on fish skin and gills become infected.
- The fish immunity decreases, fish lose appetite and wounds are not healed easily.
- Spawns and fry of large fishes get infected fast.
- The natural pond productivity drops.
- Reproductive capacity of fish decreases.
