### **Feed for shing and application**

Generally granular pellet feeds are used in shing culture. Shing are fed at 4- 5 pm in the afternoon and 10 – 11 pm at late evening. After stocking, 2 – 3 g shing fry are fed with 20% feed of total biomass. From the second month up to next 2.5 – 3 month, 10% feed of total biomass is applied. From then only 5% feed of total biomass is applied up to harvesting. Under this culture management, shing grow up to 40 – 50 g within 8 – 9 months.
