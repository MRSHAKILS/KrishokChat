### Planned excavation of fish ponds to ensure appropriate environment for fish culture

Most of the ponds in our country are built to raise the homestead or for some other household purposes therefore, even someone wishes, can not make sure all elements of a culture pond. Therefore, during the excavation of new ponds suggestion should be taken from the fisheries experts so all necessary elements of a culture pond can be ensured. The aspects need to be considered in the excavation of a new pond in an area are described here:

- What is the soil type of the area where the pond will be excavated? Sandy soil, loamy soil, sandy loam or red soil. If these are not considered during pond excavation, various problems like low water retention capacity, fast spoilage of pond dyke etc will be created in the long run.
- How is the flood situation of the area?
- How is the ownership scenario of the pond at present and in the future?
- At what month, pond excavation has been planned?
- The location where pond will be excavated – is it lowland, highland or with slope?
