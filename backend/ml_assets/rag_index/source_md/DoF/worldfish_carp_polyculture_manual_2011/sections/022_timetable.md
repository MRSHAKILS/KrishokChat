# Improved Carp/Carp-Shing Poly Culture in Pond and Dyke Cropping

Training Course

Goal and Objectives of the Training

Training goal

To improve the knowledge and skill of participants on carp/carp-shing poly culture in pond and dyke cropping so they can get more yields from carp/carp-shing poly culture in pond and dyke cropping

Overall objectives

- At the end of the course the trainees will be able to explain the following aspects –
- Basic fish culture concept
- Biology of the cultivable species in carp poly culture
- Carp poly culture management
- Different fish culture methods
- Carp poly culture planning and data keeping
- Economic analyses of carp poly culture
- Vegetable farming in pond dyke and economic analyses
