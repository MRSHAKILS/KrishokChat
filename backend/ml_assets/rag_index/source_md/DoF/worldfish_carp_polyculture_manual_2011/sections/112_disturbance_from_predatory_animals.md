### **Disturbance from predatory animals**

Snakes, frogs, crabs, otter etc. directly predate on fish and prawn and significantly reduce the production.

### **Preventive measures**

- Whenever seen, snakes, frogs, crabs and otter should be killed at once.
- To control otter, lime filled egg shell can be placed on the pond dyke.
- Crabs can be caught using a bamboo trap – chai.
- Grass of the areas should be mowed where frogs lay their eggs like where embankment meets pond water.
- Ponds should be kept free of weeds, bush and hedge.
