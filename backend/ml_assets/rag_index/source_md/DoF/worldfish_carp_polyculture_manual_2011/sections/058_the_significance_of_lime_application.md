# The significance of lime application

A. Assist in maintaining equilibrium state of hydrogen and hydroxyl ions, that is, helps in keeping the pH neutral. As a result the appropriate environment is created for the growth of planktons.
B. Provide calcium and other important ions for the growth of plankton. Calcium and silica help in the formation of physical structure (skeleton).
C. Increase the supply of carbon dioxide for photosynthesis
D. Free the phosphorus bound in sediment for plankton growth

Types of limes

|  Name of lime | Chemical structure | Availability  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Lime stone | CaCO_{3} | Generally not available in the market  |
|  Burnt lime or Quick lime | CaO | Available as lime stone in the market  |
|  Slaked lime | Ca(OH)_{2} | Available in the market in powder form  |
|  Dolomite | CaMg(CO_{3})_{2} | More availability in shrimp farming area  |
|  Gypsum | CaSO_{4}.2H_{2}O | Available in the markets of some areas  |

The uses of lime

|  Name of lime | Effectiveness  |
| --- | --- |
|  Burnt lime or Quick lime | This lime is excessive alkaline. The application of this lime instantly increases the pH rapidly. Its application in dry pond is safe.  |
|  Lime stone / Dolomite | If water pH is less than 7 and hardness and alkalinity are less than 20 mg / L, dolomite application is very effective in organic matter rich pond sediments  |
|  Gypsum | Gypsum is very effective in controlling turbidity created by washed sediments. If water hardness is low and alkalinity is high (if linked to carbonate / bicarbonate / hydroxyl ion and other ions like aluminum), gypsum woks well.  |
