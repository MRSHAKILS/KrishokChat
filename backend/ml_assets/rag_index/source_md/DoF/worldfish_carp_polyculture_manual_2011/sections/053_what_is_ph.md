### What is pH ?

pH is the estimates of negative hydrogen ion concentration, ranges between 1 and 14. The acidity and alkalinity of water is measured by pH. Here pH 7 is neutral, pH more than 7 indicates alkalinity (7 - 14) and pH less than 7 indicates acidity (7 - 1).

![img-83.jpeg](images/img-83.jpeg)

The optimum pH range of water is 7 - 9 for fish farming. If pH is less than 4 or more than 11, fish may die.

**The source of acidity:** The main source of acidity is black soil or acid sulfate rich soil and polluted waste materials.
