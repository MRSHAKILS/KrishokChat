# **Preparation of dyke of pond / gher**

Vegetables / crops should be selected depending on the type of the dyke. Culturing all kind of vegetables / crops in a particular dyke is not possible. Dyke should be prepared such a way that it suits that particular vegetable / crop. Based on the land type, pond dykes can be of many types, such as:

- High pond dyke
- Medium high pond dyke
- Low pond dyke
- Medium low pond dyke
- Wide pond dyke
- Narrow pond dyke etc.
