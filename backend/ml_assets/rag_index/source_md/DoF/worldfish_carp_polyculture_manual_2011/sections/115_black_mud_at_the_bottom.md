### Black mud at the bottom

Due to build up of extra feed and organic matter at the pond bottom, soil of the bottom turns to dark and smelly. Particularly, the problem can be severe in a pond, where fish culture is going on for long time. As a result, fish may face mass mortality due to the toxic gas at the bottom.

### Preventive measures

- Before fish stocking, excessive bottom mud should be removed.
- If mass mortality of fish fry is observed during culture period, water should be exchanged right away and fish density should be reduced.
- Application of feed and fertilizer should be stopped.
