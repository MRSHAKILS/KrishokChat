### **Excess feed application**

If more feed are used than necessary, a large portion of the applied feeds are accumulated at the pond bottom and destroy the aquatic environment. As a result, fish easily get infected by different diseases and die.

### **Preventive measures**

- The exact feed quantity should be determined before application.
- The excess mud built up at the place of food application should be removed from time to time.
