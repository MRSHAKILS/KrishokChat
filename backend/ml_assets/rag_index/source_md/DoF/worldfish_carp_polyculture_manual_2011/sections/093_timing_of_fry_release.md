### Timing of fry release

Fish fry can be released in the pond at any time of the day in cool weather. However, releasing fry in the pond during early morning or late afternoon is better. Fry should not be released in ponds /ghers in the bright sun of noon time, in a cloudy or humid day (particularly in a day of low pressure).
