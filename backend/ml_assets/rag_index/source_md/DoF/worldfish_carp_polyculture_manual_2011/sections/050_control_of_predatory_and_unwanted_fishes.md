### Control of predatory and unwanted fishes

Fish culture undoubtedly is a profitable venture. Even though, many people do not get much profit from fish culture and many even loss money. Among many reasons of the loss in fish culture, a notable one is most of the stocked fish disappear during culture and not found during harvesting. the main reason behind this disappearance is predation by the predatory fish. Therefore pond should be free of predatory fishes before the fish stocking.
