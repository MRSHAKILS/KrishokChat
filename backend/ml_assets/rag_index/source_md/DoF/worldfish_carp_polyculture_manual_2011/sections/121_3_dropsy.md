### 3. Dropsy

Fish get the disease because of bacterial infection. Mainly carps and shing type of fish are infected. Generally the infection is severe in the summer.

#### Symptoms

- Fluids are accumulated in the abdomen and under the scales.
- Fish abdomen looks like a balloon berceuse of swelling.
- Lesions on the skin are observed, intestine turns to bulgy and scales protrude.

#### Control

- Fish should be fed with 1-2 g oxytetracycline (Renavet) / keg feed over 4 – 7 days
- Oxytetracycline injection – @ 3 mg / 400 g fish

#### Prevention

- Application of balanced feed, less organic fertilizer and application of 1 kg lime / dec
