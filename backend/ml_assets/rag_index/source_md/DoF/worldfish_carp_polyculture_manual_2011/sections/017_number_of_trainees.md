### **Number of Trainees**

Maximum 25 trainees can take part in the training. Because, the way the participatory techniques have been designed, if the number of trainees are more than 25, the problems may arise in effective communication between trainers and trainees. Mainly the manual has been designed for target farmers under CSISA-BD project.
