### **Duration of the Training**

The manual has been developed for a 2-day fundamental training course. However depending on the actual need of the trainees, timetable can be modified within training duration of the two days. In general the daily programme should be continued from 10 am to 14 pm. The venue of the training should be in an area where farmer live or near to the tilapia pond or gher.
