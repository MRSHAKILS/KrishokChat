### Aspects need to be considered in selecting feeds

The fish farmers in our country mainly use oil cake and rice bran as supplementary feed for fish. Other than the two, farmers use such type of food materials that are not economically profitable and even a few materials are detrimental for pond ecosystem. The main objective of the application of supplementary feed is production of more fish. Therefore, in selecting feeds for profitable culture of fish and prawn, the following important aspects should be considered –

- Easy availability of ingredients
- Financial ability of fish farmers
- Cost of ingredients
- Preferred feed for fish
- Nutrient demand of fish
- High food conversion ratio
