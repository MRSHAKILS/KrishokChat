### Vegetable model in dyke and slope of pond

|  Suitable place | Rabi crop | Kharif crop  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  Pond dyke and slope | Tomato, lablab bean, knol khol and bottle gourd | Bitter gourd, cucumber, bottle gourd, wax gourd, lady's finger / okra, sweet gourd  |

*Other than the listed crops, farmer may select any other crop depending upon the dyke and season
