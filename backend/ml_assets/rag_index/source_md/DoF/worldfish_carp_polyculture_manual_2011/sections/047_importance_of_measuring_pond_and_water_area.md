### Importance of measuring pond and water area

In fish culture, farmers first should know the water area of the pond / water body. If water area is not calculated properly, problems may be created in every aspect of fish culture. Such as, a. Determination of lime requirement, b. Determination of fertilizer requirement, c. Estimation of stocking density of fish and d. Rotenon application etc.

Measurement method of pond and water area: 1. Hand (cubit) / Bamboo method, 2. Tape method and 3. Foot (leg span) method

Usually, the unit of pond measurement is decimal. 435.6 square feet = 1 decimal, 100 decimal = 1 acre;
