### **Carpio**

The head of carpio is much smaller compared to their body, broad abdomen and dorsal side convex like a bow. Body colour is light brown. Body is covered by scale. It is assumed that their original habitat is temperate regions of Asia particularly China. The fish was imported to our country from Hungary in 1960.

They live in the bottom layer of haor, baor and ponds. They intake foods from the bottom of the ponds. For food, they comb through bottom mud and the soil of pond embankments and surrounding and made the soil loose. As fry they eat zooplanktons, the adult carpio prefer to feed on plankton, small / large insects, small snail, earthworm, decomposed organic matters etc. This is also a fast growing fish. Under poly culture system, the carpio can grow up to 1- 3 kg in a year.

They become sexually mature in 5 – 6 months. They are capable to spawn in captive water and spawn twice a year (January – March and July – August). The fish seed can be produced in the hatcheries as well by artificial breeding.
