# Method of application:

a) Half of OF and all of P, S, Zn and B should be applied as basal during final land preparation. Remaining OF should be applied in pits before planting of seedlings.
b) N and K should be applied in three equal splits at 20, 40 and 60 DAT as ring method under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil as soon as possible for better utilization.

# Method of application:

a) Half of OF and all of P, S, Zn and B should be applied as basal during final land preparation. Remaining OF should be applied in pits before planting of seedlings.
b) N and K should be applied in three equal splits at 20, 40 and 60 DAT as ring method under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil as soon as possible for better utilization.
c) Instead of urea, Urea Supper Granule (USG) might be applied \(7 - 8\mathrm{cm}\) deep into the soil and \(8 - 10\mathrm{cm}\) apart from the plant and as ring method.
