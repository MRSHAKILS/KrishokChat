# OAT (*Avena sativa*)

Yield Goal: \(50 \pm 5\) t/ha/year

|  Soil Analysis Interpretation | Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha/year)  |   |   |   |   |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   |  N | P | K | S | Zn  |
|  Optimum | 0–15 | 0–3 | 0–4 | 0–1.5 | -  |
|  Medium | 16–30 | 4–6 | 5–8 | 1.6–3.0 | 0-1.0  |
|  Low | 31–45 | 7–9 | 9–12 | 3.1–4.5 | 1.1-2.0  |
|  Very low | 46–60 | 10–12 | 13–16 | 4.6–6.0 | 2.1-3.0  |

# Method of application:

a) All of P, K, S and Zn and \(50\%\) of N should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
b) Remaining \(50\%\) of N should be top dressed after 30 days of sowing/planting.
