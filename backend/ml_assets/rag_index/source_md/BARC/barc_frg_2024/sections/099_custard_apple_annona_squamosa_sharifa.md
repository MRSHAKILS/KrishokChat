Method of application:

Fertilizers should be applied in three split doses

- 1st split : April – May
- 2nd split : August
- 3rd split : October – November

*Fertilizer recommendation has been estimated on the basis of production of made tea in kg/ha

Method of application:

a) All of P and Zn; and half of N and K should be applied during April–May with onset of shower. Remaining half of N and K should be applied within July to first week of August.
b) Urea is generally used in tea cultivation as a source of nitrogen; and TSP/Rock phosphate/DAP and MOP might be used as the sources of P and K, respectively.
c) Zinc should also be applied as foliar spray four times a year- twice during mid-April to Mid-June and twice during mid-September to mid-October.
d) If soil pH is below 5.0, dolomite [(CaMg(CO₃)₂] application at 1-2 t/ha is suggested, usually after a good shower.
