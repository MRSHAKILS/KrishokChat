## 6.5 Floating agriculture

Floating agriculture is an age old agricultural practice in the southern districts (Barishal, Goplaganj and Pirojpur) of Bangladesh, where the agricultural lands remain water-logged for a longer period. The United Nation (UN) organization recognizes it as a national heritage of Bangladesh. The scientists of Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI) has taken an initiative since long time to improve the production practices under the floating agriculture system, mostly on vegetable and spice crops for improving yield. Their effort has brought a good success.in it.
