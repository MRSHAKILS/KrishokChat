# Method of application:

a) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, full amount of recommended organic and chemical fertilizers should be applied and mixed thoroughly with the soil into the pits followed by irrigation.
b) After plantation, fertilizers and manure should be applied in four equal splits at 2-3 months of intervals during March-April, May-June, September and October. Fertilizer should be applied around the tree up to canopy spread leaving \(50 - 100\mathrm{cm}\) from the tree base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation. Under hill slope conditions, drilling method should be followed.
c) For the highly acidic soils, dolomite should be applied at the rate of \(250\mathrm{g}\) /tree every 3-4 years interval.
