## SPONGE GOURD (*Luffa cylindrica*)

(Var: BARI Dhundul-1, BARI Dhundul-2, BARI Hybrid Dhundul-1 and others)

Yield Goal: 35.0 ±3.5 t/ha

### Method of application:

1. All of OF, P, K, S, Mg, Zn and B should be applied in pits 5–7 days before planting and mixed thoroughly with the soil.
2. Nitrogen should be applied around the plant as side dressing at 15, 35, 55 and 75 DAT under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.

## CUCUMBER (*Cucumis sativus*)

(Var: All cultivars)

Yield Goal: 25 ± 2.5 t/ha

|  Soil Analysis Interpretation | Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha) |   |   |   |   |   | OF (t/ha)  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   |  N | P | K | S | Zn | B  |   |
|  Optimum | 0–25 | 0–9 | 0–15 | 0–5 | - | - | 5  |
|  Medium | 26–50 | 10–18 | 16–30 | 6–10 | 0–2.2 | 0–1.0  |   |
|  Low | 51–75 | 19–27 | 31–45 | 11–15 | 2.3–4.4 | 1.1–2.0  |   |
|  Very low | 76–100 | 28–36 | 46–60 | 16–20 | 4.5–4.8 | 2.1–3.0  |   |

### Method of application:

1. All of OF, P, S, Zn and B; and one-third of N and K should be applied in pit 5–7 days before planting and mixed thoroughly with the soil.
2. Remaining N and K should be applied during 20, 35 and 50 DAT around the plant as side dressing under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.
