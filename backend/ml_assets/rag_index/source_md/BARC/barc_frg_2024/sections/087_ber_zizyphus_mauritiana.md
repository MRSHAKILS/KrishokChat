# Method of application:

a) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, full amount of manure and P should be applied separately and mixed thoroughly with the soil into the pits, followed by irrigation.
b) After plantation fertilizers and manure should be applied in two equal splits. First installment should be applied during April-May, the second installment should be applied during September-October. Fertilizer should be applied around the tree up to canopy spread leaving \(1.00 - 1.50\mathrm{m}\) from the tree base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation. In hill slope conditions, drilling method should be followed.
