# Fertilizer recommendation for seedbed

Usually, fertilizer application is not required for rice seedbed. For low fertile soils, manure at the rate of 2 kg/m² can be applied. In case of sulphur deficiency, gypsum should be applied at the rate of 10 g/m² as topdressing. Yellow coloured seedlings due to cold injury in Boro season cannot be recovered by N or S topdressing, rather the seedbed should be covered with transparent polythene.

# Method of application (Irrigated culture):

a) Two-thirds of the N and all of P, K, S, Mg, Zn, B and organic manure (if used) should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
b) Remaining one-third N should be applied after 17-21 days of sowing (DAS) after first irrigation.
