# Method of application:

1) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, full amount of recommended organic and chemical fertilizers should be applied and mixed thoroughly with the soil into the pits followed by irrigation.
2) After plantation, fertilizers and manure should be applied in two equal splits. One half should be applied just after harvesting of fruits (June-July) and another half should be applied in September-October. Fertilizer should be applied around the tree up to canopy spread leaving \(1.0 - 1.5\mathrm{m}\) from the tree base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation.
