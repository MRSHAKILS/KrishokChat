# Method of application:

a) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, full amount recommended organic and chemical fertilizers should be applied and mixed thoroughly with the soil in the pit followed by irrigation.
b) After plantation fertilizers and manure should be applied in three equal splits during February, May and October. Fertilizer should be applied around the tree up to canopy spread leaving \(50 - 100\mathrm{cm}\) from the tree base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation. In hill slope conditions, drilling method should be followed.
