## INDIAN SPINACH (*Basella alba*)

(Var. BARI Puisak-1, BARI Puisak-2)

Yield Goal: 30.0± 3.0 t/ha

|  Soil Analysis Interpretation | Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha) |   |   |   |   | OF (t/ha)  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   |  N | P | K | S | Zn  |   |
|  Optimum | 0-30 | 0-10 | 0-13 | 0-5 | - | 5  |
|  Medium | 31-60 | 11-20 | 14-26 | 6-10 | 0-1.0  |   |
|  Low | 61-90 | 21-30 | 27-39 | 11-15 | 1.1-2.0  |   |
|  Very low | 91-120 | 31-40 | 40-52 | 16-20 | 2.1-3.0  |   |

Method of application:

1. All of OF, P, K, S and one-third of N and K should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
2. Remaining N and K should be applied in two equal splits at 14 and 28 days after planting under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.

(Winter)
