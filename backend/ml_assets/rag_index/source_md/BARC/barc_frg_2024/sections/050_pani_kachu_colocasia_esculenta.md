# Method of application:

a) Half of OF, K and all of P, S, Zn and B should be applied as basal. Remaining half of OF should be applied in pit before planting of seedlings.
b) Full N and half of K should be applied in three equal splits at 10-15, 30 and 50 days after transplanting as ring method under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.
