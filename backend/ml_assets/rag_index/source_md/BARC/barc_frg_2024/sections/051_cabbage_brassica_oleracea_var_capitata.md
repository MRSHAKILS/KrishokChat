## BATISAK (*Brassica parachinensis*)

(Var. BARI Batisak-1)

### Method of application:

1. Half of N, K and OF and all of P and S should be applied as basal during land preparation. Remaining half of OF should be applied in pit before planting of seedlings.
2. Remaining half for both N and K should be applied at 20-25 DAS under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.

(Var. BARI Gima Kalmi-1)

|  Very low | 91-120 | 31-40 | 40-52 | 16-20 | 5.8-7.6 | 2.9-4.2 | 1.7-2.4  |   |

### Method of application:

1. All of OF, P, K and S and one-third of N should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
2. Remaining N should be applied as top dress after each harvest under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.
