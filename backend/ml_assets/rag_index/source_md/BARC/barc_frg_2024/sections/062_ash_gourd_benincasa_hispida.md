# Method of application:

b) All of OF, P, S, Zn and B and half of N and K should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
b) Remaining N and K should be applied in 2 equal splits at 25 and 50 DAP for bulb production; and in 3 equal splits at 25, 50 and 75 days after planting for seed production.
