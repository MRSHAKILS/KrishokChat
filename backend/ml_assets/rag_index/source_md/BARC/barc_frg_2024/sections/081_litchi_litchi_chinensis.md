# Method of application:

a) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, whole amount of manure, P and K should be applied and mixed thoroughly with the soil into the pits, followed by irrigation.
b) After plantation fertilizers and manure should be applied in three equal splits. First installment should be applied after fruit harvest. The second and third installments should be applied during October and March, respectively. Fertilizer should be applied around the tree up to canopy spread leaving \(50 - 100\mathrm{cm}\) from the tree base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation. Under hill slope conditions, drilling method should be followed.
