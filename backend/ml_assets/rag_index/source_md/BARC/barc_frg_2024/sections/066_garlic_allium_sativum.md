# BLACK CUMIN (*Nigella sativa*)

(Var. BARI Kalozira-1)

Yield Goal: 1.0 ± 0.10 t/ha

|  Soil Analysis Interpretation | Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha) |   |   |   |   |   | OF (t/ha)  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   |  N | P | K | S | Zn | B  |   |
|  Optimum | 0–20 | 0–8 | 0–15 | 0–5 | – | – | 2  |
|  Medium | 21–40 | 9–16 | 16–30 | 6–10 | 0–1.0 | 0.0–0.7  |   |
|  Low | 41–60 | 17–24 | 31–45 | 11–15 | 1.1–2.0 | 0.8–1.4  |   |
|  Very low | 61–80 | 25–32 | 46–60 | 16–20 | 2.1–3.0 | 1.5–2.1  |   |

Method of application:

a) All of OF, P, K, S, Zn and B should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
b) Nitrogen should be applied in two equal splits as topdress at 30 and 55 DAS under moist soil condition.
