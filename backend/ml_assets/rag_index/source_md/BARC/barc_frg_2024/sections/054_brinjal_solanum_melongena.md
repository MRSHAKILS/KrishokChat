# OKRA (Abelmoschus esculentus)

(Var. BARI Dherosh-2)

Yield Goal: 25.0 ± 2.5 t/ha

|  Soil Analysis Interpretation | Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha) |   |   |   |   |   | FYM* (t/ha)  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   |  N | P | K | S | Zn | B  |   |
|  Optimum | 0–30 | 0–10 | 0–23 | 0–5 | – | – | 5  |
|  Medium | 31–60 | 11–20 | 24–46 | 6–10 | 0.0–1.0 | 0.0–0.7  |   |
|  Low | 61–90 | 21–30 | 47–69 | 11–15 | 1.1–2.0 | 0.8–1.4  |   |
|  Very low | 91–120 | 31–40 | 70–92 | 16–20 | 2.1–3.0 | 1.5–2.1  |   |

Method of application:

a) All of OF and all of P, S, Zn and B; and one-fourth of N should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
b) Remaining three-fourth N should be applied at 20, 40 and 60 DAS under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.

# RADISH (Raphnus sativus)

(Var. BARI Mula-1, BARI Mula-2, BARI Mula-3, BARI Mula-4)

Yield Goal: 60.0 ± 6.0 t/ha

|  Soil Analysis Interpretation | Nutrient Recommendation (kg/ha) |   |   |   |   | OF (t/ha)  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|   |  N | P | K | S | Zn  |   |
|  Optimum | 0-37 | 0–15 | 0-30 | 0–6 | – | 5  |
|  Medium | 38-74 | 16–30 | 31-60 | 7–12 | 0–1.5  |   |
|  Low | 75-111 | 31–45 | 61-90 | 13–18 | 1.6–3.0  |   |
|  Very low | 112-148 | 46–60 | 91-120 | 19–24 | 3.1–4.5  |   |

Method of application:

a) All of OF and P, S, Zn; and one-third of N and K should be applied as basal during final land preparation.
b) Remaining N and K should be side dressed in two equal splits at 21 and 35 DAS under moist soil condition and mixed thoroughly with the soil.
