# Method of application:

a) The full dose of cowdung, one-third of N, full of P, half of K, and full of S should be applied during final land preparation.
b) Remaining two-third of N and half of K should be applied in two equal splits, the first split at 20 days after sowing (DAS) and the second split at 35 DAS.
