# Method of application:

a) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, entire amount of manure and P should be applied separately and mixed thoroughly with the soil in the pit, followed by irrigation.
b) After plantation fertilizers and manure should be applied in two equal splits. First installment should be applied during May-June and the second installment should be applied during September-October. Fertilizer should be applied around the tree up to canopy spread leaving \(50 - 100\mathrm{cm}\) from the tree base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation. Under hill slope conditions, drilling method should be followed.
