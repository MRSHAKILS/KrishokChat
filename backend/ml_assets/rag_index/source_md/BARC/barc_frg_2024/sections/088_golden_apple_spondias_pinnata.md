# Method of application:

a) Before 15-20 days of sapling plantation, total amount of recommended organic and chemical fertilizers should be applied and mixed thoroughly with the soil into the pits followed by irrigation.
b) After plantation fertilizers and manure should be applied in two equal splits. First installment should be applied during April-May, the second installment should be applied during September-October. Fertilizer should be applied around the plant base and mixed thoroughly with the soil followed by irrigation.
