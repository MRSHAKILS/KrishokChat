## 2. Brinjal

Brinjal belongs to the family Solanaceae and as known under the botanical name Solanum melongena. Brinjal is one of the important winter and summer vegetables which is grown extensively in Bangladesh. In Brinjal, a large variation of plant types, fruit colour, shape and size are available. At present, it occupies 46.57 thousand hectares of land and produces about 3.40 lac metric tons (BBS, 2011).

### 2.1 Varieties

Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI) has developed 12 brinjal varieties (8 OP and 4 Hybrid) for winter and summer season which produce seeds under local climatic condition. Some popular local varieties-Singhnath, Islampuri, Khatkhotia, Dohazari etc. are cultivated widely in Bangladesh. The main features of the important BARI released varieties are given below:
