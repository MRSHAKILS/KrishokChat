### 1.2 Production Technology

#### Land and soil

Medium high to high land with loamy to sandy loam soil is the best suitable for potato cultivation.

#### Seed rate

1.5- 2.0 t/ha (whole tuber); 1.2 -1.5 t/ha (cut tuber)

#### Seed treatment

Seed should be treated with 3% boric acid solution before sprouting for controlling the common scab disease. In this case, boric acid should be melted with light heat water. After cooling of the boric acid solution, it should be sprayed on potato tuber.

#### Time of sowing

15 to 30 November is the optimum time but sowing may be done in October to December.

#### Land preparation

Soil should be tilth properly by ploughing and laddering.
