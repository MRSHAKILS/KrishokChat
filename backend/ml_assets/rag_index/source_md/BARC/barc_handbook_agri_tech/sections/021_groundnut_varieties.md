## 2. Groundnut

Groundnut is an important oil seed crop. It is also the best edible oil seed crop. In Bangladesh, it occupies third place in respect of area and production. At present, in rabi (winter) and kharif (summer) season, a total of 31.58 thousand hectares land is under groundnut cultivation and produce 54 thousand metric tons (BBS, 2011). Production can be increased by using high yielding variety (HYV) and improved production technology. The crop seed contains 48-50% oil and 22-29% protein. Although the quality of groundnut oil is superior to mustard oil but it is not popular in this country as cooking oil because of consumption habit. It is mainly consumed as roasted nut and as a confectionary item.
