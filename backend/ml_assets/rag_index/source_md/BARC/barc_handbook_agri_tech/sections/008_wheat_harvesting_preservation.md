# **Control measures:**

- Use resistant variety.
- Spraying of Tilt 250 EC 2 times (at heading and 15 days later) @ 0.5 ml per litre of water is effective to control the incidence of bipolaris leaf blight disease and also leaf rust in case of late sown wheat. It also controls black point disease and improves the quality of seed.
- Seed treatment with Provax-200 @ 3g per kg of seed could reduce the incidence of seed borne diseases like Bipolaris leaf blight, black point, etc.

# **Major pests:** Bird and Rat

# **Control measures:**

- Bird should be kept away for 10-12 days after sowing.
- Rat should be controlled using traps or poison (Zinc phosphide, Racumin, etc.) or by any other traditional means.

# **2.4 Harvesting and Seed preservation**

In case of seed plot, the off type plants should be rouged out at least 3 times starting from flowering up to maturity stage to ensure varietal purity. Crop should be

harvested at full maturity in a sunny day. Usually, wheat is harvested by hand. Wheat should be threshed by paddle thresher or power thresher. Wheat seeds should be dried up under sun to bring the moisture below 12%. It
