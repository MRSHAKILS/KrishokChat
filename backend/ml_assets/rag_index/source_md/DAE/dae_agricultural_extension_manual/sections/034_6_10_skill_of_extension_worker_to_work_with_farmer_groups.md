## 6.10 Skill of Extension Worker to Work with Farmer Groups

The duration and effectiveness of farmer groups solely depend on the skill of extension worker or SAAO with farmer groups. Therefore, extension workers or SAAO should be aware of their skills in this regard.

The following required skills should be achieved:

i. Skill and experience for maintaining good relationship with group members
ii. Skill on identifying and managing the demands of group members
iii. Experience in managing any influential member for maintaining cohesiveness among the members of the group
iv. Patience and sharp in dealing with matters for all the activities with group
v. SAAO should be kind hearted to quickly solve problems faced by group member
vi. SAAO should be skilled in management system as a helping hand
vii. Ability to acquire modern and suitable knowledge on technology
viii. Expert in creating environment for interaction with members of group
ix. Ability to connect with the agricultural product supply and agriculture market.
